## 1. The facts that matter

1. **Prices at CHR 4** **[code ✓]**. Buy = (value × A + 50)/100, with
   A = max(100, owner greed + race factor + 125 − 200), where 125 is `adj_chr_gold` for CHR 4
   (`store.c:183-222`, `tables.c:1226`). No haggling. At CHR 4, A is 135–170% depending on the owner:
   - **Armoury, owner Kon-Dar (a Half-Orc): 135%**, the cheapest owner in town for us.
   - **Alchemist, Temple: 154–161%.** Owners there differ by only ~5%.
   - **Black market: ×3 on top.**

   The formula reproduces mission 8's prices (Phase 24, Enchant 52 at 75% off and 151 at 25% off,
   Dagger 16, Soft Leather 24). One exception: CCW 152, where Temple owners give 154–159 at CHR 4.

2. **Where things are sold** (`init2.c:1066ff` store tables) **[code ✓]**:
   - **Only the Temple (4)** sells CLW, CSW, CCW, Boldness and Heroism.
   - **Only the Alchemist (5)** sells Phase Door, Word of Recall, Enchant scrolls, Identify, Magic
     Mapping and Door/Stair Location.
   - **The Magic shop (6)** has the Staff of Teleportation.
   - **Not for sale anywhere:** Scroll of Teleportation (it's in the ironman table only,
     `init2.c:1510`), Berserk Strength, and Teleport Level. Deep Descent doesn't exist in 1.5.3.
   - The Black market draws random items of object level 30–54 (plus a rare Healing potion).
   - **The Pilot sent 15 `shop 5` goals for cures and Boldness** (S4). Those belong to shop 4.

3. **Restocking** **[code ✓]**. Every store is maintained every 10 × STORE_TURNS = **2500 turns**
   (`dungeon.c:1909`). At the configured 75 frames per second that's every **~33 s of real
   time**, occupied or not. Each pass drops 1–9 stock slots and adds 1–9. Nothing is always
   stocked. S1's simulation, *without purchases*, gives these odds of finding an item in stock:
   Phase Door and WoR ~98%, CCW ~93%, CLW ~88%, Main Gauche and Rapier ~77%, Flask ~98%. What our
   logs saw: Temple CLW 14 of 17 visits, Main Gauche 6 of 13, Rapier 6 of 13, Phase Door 41 of 42,
   WoR 42 of 42. The gap is probably other buyers and enchanted variants sitting in their own slots
   under another name. **Stock changes every half minute, so if something is missing, wait a
   minute and look again.**

4. **Discounts.** A new item is 25/50/75/90% off with odds 1/50, 1/300, 1/600, 1/1000. An owner
   change marks ~10% of the stock "on sale" at 50%. The Alchemist showed `{25% off}` WoR lines on
   most visits, and Dive03 paid 59/117/176 instead of ~235 for them. **Always buy the discounted
   line first.**

5. **Selling pays little, and unknown items pay the base value of their kind** **[code ✓]**.
   The sell price uses A flipped (min 100%): about 38–65% of value depending on the owner. And
   `object_value()` uses the *base kind* value unless the item is fully known
   (`object2.c:1362-1387`). That's how Wormtongue's Soft Studded Leather of Resistance sold for 17
   (buy-back 19,834), and why three Tulwars of different enchantment all paid 86. What shops paid
   us: potions and scrolls 7–9, wands 24, staffs ~33, rods ~45, rings 20. Weapons and armour paid
   25–30% of retail (Broad Sword 102, starting Chain Mail 358).

6. **Light burns in town. Food doesn't** **[code ✓]**. Fuel burns once every
   `level_speed/1000 ÷ timefactor` game turns (`dungeon.c:1103-1108, 1485-1492`). **Resting with
   no monster in view sets timefactor to 1000%, even in town** (`xtra2.c:5164ff`), while turns in
   town stay at normal speed (`time_factor()` returns 100 at depth 0, `xtra2.c:5254`). So
   **resting in town burns light ~10× faster per real second.** That's how Dive04's three torches
   died. Store-bought torches and lanterns come **half full** (2500 / 7500, `store.c:992-995`).

   | light | awake in town | at 500 ft | at 1500 ft | resting in town |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | store torch (2500) | ~21 min | ~27 min | ~31 min | **~2–3 min** |
   | store lantern (7500) | ~62 min | ~82 min | ~95 min | ~6–8 min |
   | full lantern (15000, refilled with flasks) | ~2 h | ~2.7 h | ~3.2 h | ~12–15 min |

   A ration lasts ~27–32 real minutes in the dungeon at normal speed; food doesn't digest in town.

## 3. Shopping lists for the throwaway warrior

**Start:** sell the starting Broad Sword (~102) and Chain Mail (~358) at once (user advice,
HANDBOOK). With starting gold that gives ~560.

**0–500 ft**
- **~150 gold:** 3 Phase Door (72) · 3 CLW (70) · 2 Flasks (10), if carrying a lantern.
- **~500 gold** (after selling the kit):
  - Main Gauche (38; else a Dagger, 16, and look again in a minute).
  - Soft Leather Armour (25), Small Leather Shield (41), Cap (16), Soft Boots (10), Gloves (5),
    Cloak (5).
  - Brass Lantern (55) + 3 Flasks (15).
  - 5 Phase Door (120) · 5 CLW (118) · 2 Rations (10).
  - Total ≈ 460.
- **~2000:** the above, plus 1 WoR (on sale if there is one) and 2 Enchant To-Dam **only at a
  discount**.

**500–1000 ft** (the Borg wants 3 CCW + 2 teleport-class escapes from 500 ft; for us, escapes =
Phase Door + WoR)
- **~500:** 1 WoR (~235, or a discounted line) · 5 Phase Door (120) · 5 CLW (118) · 2 Boldness (31).
- **~2000:**
  - 2 WoR (~470) · 8 Phase Door (190) · **3 CCW (~470)** · 3 CSW (~190) · 5 CLW (~118).
  - 2 Boldness (31) · 1 Heroism (55) · 1 Door/Stair Location scroll.
  - Total ≈ 1,550.

**1000–1500 ft** (the Borg: 4+ CSW/CCW and 4+ escapes from dl20; Free Action is required; see the
danger and Borg memos)
- **~2000:** 2 WoR · 10 Phase Door · 4 CCW (~630) · 4 CSW (~250) · 2 Boldness · 1 Heroism.
  Total ≈ 1,900.
- **Save for a Staff of Teleportation** (3,100+). It's the only buyable long-range escape, and it
  works blind and confused. It's the backup escape the forum memo (§1.4) calls for.

**Never buy:** enchanted starter armour (Dive03 paid ~2,250 for +3/+4 leather that later sold for
1–6), full-price Enchant scrolls, or Scrolls of Teleportation (none exist to buy).

## 4. Selling tips

- **Identify before selling anything that might be magical** (Identify is at the Alchemist in 41 of
  42 visits, 20–80 gold). An unknown item sells at its plain base value, whatever it really is.
- **Worth selling:** weapons and armour (25–65% of value depending on the owner; the Armoury's
  Kon-Dar pays 65% to a Half-Orc), unique drops *after* inspecting them, and identified
  staffs/wands/rods/rings (they sell at their real value once known; unknown ones pay 20–45).
- **Not worth the walk:** potions and scrolls (7–9 each). Drop or use them instead.
- **Never sell:** probable specials (the shop goal already refuses), and Staffs of Door/Stair
  Location or Teleportation (Dive03 sold one for 33 that the shop re-lists at 219–900).

## 5. Recommendations

**Pilot**
1. **Route by store:** cures and Boldness/Heroism → shop 4; Phase Door, WoR and Enchant → shop 5.
2. **If an item is out of stock,** wait ~40 s in town (not resting) and look again: stock turns
   over every ~33 s.
3. **Prefer discounted lines** (`{N% off}`) when several lines of an item are listed.
4. **In town, unwield the light or don't rest** (resting burns fuel ~10×). Buy a lantern early:
   store torches come half full.
5. **Budget:** the lists above as buy orders per band, with the existing `@MAX` price caps.

**Navigator**
- **Carry the Borg's minimums from 500 ft:** 3 CCW + 5 Phase + 1–2 WoR. From 1000 ft: 4 CCW/CSW +
  8–10 Phase + 2 WoR. Go back to town below those counts, but not within a minute of arriving
  somewhere (Borg memo §1.4).
- **Treat a Staff of Teleportation as the first big purchase** once the basics are covered.
