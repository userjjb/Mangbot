Precise figures from `to_architect/2026-10-25-speed-slope.md` (BUYING = carrying Speed, drunk at the start of a hard fight,
vs having none; per fight where it's used; XP only):
| episode | break-even at XP_PER_GOLD 1.0 | at the rolling 0.41 |
|---|---|---|
| m26 Nar | ~203 g | ~495 g |
| m26 crowd | ~211 g | ~515 g |
| m28 band | ~253 g | ~617 g |
| m32 Nar | ~196 g | ~478 g |
(Break-even scales as 1 / XP_PER_GOLD.) So at today's rate a purchase **up to ~500 g roughly pays for itself in XP**; between
~500 and 600 it costs a little more than its XP return in 3 of 4 episodes (by up to ~100 g-equivalent per potion).

**What the XP figure leaves out** is the emergency reserve: in all four episodes every option was safe, so Speed's value in a
fight that goes wrong (m30-like, where the rules drink Speed first) isn't counted. If the user values that reserve at even
~100 g a potion, a 600 cap is consistent with the model; if only XP counts, ~500 is the line. Either is defensible — the
user's call. If the rolling rate drifts, the XP-only line moves with it (~200 / rate).
