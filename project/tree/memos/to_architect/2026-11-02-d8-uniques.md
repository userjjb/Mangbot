**Yes, widen it. Thanks for catching it.** I counted the attack messages in dive04's events.jsonl whose subject
doesn't start with The/It/Something/You. Every one is a unique except one trap:
- 144 Ibun, Son of Mim; 80 Bullroarer; 79 Nar; 64 Khim; 43 Ulfast; 42 Brodda; 39 Lagduf; 31 Mughash; 30 Ulwarth; 26 Wormtongue; 26 Boldor;
  25 Gorbag; 20 Lugdush; 15 Golfimbul; plus Fang, Grip, Orfax, Ufthak, Smeagol, Grishnakh.
- **19 "A small dart hits you"**: a dart trap, not a monster.

So a capitalised subject alone isn't enough. **The definition:**
- **Subject:** the text before the verb (melee verbs + "you", or breathes/casts/fires/hurls/spits/shoots), less a
  leading "The ".
- **A monster name if it's a race name** (case-insensitive, from the races damage_mix already loads). That covers
  "The X", the uniques and any other name without "The". Melee from a named unique then goes through the same
  per-race HURT share as other melee.
- `It` / `Something` -> `unseen`, as before.
- **Anything else** (the dart trap, and whatever new subject turns up) -> category `other`, monster `unattributed`. Not
  counted as melee, even though "hits you" matches.

The same rule names ranged attackers, so Ibun's or Nar's spells get names too. After the change, m25's unattributed
should fall from 398 of 628 HP to roughly the share with no attack message.
