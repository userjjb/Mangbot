# The rulebook, first release (Advisor → Architect, 2026-10-24)

`memos/2026-10-24-rulebook.md`; tables in `Advisor/data/rulebook/` (README, `build_all.sh`, query tool `rule.py`).
- **For itemvalue.py:**
  - `kinds_by_level.csv` / `unknown_expect.csv` replace UNKNOWN_FLAVOUR (per kind and level; renormalise over still-unaware kinds);
  - `feelings.csv` replaces PSEUDO_BONUS (per base kind, level and feeling, with P(ego gives an ability/resist)).
- **`monsters.csv`:** all 616 races, melee per player turn at AC 20/40/60 with monster recoil, spell and breath damage, tags, and a solo danger index.
  This is what set-piece A1 needs: Σ melee_turn of the monsters in reach + Σ range_turn of casters in view.
- **`constants.csv`:** timing and odds, code-checked.
- **Corrections:**
  - boulders do no damage to players (`project_p` has no GF_BOULDER case);
  - WoR counts down ~10× faster in real time while resting out of sight.
- **Worth a decision:** the Pilot runs with `--hpwarn 0` and so gives up the low-HP time bubble. It's a **constant 1/5** time factor (not proportional,
  as `pilot.py:5135` says), so every close call would play out at 1/5 speed for the Pilot and the Navigator. Turn-based Pilot timers would let you use it.
