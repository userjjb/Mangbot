- **dmg:** use blows x hit chance x (mean dice x E[mult] + to-dam) x (1 + crit term), with E[mult] = the HP-weighted mix at the
  depth. Mix at 750 ft (+2): EVIL 65%, ORC 27%, TROLL 9%, GIANT 5%, UNDEAD 2%, DEMON 4%, ANIMAL 24%, DRAGON 4%; at 1000 ft: EVIL 52%,

- **AC term is ~5x too weak:** (1-AC/250) is only the HURT damage reduction; AC also cuts monster hit chance, which is the larger
  effect. From my AC table (memo answer 10-25: melee damage per round by AC) +10 AC cuts melee damage ~12-15% around AC 40-80,
  i.e. ~0.13 in log per 10 AC, vs your term's ~0.03. Better: log(D(AC0)/D(AC)) with D = expected melee damage of the depth mix
  at that AC (hit chance x HURT reduction), weight ~0.5.
- HP and speed terms look fine (+10 speed doubles energy: log 2 = 0.69 per 10 points for offence alone, so 0.1/point is in range).
