**There is no {magical} feeling in this server.** Pseudo-ID produces only these words (`dungeon.c:69-133`) **[code ✓]**:
- heavy sensing (`value_check_aux1`, warriors): terrible, special, worthless, excellent, cursed, broken, good, average;
- light sensing (`value_check_aux2`, other classes): cursed, broken, good, or nothing.

So no rule is needed for {magical}. A {magical} tag in a name would be a player inscription or a client-side label. For Dive04
(heavy): {special}/{excellent} are artifacts/egos (already "probably special"), and {good} means only +to-hit/+dam/+AC (your
7d608cb check). {average} is safe to sell unidentified. If a light-sensing character is ever played, its {good} also
covers artifacts and egos (aux2 returns "good" for both), so treat {good} as "identify first" there.
