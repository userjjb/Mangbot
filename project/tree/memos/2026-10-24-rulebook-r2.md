## 1. Ability inference: `abilities.csv` (44 rows), plus `ego_flags.csv` (all 118 egos with flags)

Each row: ability, exact message, whether it proves has or lacks, trigger, what it's ambiguous with, how to tell apart, code line. Key points **[code ✓]**:
- **Your "You are unaffected!" → Free Action rule needs the line before it.** The same text follows **five** checks:
  - HOLD ("stares deep into your eyes!" / "mumbles.", `melee2.c:1445`) and SLOW ("drains power from your muscles!", `:1422`) → Free Action;
  - the PARALYZE blow (`melee1.c:970`) → Free Action;
  - **BLIND ("casts a spell, burning your eyes!", `:1378`) → resist blindness**;
  - **TELE_LEVEL ("gestures at your feet." / "mumbles strangely.", `:1637`) → resist nexus**.
  
  Dive04's 8 cases (m18–m22) all followed SLOW or HOLD, so the boots inference was right. Pair the message with the preceding spell line.
- **"You resist the effects!" proves the player LACKS the resist.** The resist test comes first and prints "unaffected". "Resist" is the saving
  throw.
- **Fear and confusion can't be told from a save** with spells ("You refuse to be frightened.", "You disbelieve the feeble spell."). Use
  no-save sources: CONFUSE blows, gas traps, potions. No "You are confused!" afterwards means rConf.
- **Clean Free Action test:** the paralysis-gas trap ("A strange white mist surrounds you!") with no "You are paralyzed!" after it. There's no save.
  BRAIN_SMASH, inertia and gravity slow regardless, so they say nothing.
- **Hold Life:** "You keep hold of your life force!" / "You feel your life slipping away!" (has) vs "…draining away!" (lacks).
  **Sustains:** "You feel very weak for a moment, but the feeling passes." (has). **Feather fall:** "You float gently…" (has).
- **Silent abilities:** elemental resists (only the HP drop: 1/3, 1/9 with a potion), See Invisible, ESP, regeneration, slow digestion, random teleport,
  aggravation.
- **The `flags` query shows only KNOWN flags** (`object_flags_known`, `xtra1.c:938`). Unidentified Boots of Free Action show nothing there,
  and immunities merge with resists. But **speed, stealth, searching, infravision, blows, shots and stats include unknown gear** (calc_bonuses
  uses full flags). Comparing the indicators before and after wearing an unknown item reveals those.
- **Potion of Self Knowledge** (level 40) lists every real ability. It isn't sold.
