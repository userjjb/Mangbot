# Memo: what the Angband Borg teaches the Pilot and Navigator (Advisor → Architect, 2026-09-28)

**Backlog topic 2, with the core of topic 7** (judging a group of monsters and slow drains, which
the Architect asked for after Dive03's death). **Audit trail:** `Advisor/studies/2026-09-28-borg/`.
**Source:** `Advisor/data/borg/angband-src/src/borg/` (vanilla Angband 4.2.x, HEAD 2026-09-22).

## How this was made

- **Sources:** the current Borg (118 files, ~64k lines of C), its config `borg.txt`, its doc
  `docs/hacking/borg.rst`, and its git history (299 commits, 2011–2026). For our side: `pilot.py`,
  `world.py`, `mover.py`, `glyphs.py`, `HANDBOOK.md`, `design_pilot.md`, the Navigator agent file.
- **One pass, seven Clerks:** danger model (D1), caution/escape/heal/rest (D2), depth readiness and
  gear scoring (D3), the main loop and leaving levels (D4), inventory (D5), history and known failures
  (D6), and our Pilot described in the same categories (P1). The Advisor checked the claims this memo
  relies on in the code (marked **[code ✓]**), and corrected three Clerk errors.
- **Caveat:** the Borg plays *vanilla 4.2*, not MAngband 1.5. Its control logic transfers; its
  game numbers don't always (one clash is noted in §6). It also plays turn-based, with the game
  paused while it thinks, and it reads game memory directly (monster HP, which walls are permanent,
  full item flags) instead of the screen (`borg-think.c:147-171`). Our Pilot can do neither.

## 1. The findings that matter most

1. **The Borg judges the whole group, pessimistically, against current HP.** `borg_danger(grid)`
   adds up every tracked monster's danger. For melee that is the **maximum** roll of each blow
   (`d_dice × d_side`, `borg-danger.c:110-111`), with no discount for armour or for how rarely a
   monster casts: `borg_danger()` forces `full_damage = true` before its loop (`:2849`) **[code ✓]**.
   Fast monsters count extra hits. Each stat-drain blow adds **+150** (+100 more if STR < 10) unless
   the character has the sustain (`:324-339`) **[code ✓]**; a paralysing blow adds +200 without
   Free Action. Unknown monsters count as 1000. The total is capped at 2000.
   The number is compared with **current HP** ("avoidance"), in tiers:

   | danger vs current HP | Borg action | where |
   |---|---|---|
   | > 0.3× | won't walk into that square ("icky") | `borg-flow.c:440-452` **[code ✓]** |
   | > 0.33× | swaps in defensive gear; a fight target is skipped above 0.33–0.5× | `borg-caution.c:1025-1031`, `borg-flow-kill.c:1796-1798` **[code ✓]** |
   | > 0.6× (clvl ≤ 35) | escape: stairs if on them, else Phase Door | `borg-escape.c:999-1012` **[code ✓]** |
   | > 1.0× | takes the stairs it stands on; phase then teleport | `borg-caution.c:1118-1121`, `borg-escape.c:893ff` |
   | > 1.5× (no unique near) | emergency teleport | `borg-escape.c:721-738` **[code ✓]** |
   | > 2.0× | leave the level | `borg-caution.c:1087-1099` **[code ✓]** |

   **Dive03's death through the Borg's eyes** (4 Uruks, HIT 3d5 ×2 each; a Giant red scorpion,
   BITE 2d4 + STING LOSE_STR 1d7; 253 HP): danger ≈ 4 × 30 + (8 + 7 + 150) ≈ **285**. That exceeds
   0.6 × 253 = 152, so the Borg phases or takes the stairs at once. It also exceeds 0.3 × 253, so
   the Borg would never have walked next to that group. Our Pilot's per-monster rules saw an Uruk at
   18 per turn (< 253/3) and a scorpion at 9, and nothing fired (P1 confirms that only breath is
   summed across a group, `pilot.py:1043-1046`).

2. **The Borg never dives under-levelled.** In its default configuration, `borg_prepared(depth)`
   refuses any depth below the character's level (**clvl ≥ dungeon level**, until clvl 50,
   `borg-prepared.c:559-561`) **[code ✓]**. A warrior needs:
   - clvl ≥ dl − 4 at dl10–19 (`:222-226`);
   - clvl ≥ dl + 5 at dl21–38 (`:305-310`), i.e. clvl 30 at 1250 ft;
   - clvl 40 from dl34 (`:374-376`) **[code ✓]**.

   Gear gates (**[code ✓]** `:212-389`):

   | from | the Borg requires |
   |---|---|
   | dl10 (500 ft) | light radius 2; 2 teleport-class escapes; 3 CCW (clvl < 30); **See Invisible or ESP** |
   | dl20 (1000 ft) | Free Action (worn) |
   | dl21 (1050 ft) | resist fire + 2 of acid/cold/lightning |
   | dl26 (1300 ft) | all four basic resists; 6 escapes; 10 CSW+CCW (clvl < 30) |
   | dl40 (2000 ft) | resist poison, resist confusion |

   When it isn't prepared, it won't take `>` and it climbs. When it's prepared 5 levels deeper
   than where it stands, it dives fast. It recalls down only to a depth it's prepared for (60% of
   its maximum depth). Our stair-scummer is under-levelled by design (clvl ≈ 18–28 at dl21–30 in
   the danger memo). The Borg wins games under the opposite policy; this is the sharpest doctrinal
   contrast of the study (§4).

3. **Being stuck counts as danger in its own right.** A counter (`times_twitch`) rises every time
   planning finds nothing to do (`borg-think-dungeon.c:2124`). It phases while the count is under 3
   and teleports while it's under 5. Past 20 it sets "flee the level". Past 50 it forces a phase
   whatever the danger says, then resets (`borg-escape.c:1239-1260`) **[code ✓]**. Separately:
   - `antibounce` at 300 clears the goal, at 500 forgets the tracked monsters, and at 700 leaves
     the level;
   - more than **3 real escapes on one level** (teleports and recalls; phases aren't counted) makes
     it leave the level (`borg-caution.c:855-884`) **[code ✓]**.

4. **Every "go back" decision has a cooldown.** The history's clearest bug class is oscillation:
   the Borg arrived, decided to restock, left, came back, and did it again. The fixes are timers:
   no restock trip in the first 100 turns on a level, 400 before a restock-driven flee, and 200 since
   the last town visit (`borg-prepared.c:828-840`, commits `19944fc9`, `2e1e1d16`) (D6).

5. **Heal only when the heal wins; rest only when it's safe.** A cure potion is used only if
   `danger < current HP + heal amount`, and never if the heal is under a third of the danger ("no
   rope-a-dope"). Otherwise the Borg escapes (`borg-caution.c:60-667`). It rests only if no monster
   is adjacent, no breeder is within 10 squares, no monster in view has a ranged attack, and
   per-monster danger is under HP/10 (`borg_check_rest`, `borg-flow-misc.c:1213-1362`) (D2).

6. **Invisible or unlocated attackers go into a regional memory, not a guess.** Each "It hits you"
   adds 4 × (depth/5 + 1) fear to the 11 × 11 region, and less to neighbouring regions. The fear
   decays by 1 every 10 game turns and is wiped on a new level (`borg-update.c:725-763, 2440-2450`)
   (D1). Fear in a region raises danger there, so the Borg rests and walks elsewhere.

7. **Items are found again by identity at every step.** The Borg's own comment says slots "could
   have moved… since other objects are being taken off and dropped", so it re-locates each item by
   type every step (`borg-item-wear.c:1135-1137`) (D5). This is our Pilot's wrong-letter bug class.
   A related Borg bug: a "use any item that fixes hunger" rule drank Potions of Sleep and Confusion
   (commit `df973d13`). Rules like "use anything that does X" must check side effects.

8. **Even the Borg struggled with free-text messages.** It reads almost everything from game
   memory, but it detects kills by matching message suffixes. That table needed years of patches as
   phrases were missed (commit `28baec67`: "die.", "shrivels away in the light!", plurals, and a
   dropped first character on continued messages) (D6). Our Pilot has no memory to fall back on, so
   its message patterns should be treated as a living list, fed by the unmatched-message logs.

## 2. What doesn't transfer to real-time multiplayer

- **Fallback cascades.** `borg_escape()` tries up to eight methods in turn (spell, scroll, staff,
  activation…). In real time, each failed attempt costs a monster turn. **Pick one action per tier
  in advance**: stairs if on them, else the tagged Phase Door, else the tagged teleport.
- **Heavy simulation.** The Monte-Carlo landing check (100 sampled squares) and "simulate every
  defence and pick the best" assume a paused game and a full danger model of every square. The
  *idea* transfers cheaply: prefer a phase only when few monsters are within 10 squares.
- **Memory reads** (monster HP, permanent walls, item flags). The Pilot has to infer these, or act
  as if it doesn't know them.
- **The pessimism does transfer, and it suits us.** Maximum-roll danger with no AC discount looks
  crude, but our Pilot sees the map up to 0.5 s late, auto-retaliate spends its turns (forum memo
  §1.3), and a potion takes a turn. A built-in safety margin is exactly what a slow reactor needs.
- **Shared levels.** The Borg assumes a level is only its own. In MAngband another player can wake,
  kill or lead monsters to us, which is one more reason to use margin-based thresholds, not exact
  sums.

## 3. Recommendations: Pilot (reflex rules), most valuable first

> **Update (2026-09-28, run post-mortem memo §2):** replayed on Dive03's logs, the 0.6× tier fires
> only 1–5 s before HP falls below 50%. Use 0.3× as "disengage / don't approach / leave on
> arrival", 0.6× as "escape now", drain weight 50 (not 150), and exclude stationary monsters.

1. **Group danger** (answers topic 7). Each tick, compute
   `group = Σ over monsters within reach of (melee_max × speed_x + 150 × drain_blows + 200 × paralysing blows if no FA)`.
   "Within reach" means distance ≤ 1 + speed_x, which covers adjacent monsters and ones that
   can close this turn. Add breath per the existing `pack_breath`. `melee_max` and `drain_blows` are
   now in `danger_table.csv` (added 2026-09-28; **please re-copy the table**). Then, against current
   HP:
   - **> 0.3×**: don't start a fight or walk toward the group; take the stairs back if just arrived;
   - **> 0.6×**: escape now (stairs underfoot, else Phase Door);
   - **> 1.0×**: take the stairs or teleport;
   - **> 2.0×**: leave the level.

   These are the Borg's tiers. They are worth replaying against the Pilot's logs from missions 5–8
   before relying on them, to check they would have fired at the deaths and not on every orc pack.
2. **Stat drain = danger.** The +150 per drain blow in rule 1 makes a drainer count at once. Add the
   Navigator's open suggestion as a backstop: **leave after the second drain from a visible monster**
   (`stat_drained` already exists, `pilot.py:1813`).
3. **A stuck ladder.** `watch_progress` detects no movement (≤10 squares in 2 min). Escalate:
   replan → Phase Door → take any stairs → recall, and reset the ladder on progress. Treat repeated
   failed actions (e.g. the same command three times with no change) like no movement.
4. **Escapes per level.** After **3 teleport-class escapes** (or 3 emergencies, which the Pilot
   already counts within 90 s, `pilot.py:1264-1281`) on one level, leave the level.
5. **Cooldowns on "go back".** Every rule that leaves a level to restock, shop or recover should
   require a minimum time on the level (e.g. 60 s) unless danger forces it.
6. **Rest gate.** Rest only if no monster is adjacent, no breeder is within 10 squares, and no monster
   in view casts or breathes (from `spells` in the table). Otherwise walk away first. The Pilot
   already retreats if something is in view (`pilot.py:1312-1319`); add the breeder and caster
   checks.
7. **Invisible attackers.** Keep a decaying "unseen hits" score per region of the map (the Borg's
   `fear_region`), and avoid resting or pathing there. The Pilot's `unseen_attacker` flee is the
   emergency version; the region memory stops it walking back into the same spot.
8. **Items by identity.** Resolve every item by name or inscription at the moment of use, never by
   a letter remembered from an earlier report (the Pilot accepts both, `pilot.py:1923-1945`).

## 4. Recommendations: Navigator (doctrine)

- **Depth by level: a decision for the user and the Architect.** The Borg requires clvl ≥ dl
  everywhere, and more for warriors below dl20. Our doctrine dives under-levelled to move fast.
  Suggested middle ground for the throwaway warrior, to test in missions:
  - `max_depth` ≤ (clvl + 4) × 50 ft down to 1000 ft;
  - clvl ≥ dl below that;
  - never below 1000 ft without Free Action.
- **Adopt the Borg's gear gates as `max_depth` checkpoints:** See Invisible by 500 ft (earlier than
  our 1000 ft); Free Action by 1000 ft; resist fire + two basics by 1050 ft; all four basics by
  1300 ft. **Keep our earlier resist-poison and resist-confusion checkpoints** (1000 ft and before
  1900 ft) while we dive under-levelled; the Borg can wait until 2000 ft because its HP is higher at
  each depth.
- **Consumables to carry:** from 500 ft, at least 3 CCW and 2 teleport-class escapes; from 1000 ft,
  at least 4 CSW+CCW and 4 escapes (Borg `borg_restock`). Below those counts, head back to town, but
  not within the first minute on a new level.
- **Recall depth:** recall down only to a depth the character meets the checkpoints for (the Borg
  uses 60% of its maximum depth when it isn't prepared for more).

## 5. Tooling ideas

- **A death snapshot:** the Borg can write the map, recent messages, stats and its internal danger
  values on death (`borg_save_death`, the `.map` snapshot, `docs/hacking/borg.rst`). A Pilot
  equivalent (the last 30 s of danger scores, group sums and reflex decisions) would make every death
  a post-mortem (backlog topic 3).
- **Continuous test runs:** the Borg's `cheat_death` keeps a test character playing after death.
  The equivalent for us is automatic re-creation of throwaway characters on the test server.
