## 1. Recall arrivals: the level told us, and the Pilot didn't listen

**m24, re-traced from the logs** **[log ✓]**:

| time after the WoR read | event |
|---|---|
| 0 | WoR read in town (12:25:21) |
| +16 s | "You feel yourself yanked downwards!" **"You feel there is something special about this level."** 600 ft, HP 265/265 |
| +16 to +88 s | `goal explore` walks into the ring corridor of a dark inner room; no Door/Stair Location (rod in the pack), no Magic Mapping (3 scrolls) |
| +88 s | an arrow; `structure`: "a dark inner room" |
| +108 s | band within 10, "flee: no stairs known" → **reads the last WoR**; pit identified at the same moment (95 awake orcs) |
| +127 s | yanked home at 158/265 |

**Mechanics** **[code ✓]**:
- **The feeling is a pit/nest/vault warning.**
  - A nest, pit or vault sets `good_item_flag` whenever `randint1(Depth²+1) < 300` (`generate.c:1958`, also 2271, 2520, 2560). That is always true at levels ≤ 17 (850 ft).
  - The flag forces feeling 1, "You feel there is something special about this level." (`generate.c:4239`). An artifact on the floor sets it too.
  - It is blanked to "Looks like any other level." if the player generated a level less than 1000 game ticks earlier, about 13 s (`generate.c:4244`).
  - "You feel your luck is turning..." is only rating 21–30: out-of-depth monsters or items. It is not a pit signal.
- **A recall arrival is a random empty floor square** (`new_player_spot`, `generate.c:315-338`).
  - Only vault squares are excluded. There is no distance from monsters and no grace period.
  - **Pit and nest monsters are generated awake** (`generate.c:1974, 2280-2324`). Ordinary ones start asleep.
- **A stairs arrival puts you on the way back.** Going down places you on the level's last up staircase, and going up on its last down staircase
  (`dungeon.c:2127-2136`; scatter d=0). Taking the stairs straight back is an instant escape that costs no scroll. A recall arrival
  has none.
- **A second WoR read cancels a pending recall** ("A tension leaves the air around you...", `spells2.c:1195`). The return scroll must
  never be read while a recall is already counting down.
- **No instant exits are sold:**
  - Deep Descent doesn't exist in 1.5.
  - Scrolls of Teleportation and Teleport Level aren't in any shop.
  - The Staff of Teleportation is 2000 g at the Alchemist (R1, `shop_stock.csv`).
- **Detection range:** Door/Stair Location and Magic Mapping cover ±52 columns × ±23 rows, about 53% × 71% of the 198×66 level
  (`spells2.c:1212`).
- **How common orc pits are:** about 2.5% of levels at 600 ft (R1's simulation of room selection). With nests and vaults, the "special" feeling is more common than that.

**Base rate (R2):**
- Of the 13 recall arrivals in m17–m24, 6 were bad (HP < 60%, or the WoR re-read within 5 min). Four of those were m22's mob levels, which got into trouble 4–8 minutes in, while exploring.
- **m24 is the only arrival that lost its return scroll at once.**
- Door/Stair Location or Magic Mapping was in the pack on m24 and on three of the m22 arrivals, and was used on none of them before the trouble. The Pilot's own
  Door/Stair use fired once (m19, +9 s, under `goal dive`), never under `goal explore`.
- Stairs from town were used 3 times, with no bad outcomes.

**Recommended arrival protocol (Pilot):**
1. **On every recall arrival**, zap Door/Stair Location at once. The rod is free, and this is the one arrival with no staircase underfoot.
   If no stairs are in range, read Magic Mapping.
2. **If the arrival said "something special about this level"** (≤ 850 ft), treat the level as having a pit, nest or vault:
   - set the goal to reach the nearest stairs first;
   - put avoid zones on unlit inner rooms (you added this after m24);
   - explore only after a staircase is known and within about 30 squares.
3. **When a band forms and stairs are known, take the stairs, not WoR.** Phase Door to break contact first, as the A1 set-piece says.
   With one WoR left, read it only when no stairs are within reach and HP is falling, and never while a recall is pending.
4. **Trip design:**
   - With exactly 2 WoR and gold under the price of a third, go down by stairs (your new `via=stairs`).
   - Or recall and keep the second scroll strictly for the end of the trip. Use stairs-back-and-forth (each arrival lands on a staircase) to leave bad levels.
5. **Lead from R2:** 4 of 13 recalls landed 50–100 ft shallower than the `{@R}` inscription asked for (m17, m21, and twice in m22). I haven't checked why.
   It matters if the gate assumes the exact depth.

## 2. Depth band for Dive04 (clvl 22, 265 HP, no Free Action, STR drained)

**Our missions** (`xp_by_depth.csv`, m17–m24, per dungeon minute):

| band | minutes | XP/min | lowest HP |
|---|---|---|---|
| 300–450 ft | 57 | **8.5** | 72% |
| 500–650 ft | 71 | **58** | 41% (m22) |
| 700+ ft | 36 | 42 | 27% (m22) |

m24 spent 26.5 of its 28.5 dungeon minutes at 300–450 ft (6.3 XP/min). Its 1.5 minutes at 600 ft gave 310 XP. **That depth choice fully explains m24's low XP.**
Town took 102 of the 285 mission minutes (36%).

**Model** (`depth_value.csv`, clvl 22, counting only monsters the hunt check allows):

| depth | XP/kill | HP lost/kill | XP per HP | P(level holds a LEAVE or p90 > 100% race) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 300 ft | 2.1 | 0.4 | 6.0 | 2% |
| 400 ft | 4.0 | 0.9 | 4.7 | 43% |
| 500 ft | 6.2 | 1.3 | 4.9 | 61% |
| 650 ft | 10.3 | 2.3 | 4.6 | 51% |
| 750 ft | 15.9 | 2.9 | 5.6 | 45% |
| 1000 ft | 27.0 | 4.7 | 5.8 | 39% |

**XP per HP lost is flat from 400 ft to 1500 ft, while XP per kill rises about 2.5× from 400 to 650 ft.** Going deeper costs no more HP per
XP. What limits depth is escape capacity against the occasional severe level, and bad arrivals.

**Recommendation:**
- **Work 500–650 ft**, with the arrival protocol.
- **Go to 700 ft only when stocked:** at least 3 WoR, at least 5 CCW, Phase at least 10.
- **Stay above about 750 ft until Free Action is found.** That is where paralysers appear (Homunculus is level 15; ghouls and carrion crawlers deeper).
- **Don't plan trips at 300–450 ft** except to rebuild gold when the gate refuses deeper ones: they earn about 7× less XP per minute.
- **Restore Strength** is the best gold-for-XP buy (tier 3 already). STR sets blows, and blows set the kill time that every number above depends on.

**Ratings out of date for this character.** At Dive04's profile, `fight_dive04.csv` gives several LEAVE/AVOID uniques a duel p90 of 30% of HP or less:
- **Brodda:** rated LEAVE for melee strength only. p90 24% of HP; his drop averages ~2,200.
- **Wormtongue:** p90 17%; drop ~9,700, DROP_GREAT. His rating comes from frost bolts, HEAL and SLOW, and you have no Free Action.

**Proposal for the hunt check.** Keep the AVOID/LEAVE veto only where the reason is qualitative: paralysis without FA, drains, breeders,
invisible, TELE_TO, summoners, escorts (the band rule). Let `fight_sim` decide where the rating was about strength.
**What I'll do.** If you agree, I'll add a `veto_reason` column (`qualitative` or `strength`) to `monsters.csv` in the next rulebook build.

## 3. Plan news in the latency metrics

`latency.py` counts any attention event whose `what` is in URGENT as an alert, including those the plan raises and handles itself
(`structure`, `danger_seen`…).
- **Tag them** (`source: "plan"`) and **exclude them from alert→contact and alert→order**. Those metrics measure how fast the Navigator reacts
  when *it* has to decide.
- **Report the count of plan news per mission separately**, as a measure of how much the plan tells the Navigator.
- **Keep untagged** any plan event that asks the Navigator for a decision (e.g. a refused hunt it may override).
