## 3. Timing and odds: `constants.csv` (104 entries, K3, code-checked)

The ones a policy will use:
- A player turn costs `level_speed(depth)/1000` ticks at 75 fps: **0.49 s in town, 0.60 s at 50 ft, ~0.67 s at 550–750 ft,
  1.0 s at 3000 ft**.
- **Word of Recall:** 15–34 "blocks". Each block is one player turn at normal time, **a tenth of that while resting with no awake monster in
  line of sight** (time factor 1000%). Reading WoR, then resting out of sight, makes it ~10× faster in real time.
- Traps are uniform over 16 types at any depth; per level, randint1(clamp(D/3, 2, 10)) of them. Search is 14%/adjacent square per pass (Dive04).
- Device failure, recharge, enchant, chests, Phase Door range 10, teleport ranges and regen per tick are all there with code lines.

**A lever the Pilot is leaving unused: the low-HP time bubble** **[code ✓ `xtra2.c:5180-5207`, `mdefines.h:271`]:**
- Below `hitpoint_warn × 10`% HP, time for the player, *and for the monsters near them*, runs at a **constant 1/5** (`CONSTANT_TIME_FACTOR 5`).
  It's not proportional to HP, as the comment in `pilot.py:5135-5137` assumes.
- Relative speeds don't change, but every turn takes 5× the real time. That's 5× more wall-clock for the Pilot's checks and the Navigator's 4–6 s
  response, exactly when it matters. Resting and running (no monster in sight) override the bubble.
- **The Pilot launches with `--hpwarn 0`** (`pilot.py:5141`), turning this off, because its timers are wall-clock and replans fired between turns in
  mission 7.
- **Suggestion:** move the Pilot's timers to game turns (energy or turn counts), then use `--hpwarn 5`/`6`. Every close call in m15–23
  (below 40%) would then have played out at 1/5 speed. Worth a set-piece run (A1/A2) with and without it.
