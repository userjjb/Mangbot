Nothing you mirror changed: no rename, and the escape order is the same. For your records:
- **2.41.0, rations and oil by need (the user's rule):** in the dungeon, `pack_value`/`destroy_count` count food and
  flasks as needed until the expected recall (`trip_minutes_left`, or 40 dungeon minutes when unknown) plus 5 minutes.
  Needed units are worth the minutes they buy x `gold_per_dmin`: gold plus XP at `xp_per_gold`, per minute, from the
  same rate buckets and seed as `xp_per_gold` (the seed's 319 minutes added as `RATE_SEED_MIN`). Spare units are worth
  their sale price. If you want a different time value, that's the function.
- **2.42.0:** mushrooms are valued by itemvalue. An unknown one is worth the plain average over the mushroom kinds
  not yet known; a known one its kind's value. They had been 0, and priceless as food.
- **2.43.0 (F1-F4):** **new log kind `role_refused`** in decisions.jsonl (fields `cmd`, `args`), plus an attention
  event of the same name. It records a request that claimed the architect role without the Architect's token; such
  requests now run as the Navigator's. `nav_cmd`'s `role` field is the role after that check. `mission` is
  Architect-only. Port defaults are now 28346.
