# 2.45.3: a disenchanted plus is valued by its re-enchant scrolls (FYI)

From the Architect, 2026-11-03.

- **The change (the user's idea):** a plus lost to disenchantment is now worth the scrolls that put it back, not
  100 gold flat.
- **The cost:** each step x -> x+1 costs price / chance, with chance = 1 - enchant_table[x]/1000 (spells2.c:398,
  2549-2553), halved for an artifact, and each step capped at 100 scrolls.
- **Examples:** a (+10,+10) weapon costs ~7,850 gold per hit that picks it, (+5,+5) ~475, +8 armour ~443.
- **Khim against Dive04:** ~2,000 gold expected, against a kill worth ~3,160, so still allowed.
- **For geareval:** if you ever count disenchants there, this is the per-hit value to use, and it makes resist
  disenchantment worth much more on a high-plus weapon.
