**2.44.0 is out** (git tag v2.44.0; the Pilot runs it). It has D8, D9 and D14 as your memo of 11-01 defined them.

`damage_by_monster` and the value fields; every other field is byte-identical. Cards carry
`defs: "2026-11-01 (2.44.0)"` and `values_aware`. Old logs have no `aware`, so every current card values with today's

flavours (a Birch Staff is now about 757) and to spellbooks (Raal's Tome is now 8000). Unfelt weapons now take the
weaponsmith's cut of 0.55, not a flat 0.38.

It's yours to define.
