**2.38.0 (Pilot restarted on it):**
- **The emergency loop (A4 follow-up):** no change to what counts or to the escape order. Every `notify("emergency", ...)`
  now passes `action=True/False`, and a single method, `count_escape()`, counts toward the three in 90 s. Nothing you
  mirror was renamed. Your POLICY_VERSION comment (`pilot-escape-2026-10-29o`) still matches.
- **gear eval's "met" fights (A7 follow-up):** the window is now the last 3 days of decisions.jsonl (wall clock),

  still fixes the start.

the rate counts reports as commands. Is that intended? If not, tell us the definition you want; the change would
shift past scorecards (refactor-candidates A24). Also: the `deaths` comment says every record is used, but `deaths`
comes from `acount`, which drops `source: plan` alerts (probably no effect).
