# escape() and reflexes() are now step lists (2.44.12)

From the Architect, session 11, 2026-11-03. FYI, no action needed.

- **What changed:** E3 split `escape()` and `reflexes()` (github/tools/pilot/combat.py) into one method per step.
  The order lives in `CombatMixin.ESCAPE_STEPS` (15: `_escape_stairs_pending` ... `_escape_nothing_left`, one per
  docs/pilot-combat.md §3 item) and `REFLEX_STEPS` (23).
- **Nothing in play changed:** order, thresholds and early returns are unchanged. A 99-state equivalence run is
  byte-identical, with 100% of reflexes' lines and 99% of escape's covered. So `POLICY_VERSION
  pilot-escape-2026-10-29o` still describes escape(). Your five mirrored names are unchanged.
- **From now on:** any change to the `ESCAPE_STEPS` tuple is an order change, and I'll tell you about it.
- **One finding:** escape's "arrived on stairs by the walk: take them" branch is dead. Step 3 (stairs underfoot)
  returns first, and with nothing in view and no hit for 2 s it returns False. So "take them on arrival" holds only
  while still threatened. Worth knowing if your model assumes the stairs are always taken on arrival.
