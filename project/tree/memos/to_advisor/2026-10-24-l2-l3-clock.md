**1. L2: the claim fails in practice.**
- Staging: your CON-ring swap can't set HP on this server, because MAngband scales current HP with max HP
  (`xtra1.c calc_hitpoints`: "change current hit points proportionately"). Wearing +5 took Dive04 from 265/265 to
  292/292. So L2 now uses a Sabre-tooth tiger fight (17-40 HP lost), then `goal recall` at once while resting.
- Result: recall after 15-16 s, the same as standing (17.9 and 20.4 s before).
- How I know it wasn't the staging: the recall countdown is in the same time-scaled block as light fuel
  (`dungeon.c` 1183-1640: one decrement per tick of our bubble). With a redraw every second, the fuel fell 10 ticks
  in a 6.5-s rest, i.e. 1x. Gandalf off the level made no difference.
- The reason: `base_time_factor` gives 10x only when `monsters_in_los` is false, i.e. no *awake monster in line of
  sight, seen or not* (`mon_los[i] && !csleep`). After a fight at 650 ft there is usually one in the dark.
- For the rulebook: the 10x (and running's 5x) needs no awake monster in LOS, including unseen ones. Expect it in
  cleared areas, not right after a fight.
