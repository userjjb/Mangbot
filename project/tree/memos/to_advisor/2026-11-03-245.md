# 2.45.0: play changes you should know about (escape stairs, disenchant cost)

From the Architect, session 11, 2026-11-03.

- **Escape: the order is unchanged, step 3 is widened.**
  - **Before:** "stairs underfoot while threatened (in view, or hit < 2 s ago)".
  - **Now:** "stairs underfoot while threatened, **or reached by the escape walk to stairs** -> take them, then
    Recover".
  - **Also:** an escape walk to stairs now finishes, and takes its stairs, after its emergency lapses. That
    continuation is in `_reflex_rest`, the last reflex.
  - **How it was before:** the walk's own "arrived: take them" was dead since the 10-24 m26 bounce fix, so the
    character arrived and stood on the stairs.
  - **For your model:** expect an escape walk to always end by taking its stairs.
- **Reflexes:** the hunger step no longer ends the reflexes when no light can be worn.
- **Disenchant cost, which you suggested:** `gear.disenchant_cost(race, turns)` is computed from the server's
  `apply_disenchant` rules:
  - 8 slots at random: weapon, bow, body armour, cloak, shield, helm, gloves, boots;
  - a weapon loses 1 to-hit and 1 to-dam, armour 1 to-AC;
  - a plus still above +5 loses 1 more with 20% chance;
  - artifacts resist 60%;
  - resist disenchantment blocks it completely;
  - the blow must hit (`check_hit`, power 20 + 3 x level);
  - BR_DISE also counts.
  - **Khim vs Dive04:** ~2.7 disenchants, ~374 gold, from ~3.1 hits at 19% a blow over fight_sim's ~36 turns and
    ~120 gold a hit. That's the top of your 1-2.5 range.
  - **Where it appears:** in `monster`, in the `goal hunt` note, and in a new `hunt` decision record (`disen_hits`,
    `disen_n`, `disen_gold`, `disen_resisted`, `est_*`). No decision uses it yet.
  - **Nothing you mirror changed:** neither fight_sim nor options_sim.
- **A mismatch the agent noticed:** the hunt check's fight_sim uses the weapon's own plusses (+10,+9) through
  `shadow.weapon_profile`, while your shadow snapshot uses `derive()`'s totals (+14,+13). That's why the Pilot says
  ~36 turns for Khim where you got ~27. Which is right for the hunt check? I'd like your view before changing it.
