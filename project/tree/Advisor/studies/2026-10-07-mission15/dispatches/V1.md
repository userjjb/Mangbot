## Answer
The 450 ft block is **build_type4 ("large room"), sub-case 3 (inner pillar), with the optional "inner rooms" add-on**, centred at (49,148). It is not a pit or a nest. The coordinates match to the square. The outer room is rows 44-54 x cols 136-160. A one-wide ring corridor runs around an inner room (rows 47-51 x cols 139-157). The 11x3 block (rows 48-50 x cols 143-153) sits in the middle of that inner room. Its two secret doors are at **(48 or 50, 145) and (48 or 50, 151)**, each row chosen independently. They are reached by searching from (47|51, 144-146) and (47|51, 150-152), or from inside the closets. The 400 ft "one-wide corridor around a rectangle" is the same type-4 family, or a nest/pit (type 5/6). Nests and pits are unlit and have no loot. Their monsters are awake, and orcs open secret doors.

## Findings
1. **Room choice, per placement attempt** [SEEN] (generate.c:3187-3246; table :259-270). There are 50 attempts per level. A room is "unusual" with probability D/200, where D is the dungeon level (450 ft = 9). Then k=randint0(100), and a second D/200 roll allows the very unusual types. Order: k<10 type 8, k<25 type 7, k<40 type 6 (pit), k<50 type 5 (nest). These are tried only if the very-unusual roll passed. Then k<25 type 4, k<50 type 3, otherwise type 2. A failed attempt falls through to the next type. Minimum levels (room[] 5th field): types 3 and 4 need D>=3, types 5, 6 and 7 need D>=5, type 8 needs D>=10. For D=9, per attempt: type 4 ~1.1% (about 0.5 per level, so common), pit ~0.03% (~1.5%/level), nest ~0.02% (~1%/level). For D=5: pit ~0.5%/level. For D=12: pit ~2.7%/level. [INFERRED: arithmetic on the code, ignoring blocks already occupied.] Only one pit or nest per level (`dun->crowded`, :3057, :3120). No moat rooms exist in this generate.c (grep "moat": none).
2. **Lighting** [SEEN] (:1134, :1239, :1412): types 1-4 are lit if D <= randint1(25). That is 68% at D=9, 56% at D=12, 84% at D=5. Nests and pits are always unlit (`place_double_room(Depth, FALSE, ...)`, :1871, :2040). A lit room lights the whole block, including the ring and the inner room.
3. **Walls look identical** [SEEN] (terrain.txt:306-361). Secret door (0x30), granite basic/inner/outer/solid all have `G:#:w`, and the mimic is 56. So inner walls, outer walls and secret doors look the same as granite. Nothing on the map separates a secret door from a wall. It is only found by search (cmd1.c:553-559: "You have found a secret door", +1 exp).
4. **Geometry of type 4, 5 and 6** [SEEN] (:1406-1419, 955-959, 932-953). The room centre (Y,X) is a block centre: Y=((2*y0+1)*11)/2, so y0=4 gives 49. X=148 comes from x0=13. The outer wall is rows Y-5..Y+5 x cols X-12..X+12 (11 x 25). The ring corridor (floor) is rows Y-4 and Y+4 over cols X-11..X+11, and cols X-11 and X+11 over rows Y-4..Y+4. The inner wall is rows Y-3 and Y+3 over cols X-10..X+10, and cols X-10 and X+10. The inner room floor is rows Y-2..Y+2 x cols X-9..X+9 (5 x 19). At (49,148) that is outer rows 44-54 and cols 136-160, ring rows 45 and 53 and cols 137 and 159, inner room rows 47-51 and cols 139-157. All inner walls are FEAT_WALL_INNER (granite look).
5. **Secret door #1** [SEEN] (:1429-1435, 1880-1885, 2049-2054). Exactly one of four, 25% each, at the centre of a side of the inner wall: (Y-3,X), (Y+3,X), (Y,X-10), (Y,X+10). For 49,148 these are **(46,148), (52,148), (49,138), (49,158)**. From the ring they are searched from the three ring squares in front: (45,147-149), (53,147-149), (48-50,137), (48-50,159). The same three-wide strip inside the inner room also works.
6. **Type 4 sub-case 3 + "inner rooms"** [SEEN] (:1497-1568). Probability is 1/5 for case 3, then 1/3 for the add-on, so about 7% of type-4 rooms. It always builds a 3x3 solid pillar at rows Y-1..Y+1 x cols X-1..X+1, and 50% builds two more 3x3 pillars. The add-on then builds walls on rows Y-1 and Y+1 over cols X-5..X+5, plus end caps (Y,X-5) and (Y,X+5). At (49,148) this is exactly rows 48 and 50 x cols 143-153. The pillar splits the interior into two closets, W (49,144-146) and E (49,150-152). The secret doors are `place_secret_door(Y-3+randint1(2)*2, X-3)` and the same at X+3. That puts each door on row Y-1 or Y+1, so **(48|50,145) and (48|50,151)**, chosen independently. Monsters are `vault_monsters` around (Y,X-2) and (Y,X+2) with randint1(2) calls each, and 1/3 chance of an object at each of (49,146) and (49,150). Door squares (48,145) and (50,145) are both diagonal-adjacent to (49,146).
7. **vault_monsters has no `break`** [SEEN] (:1076-1100). Each of the 9 tries places a monster (asleep, groups allowed, monster_level D+2) on any empty floor grid within distance 1 of the point. One "num=1" call therefore fills up to the whole 3x3 neighbourhood, about 6 monsters on open floor. This differs from the intent of the code comment and probably from Vanilla. [INFERRED: I did not check Vanilla.] A closet cell pair can hold only 2. Monsters are normal level-appropriate monsters, not themed.
8. **Other type-4 sub-cases (each 1/5)** [SEEN] (:1426-1652). All use the same secret door #1 from finding 5, except case 3's inner rooms in finding 6.

   - Case 4: a checkerboard of inner walls (every cell with odd x+y). It has randint1(3) monster calls at X-5 and X+5, 1-3 traps at each of X-3 and X+3, and `vault_objects` x3 (75% item, 25% gold).
   - Case 5: a cross of inner wall through the inner room, making 4 chambers of 2x9. The doors are 4 secret doors, either on rows Y-3 and Y+3 at X±i (i=1-10) or on the side walls at rows Y±i (i=1-3). It has 2-3 `vault_objects` and 4 monster calls of randint1(4) each at (Y±1, X±4).
9. **Type 3 (cross room)** [SEEN] (:1226-1383). A 3-wide N-S arm (7-9 tall) and a 3-tall E-W arm (7-23 wide) share a 3x3 centre. Sub-case 2 (1/4) makes that centre a closed 3x3 ring of inner wall with one secret door at the N, S, W or E midpoint of the ring: (Y-1,X), (Y+1,X), (Y,X-1), (Y,X+1). Sub-case 3 (1/4) has a 1/3 chance of "pinching" the arms shut, then 1/3 of that with secret doors at the 4 pinch points. It can also have a plus or a pillar. 10. **Nests (type 5)** [SEEN] (:1852-1977). The layout is the same double room (unlit) with secret door #1. Type = randint1(D): <30 jelly, <50 animal, else undead. 11. **Pits (type 6)** [SEEN] (:2024-2325). The layout is the same with secret door #1, unlit. Type = randint1(D): <20 orc, <40 troll, <60 giant, <80 dragon, else demon. So **at D<=19 it is always an orc pit** (D=20 can also give a troll pit). 16 draws of get_mon_num(D+10) are sorted by level and every second entry is kept (8 kinds). Placement: the outer rows Y±2 and the edge columns hold the weakest kind. Kinds 1-4 form concentric bands going inwards. A 3x3 core holds kinds 5-7, with the strongest at (Y,X). All 95 cells are filled, **awake, no groups**. Orcs in monster.txt up to L19: Snaga L6, Cave orc L7, Hill orc L8, Orc shaman L9, Black orc L13, Half-orc L15, Uruk L16, Orc captain L18. Orc shaman casts (BLINK, CAUSE_1, MISSILE). Black orc, Uruk and Orc captain have ARROW_2 at 1_IN_9. Rating +10 plus the same good-item flag. No objects.
