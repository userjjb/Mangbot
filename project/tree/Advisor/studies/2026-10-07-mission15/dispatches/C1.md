# Dispatch: Chests and searching (mission 15, C1)
- Brief: chest kinds/traps/disarm/open/contents formulas and search() mechanics, with Dive04 odds at clvl 14.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-07; sources: server/cmd2.c, cmd1.c, object2.c, object1.c, tables.c, xtra1.c, init1.c, mdefines.h; lib/edit/object.txt, p_race.txt, p_class.txt; commentary.jsonl line 60 (grep only). Scratch: scratch/c1.py. All paths below are under /projectnb/jbrcs/mangband/github/.

## Answer
- A chest's trap is NOT visible on sight. It is learned only by a successful search() roll on an adjacent square: 's' command, search mode, or a step while walking (the walking search is the rare spontaneous one). Waiting or holding never searches.
- Search covers only the 3x3 around you, so a secret door two squares away is never found.
- Dive04 has skill_dis 40 and skill_srh 14. Disarm success is (40 - pval)% per attempt. Of the attempts that fail, 5/40 set the trap off. Lock-picking is the same formula, with a floor of 2%.
- Chest trap type and loot quality both come from pval = randint1(chest kind level). At depths 5-12 almost all chests are small wooden, pval 1-5, so the traps are poison gas or a STR/CON needle. Those are cheap and mostly non-lethal.
- A chest drops 2 (wooden), 4 (iron) or 6 (steel) items, or gold in 75% of the slots for small chests, at object level |pval|+10.

   | Large iron | 6 | 35 | 1000 | 150 | 4 slots |
   | Small steel | 3 | 45 | 500 | 200 | 6 slots, gold-capable |
   | Large steel | 7 | 55 | 1000 | 250 | 6 slots |
   | Ruined chest | 0 | 0 | 250 | 0 | pval stays 0, no trap, no loot |

   - All are tval 7, and the A: line is `level/1` (min depth/rarity 1), except the ruined chest at A:75/1.
   - Chests never stack (object2.c:1422, 1617).
   - A chest kind can appear only when the (possibly boosted) object level reaches its A: level. get_obj_num boosts the level with 1/GREAT_OBJ (=20, mdefines.h:172) odds (object2.c:646-665). So at L5-12, large wooden and above are boost-only.
2. pval assignment [SEEN] (object2.c:3139-3150). pval = randint1(kind level), uniform. Above 55 it becomes 55+randint0(5) (this is unreachable for the table's max level 55). Ruined chests are skipped. So pval is both the trap-table index and the lock/trap difficulty. Ranges are small wooden 1-5, large wooden 1-15, small iron 1-25, large iron 1-35, small steel 1-45, large steel 1-55.
3. Trap table [SEEN] (tables.c:2313-2378, `chest_traps[64]`). Index is pval. Indices 56-63 are all EXPLODE|SUMMON.
   - Locked only, no trap: pvals 6, 16, 26, 36, 46.
   - Small wooden, pval 1-5: POISON, STR, CON, STR, CON.
   - Large wooden adds: 7 POISON, 8 POISON, 9 STR, 10 CON, 11 POISON, 12-14 STR+CON, 15 SUMMON.
   - Small iron adds: 17 STR, 18 CON, 19 PARALYZE, 20 STR+CON, 21 SUMMON, 22 PARALYZE, 23 STR, 24 CON, 25 EXPLODE.
   - Higher levels: explosions, summons, and multi-trap combinations.
4. Trap effects [SEEN] (cmd2.c:369-439). Several can fire in one chest, in this order:
   - STR needle: "A small needle has pricked you!", 1d4 damage, do_dec_stat(STR).
   - CON needle: same message and damage, do_dec_stat(CON).
   - POISON: "puff of green gas", poisoned for +10+1d20 turns, unless resist or oppose poison.
   - PARALYZE: "puff of yellow gas", paralyzed +10+1d20 turns unless free action. Dive04 has none, so this is the dangerous one, especially combined with SUMMON (pvals 21 and 31+).
   - SUMMON: "enveloped in a cloud of smoke", 2+1d3 summon_specific at the chest square, at dungeon depth.
   - EXPLODE: 5d8 damage, "an exploding chest", and the contents are destroyed (pval=0). The chest itself survives.
   - I did not check whether do_dec_stat honors sustain.
5. Trap display and discovery [SEEN].
   - object_desc (object1.c:1648-1722) shows nothing while the chest is not "known".
   - Once known it shows (Locked) for no-trap pvals, (Poison Needle), (Gas Trap), (Explosion Device), (Summoning Runes), (Multiple Traps), (empty), (disarmed) or (unlocked).
   - search() on a chest square (cmd1.c:568-584) sets it known with "You have discovered a trap on the chest!". It does this only if chest_traps[pval] != 0. Locked-only chests (pval 6, 16 and so on) never become known and cannot be disarmed ("I don't see any traps"). Open them directly.
   - Walking onto the chest does not reveal it. Only search() at cmd1.c:507 does.
   - The user's remark (commentary.jsonl line 60) that only explicit searching or walking reveals it is CONFIRMED. The Navigator's reading of "searching eventually reveals" is also right.
   - Possible bug [INFERRED]: object1.c:1665 and 1679 index chest_traps[] with a negative pval for disarmed chests. This is an out-of-bounds read, so the (disarmed)/(unlocked) label is unreliable. It does not change the mechanics.
6. Disarm [SEEN] (cmd2.c:1022-1090).
   - i = skill_dis, divided by 10 if blind/no light, and by 10 again if confused/hallucinating.
   - j = max(2, i - pval). Success if randint0(100) < j.
   - Success: "You have disarmed the chest.", exp += pval, pval = -pval. This also removes the lock, because pval <= 0 skips the lock check on open.
   - Failure: if i > 5 and randint1(i) > 5, "You failed to disarm" and it repeats. Otherwise, "You set off a trap!" and chest_trap fires.
   - So, per attempt, set-off probability = (1-s)*5/i with s = j/100.
7. Dive04 disarm skill, computed [SEEN formulas, INFERRED arithmetic].
   - Half-Orc R: dis -3, srh 0, fos 7 (p_race.txt:115-117). Warrior C: dis 25, srh 14, fos 2 (p_class.txt:85). Warrior X: x_dis 10, x_srh 0, x_fos 0 (p_class.txt:86). Parse order is dis,dev,sav,stl,srh,fos,thn,thb (init1.c:1160-1196). I assumed the race R: line parses in the same order. The race parser code (init1.c:1887-1891) is consistent with that but I did not read the whole block.

8. Opening [SEEN] (cmd2.c:951-1013).
   - If pval > 0 the chest is locked. Picking uses the same i/j formula, so success is max(2, 40-pval)%. It gives "You have picked the lock." and +1 exp. Failure gives "You failed to pick the lock." and keeps repeating, with no set-off on failure.
   - After the lock is picked, chest_trap() fires unconditionally, trapped or not, whether or not you discovered it. Then chest_death() drops the loot.
   - Opening a trapped, non-disarmed chest therefore always triggers the trap. Disarm first, or accept it. At pval ≤ 5 the trap is poison or a STR/CON needle only.
   - The "lock-picking 14 failures" in mission 13 is consistent with 35-39% success. It is an expected sequence of retries and implies nothing odd.
9. Contents [SEEN] (cmd2.c:284-362).
   - number = (sval % 4) * 2, giving 2, 4 or 6 slots as in the table above. Loot is generated only if pval != 0. A disarmed chest has negative pval, which is nonzero, so it still drops, using ABS(pval).
   - Per slot there are 20 tries to find a clean floor grid, scattered at distance (i+15)/15. A slot is silently lost if none is found.
   - object_level = |pval| + 10 for the duration of the drop.
   - Small chests: 75% of slots call place_gold. Otherwise, and always for large chests, place_object(good=FALSE, great=FALSE) is called.
   - Nested chests are blocked (object2.c:690).
   - place_object (object2.c:3625-3700): get_obj_num(level) has a 1/20 chance of a level boost. It also takes the better of 2 or 3 picks (40%, then 50% of the rest, per the "p < 60" and "p < 10" code), so effective quality is a bit above the nominal level. apply_magic uses object_level, with a 1/1000 special-artifact chance.
   - Gold (object2.c:3860-3910): i = (randint1(L+2)+2)/2 - 1, where L is object_level (=|pval|+10 in a chest). With 1/20 chance add randint1(L+1). Cap at index 17. The coin cost table for indices 0-17 is 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,12,14,16,18,20,24,28,32,40,80 (object.txt:3847-3935). Value = base + 8*randint1(base) + randint1(8).
   - Expected value per gold slot (my script): pval 1 about 43 gold, pval 3 about 47, pval 5 about 52, pval 10 about 65, pval 15 about 78. A small wooden chest therefore gives about 2 slots at 75% gold, about 75 gold plus about 0.5 items on average.
   - Opening gives no extra exp beyond +1 for the lock. A successful disarm gives exp = pval.
10. search() [SEEN] (cmd1.c:507-585).
   - chance = skill_srh, divided by 10 if blind/no light and by 10 again if confused/hallucinating.
   - For each of the 9 squares around the player (including the player's own square), the check is `randint0(100) < chance`, independent per square.
   - On a hit, it can find an invisible trap (FEAT_INVIS becomes a real trap via pick_trap, "You have found a trap."), a secret door (FEAT_SECRET becomes a closed door, "You have found a secret door.", +1 exp), or a chest trap (see finding 5).
   - Each find calls disturb(0,0), which does not cancel search mode.
   - Dive04 skill_srh = 0 + 14 + 0 = 14, so each 's' finds a given adjacent secret door or trap chest with 14% chance. Expected about 7 searches, and the 90% quantile is about 15 searches.
   - No class or race search bonus applies at clvl 14 (x_srh = 0). Equipment TR1_SEARCH adds 5 per pval.
   - Secret doors and trap chests beyond distance 1 are never found.
11. When search() is called [SEEN].
    - `s` (do_cmd_search, cmd2.c:216-236): one search, costs one game turn of energy (level_speed).
    - Search mode (do_cmd_toggle_search, cmd2.c:242-268): sets p_ptr->searching. Speed is -10 while it is on (xtra1.c:2782-2791, display hides it). It does NOT search by itself. It makes every successful move_player step call search() once more (cmd1.c:2019-2023).
    - Walking, spontaneous (cmd1.c:2012-2017), after each successful move: search() runs if skill_fos >= 50 or randint0(50 - fos) == 0. Dive04 fos = 7 + 2 = 9, so 1/41 per step.
    - A step in search mode runs search() once for normal flow, plus possibly once more spontaneously.
    - Tunneling: 25% per turn calls search() (cmd2.c:2002).
    - Standing still (`,` stay, `g` hold, do_cmd_hold_or_stay, cmd2.c:3159) does NOT search. The search calls at cmd2.c:3185-3199 are inside `#if 0`. This matches the user's comment.
    - Search mode is canceled by disturb(...,stop_search=TRUE) (cave.c:4185).

## Contradictions and surprises
- Brief assumed "j = skill - pval". Confirmed, with a floor of 2.
- Surprise: unlocking a trapped chest never "fails safe". chest_trap() fires on every successful open, trapped or not, discovered or not. Discovery only enables disarm.
- Surprise: search mode alone is useless when stationary. It only adds a search per step and costs -10 speed.
- Surprise: locked, trap-free chests (pval 6, 16, 26, 36, 46) cannot be "found" or disarmed.
- Surprise: the Pilot must search while adjacent. Standing on a square next to the chest is enough, and the player's own square is included.
- The ruined chest is in the table at A:75/1 and is not reachable at the mission depths.
