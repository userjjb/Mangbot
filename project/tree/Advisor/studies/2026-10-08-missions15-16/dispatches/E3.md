# Dispatch: E3 damage per round with 4 blows (missions 14-16)
- Brief: measure Dive04's HP/hit and per-round damage with the Main Gauche from game messages (missions 14-16), split by weapon/blows/STR; check blows against the formula.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-08; sources: runs/pilot/dive04 decisions.jsonl + events.jsonl (epoch alignment by G4_load.py; 35 starts = 35 segments, matched), server xtra1.c/cmd1.c/tables.c, object.txt, monster.txt, pilot.py, G4.md.

## Answer
Measured with the same method as G4: Main Gauche, 4 blows, STR 18/50 gives **hit rate 0.88-0.89, 5.6 HP/hit at (+0,+1) and 7.2 HP/hit at (+0,+2), i.e. about 20 per 4-swing round at +1 and about 26 at +2** (Dive03's Main Gauche: 7.0/hit, ~23). The memo's 19.5 is low only if you count the weapon's plus: server damage per hit is dice + weapon to-dam + the STR to-dam bonus (+3 at 18/30-18/69), so the real expectation is 7 (+1) and 8 (+2) per hit. The measured numbers sit 10-20% under that, probably from overkill (see below). The blows field matched the server formula at every STR seen (18 -> 2, 18/30 -> 3, 18/50 -> 4).

## Contradictions and surprises
- The memo's "19.5 per turn" understates the damage by omitting weapon pluses; actual expectation ~22-25 for +1 and ~26-28 for +2. Measurement lands at 19.7 (+1) and 25.7 (+2) because of the estimator's downward bias.
- Hit rate 0.88-0.89 vs the memo's 0.80 assumption. Source of the 0.88: 5% auto-hit plus low AC of monsters at 250-550 ft (not checked per monster).
- Blows prediction was perfect; no surprise there.

## Leads
- Overkill bias could be removed by using a first-hit-to-death damage log if the server ever sends damage; the client does not (cmd1.c only prints damage to DMs, line 1472).
- Backstab (cmd1.c:1467-1475) multiplies damage x3 on sleeping/fleeing monsters; I did not check which classes can use it, nor how many kills were sleeping targets (would inflate HP/hit).
- A cleaner HP/hit needs killing the same species under each weapon; M16 had most kills.
