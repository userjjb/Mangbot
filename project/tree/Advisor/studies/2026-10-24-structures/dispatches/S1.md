# Dispatch: S1 - what dungeon structures look like from a partial map (generate.c)
- Brief: room types 1-8 (footprint, walls, doors, light, monsters), tunnel width, frequencies at Depth 10-20, dump glyphs, signatures.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-24; sources: github/src/server/generate.c (read 225-3390 in full), options.h, mdefines.h, cave.c scatter, monster2.c place_monster*, lib/edit/terrain.txt + vault.txt headers, tools/pilot/{structures,world,glyphs}.py, pilot.py save_level_map, all 385 dumps in runs/pilot/dive04/levels. Scripts and outputs: scratch/S1/ (sim2.py, ratios.py, vmon.py, dumps.py + *_out.txt).

## Answer
Every room type 1-6 is centred on a lattice (Y = 11*by+5; X = 16+33k when dungeon_align is on, the default), so a 9x23 "double room" can only be at 6x6 spots. Types 4, 5 and 6 are the same 11x25 shape (outer wall, 1-wide ring, inner wall, 5x19 interior); only light, contents and door layout differ. Tunnels are 2 wide only when the neighbouring square is plain granite, so 1-wide corridor runs come mostly from rings, and tunnels hugging room walls. At 650 ft a level has about 0.39 type-4 rooms and 0.04 pits/nests, so a dark double room is a pit/nest about 17% of the time and a lit one never is.

1. Lattice. room_build centres block rooms at y=((y1+y2+1)*11)/2, x likewise (generate.c:3082-3083). With dungeon_align (default TRUE, generate.c:4128; the option is the player's, generate.c:4133) bx is forced to 1 mod 3 (3197-3200). So type 1-6 centres: Y in {5,16,27,38,49,60}, X in {16,49,82,115,148,181}. Types 7: Y=11*by+11 (by 0..4), X as above. Type 8: Y=11*by+11, X in {55,88,121,154} [INFERRED from the block arithmetic and room[] table, 259-270]. [SEEN] code. Check: all 7 structures the Pilot flagged in dive04 sit at X = 16 mod 33 and Y = 5 mod 11 (scratch/S1/dumps_out.txt). structures.py scans every 11th X (CENTRE_X, structures.py:28); only 6 X values are needed, which removes false positives. Without align the sim gives ~20 rooms/level, not 27.
2. Type 4/5/6 geometry [SEEN] (place_double_room, generate.c:980-985, place_floor 961). Outer wall FEAT_WALL_OUTER on rows Y+-5 and columns X+-12. Ring = floor, 1 wide, at |dy|=4 (23 squares) and |dx|=11 (7 squares between). Inner wall FEAT_WALL_INNER on |dy|=3 (|dx|<=10) and |dx|=10. Interior 5 rows x 19 (Y+-2, X+-9). The whole 11x25 block is flagged CAVE_ROOM, glowing if lit. The ring is the longest 1-wide straight run: 21 squares between walls (corner squares touch the side ring). Ring squares are CAVE_ROOM so rubble cannot be there (alloc_object ALLOC_SET_CORR skips rooms, 702).
3. Nest/pit specifics [SEEN]. Light: place_double_room(Depth, FALSE...) so always dark (1871, 2040). One secret door at the middle of a random side of the inner wall (1880-1885, 2049-2055). Type picks use tmp=randint1(Depth): pit orc if tmp<20, troll <40, giant <60, dragon <80, else demon (2059-2191); nest jelly if tmp<30, animal <50, else undead (1890-1920). So at Depth 10-19 every pit is orcs and every nest is jellies; at Depth 20 a pit is troll 1 time in 20. Monsters come from get_mon_num(Depth+10) (1930, 2201), placed awake (slp=FALSE; confirmed monster2.c:1975-1981: csleep is set only when slp is true), no groups (place_monster_aux(..., FALSE, FALSE)). The interior is completely filled: nest 5x19=95 squares each a random pick of 64 races (1967-1976); pit 95 squares in bands, lowest-level species on the top/bottom rows and outer columns, strongest at the centre (2277-2324). The nest/pit is dropped if get_mon_num fails, but the room stays and counts as crowded (2216, 3120).

8. Tunnels [SEEN]. WIDE_CORRIDORS is defined (options.h:232). In build_tunnel the second square (row1+col_dir, col1+row_dir) is dug only if its feature is FEAT_WALL_EXTRA (2792-2808), so: moving east it is the square below, west above, south the square to the east, north to the west. NOT dug when that square is any room/vault wall (OUTER, INNER, SOLID, PERM), existing floor/door, or when the step is through CAVE_ROOM squares or the "else" branch (existing corridor), 2766-2872. So corridors are 2 wide only in solid granite; they are 1 wide along the outside of a room wall, and along vault walls. Tunnels steer toward the next room's CENTRE and walk through CAVE_ROOM squares without digging (2766-2771), including inner walls, so inner walls of pits/type 4 are never opened. Room entry: only FEAT_WALL_OUTER is pierced, the pierced square and its neighbour along the wall become floor (2892-2905, so a 2-wide gap), 25% get a door (place_random_door: 30% open, 10% broken, 20% secret, 30% closed, 10% locked/stuck, 531-585), both halves get the same door (2915-2935). Secret doors thus hide ~5% of entrances. Streamers and junction doors are placed after the tunnels.

## Partial-map signatures (what each implies, what it cannot tell apart)
- 1-wide straight run of exactly 21, wall both sides, at Y+-4 (or Y+-3 spans 7 along X+-11): ring of a type 4/5/6 room; the room is 8 rows x 22 columns inside. CANNOT tell pit from nest from type 4. Aligned centre makes the centre computable. Runs of 5-14 can also be the ring seen only partly.
- A lit 11x25 block that appears all at once: type 4, never pit/nest. A dark one: 17% pit/nest, 83% type 4 (depth-independent in this range). At Depth 13 a pit is orcs, a nest jellies: look for 'o' or stationary jellies in a room whose door is 2 wide in the outer wall.
- A ring around a 5x19 wall box with one door spot at mid-side (hidden): types 4.1-4.4, 5, 6 alike; secret doors look like wall. Four doors off the middle, or a cross of inner wall seen through a door: 4.5.
- Checkerboard interior (diagonal floor): 4.4, ~7-8 monsters + traps + items. 3x3 wall block with a door in the middle of a 5x19 box: 4.2 (treasure + ~1 guard).
- Plus shape with 3-wide arms: type 3; a 3x3 box of INNER wall with no door at the crossing: 3 inner vault (~1 monster, traps).
- Pillar lattice (wall every 2nd square in rows and columns): type 1 pillar room (5% of type 1). Type 1 rooms are the vast majority (25 of 27).
- Wide stepped rectangle union: type 2. Large template (up to 33x22 or 66x44), often with '%' walls on the outside and perfectly straight inner walls: vault.
- 2-wide corridors are the default; a 1-wide corridor beside a room wall is just a tunnel hugging the wall, not a structure. A 1-wide run that ends at a door in a room's outer wall with a wall box behind it is the ring.
- Cannot be told apart from the partial map: granite vs inner vs perm walls; secret doors vs walls; magma vs quartz without colour; pits from nests before seeing monsters; a type 4 with items from one with monsters.

## Contradictions and surprises
- vault_monsters has no break (generate.c:1084-1098): sub-variants 4.1, 4.4, 4.5 hold roughly 6, 7-8 and 21 monsters, not 1-4. [SEEN code; counts INFERRED]. This is not what the comments imply.
- Brief said "pit/nest: one secret door at the middle of one of its 4 walls": true for pit/nest and type 4.1-4.4, but 4.5 has four doors, off-centre.
- The brief's "known: 24 of 385 levels with 1-wide run >=5" was reproduced exactly; its likely main source is type-4 rings, which are ~10x as common as pits.
- Nests at Depth < 30 are jelly only, so "nest" is the wrong risk label for a warrior at 500-650 ft: jellies never move (Pilot code classify() already checks NEVER_MOVE).
