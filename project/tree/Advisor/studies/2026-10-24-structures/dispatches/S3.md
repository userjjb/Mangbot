- Brief "already known" says recognised structures = 7 dumps. All 7 are among the 24; recogniser recall on 1-wide-run dumps is 7/9.
- Nar was not a pit/nest/vault: a summon trap (strong surprise vs the m26 narrative of "inner room with monsters").

## Leads
