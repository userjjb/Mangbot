# Dispatch: S2 vault templates, pit/nest monster mixes, and the m26 mix
- Brief: vault templates (families, monsters, treasure), pit/nest hooks and contents at Depth 10-20, monster-mix signatures, awake/asleep, and what explains m26 (Nar + Orc shaman + Giant salamander + Wolf + Purple mushroom patch).
- Clerk run: 2026-10-24; sources: generate.c (1060-1110, 1655-2570, 3040-3250), monster2.c (get_mon_num, place_monster_*, summon_specific), init1.c/init2.c (vault parse, alloc table), cave.c scatter, melee2.c (doors), vault.txt (all 149 entries), monster.txt (Nar, Orc shaman), monsters.csv, and the m26 events.jsonl around t=423-428.
- Files: scratch/S2/vaults.csv (149 rows), pit_nest_races.csv (708 rows), ood_by_source.csv, scripts vaults.py, pits.py, ood.py.

## Answer
1. **The m26 mix is not a pit, nest or vault. It is a summon-monster trap.** At t=427.339 the Pilot got "You found a trap! You are enveloped in a cloud of smoke!" and the monlist at that same instant listed Giant salamander, Purple mushroom patch, Orc shaman and Nar. That trap summons 2+1d3 monsters with no race filter, level cap (Depth+Depth)/2+5 = 18 at Depth 13, awake, unique-capable. No pit or nest hook can produce the mix (Nar is a unique; 'h', 'R' and 'C' are in no hook list).
2. At Depth 10-20, build_type6 gives almost only **orc pits** (a troll pit appears only at Depth 20, and then in 1 draw of 20), and build_type5 gives only **jelly nests** (animal needs tmp>=30, undead tmp>=50, so Depth>=31 or 51 at best).
3. Vaults have no depth filter, only a Depth>=10 gate for greater ones. Seen from outside they have a '%' wall rim, no fixed entrance, long 1-wide corridors, and either a few sleeping monsters (lesser) or dozens (greater).
4. Pit and nest monsters are placed awake. Vault monsters and the type 3/4 inner-room monsters are placed asleep (subject to the race's own sleep value). "All awake on arrival" is therefore a real signature of a pit, a nest, or a summon, but not of a vault.

- Source comments say nests/pits start at fixed depths 5/30/50 and 5/20/40/60/80, but the code uses room[].level 5 for type 5 and 6 and randint1(Depth) thresholds. At D10-20 the practical result is orc pit and jelly nest only.
