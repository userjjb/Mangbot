# Dispatch: Caution, escape and defence (borg-caution.c, borg-escape.c, borg-fight-defend.c)
- Brief: describe the Borg's caution decision order, escape tiers, healing logic, resting safety
  checks, and defensive buffs, with exact thresholds and file:line citations.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-28; sources covered: borg-caution.c (2035 lines, full), borg-escape.c (1287
  lines, full), borg-fight-defend.c (3616 lines, full — all 33 `borg_defend_aux_*` functions plus
  the `borg_defend()` dispatcher), borg-caution.h, borg-escape.h, borg-fight-defend.h (full). Also
  peeked (not fully, to interpret calls made from the assigned files) at `borg_check_rest()` in
  borg-flow-misc.c:1213-1362 and the "Just Rest" section of `borg_recover()` in
  borg-recover.c:300-440, and grepped `borg.avoidance =` assignments in borg-update.c/
  borg-think-dungeon.c.

## Answer
`borg_caution()` is NOT a single ordered decision tree with one danger check; it interleaves
several distinct sub-systems, called in this fixed order each game turn: (1) nasty-condition and
"too many escapes" checks that set `goal.fleeing`/`goal.leaving` flags, (2) heal-then-defend (or
defend-then-heal for Mages), (3) recall-wait resting, (4) danger-threshold flee/leave flag-setting,
(5) take stairs if flagged, (6) light/food emergencies, (7) "desperate for stairs" foot-race to
known stairs, (8) retreat to a remembered "happy grid" up to 10 steps away, (9) `borg_escape()`
(phase/teleport/recall/stairs, 8 internal danger tiers), (10) back away one square to an adjacent
safer grid, (11) status cures (confusion/fear), (12) low-HP flee flag setting, (13) reserve
EZ-heal potions, (14) recall if fleeing, (15) last-ditch unknown-item use. Healing itself
(`borg_heal()`) is a separate ~600-line function called from step 2, gated by `danger < CURHP +
heal_amount` at each tier so it never "heals into" a lethal hit. Defence (`borg_defend()`) is not
ordered logic either: it evaluates all 33 candidate buffs/attacks by "danger before minus danger
after" and does whichever scores highest, similar to the attack-selection pattern elsewhere in the
Borg. Resting itself is not decided in these three files at all — it lives in `borg_recover()`
(borg-recover.c) and is gated by `borg_check_rest()` (borg-flow-misc.c), which checks regional
fear, monster proximity/LOS/ranged-attack, breeders, and a `borg.no_rest_prep` cooldown timer that
buffs (bless, speed, hero, resists, etc.) set in borg-fight-defend.c to stop resting right after
casting a prep spell.

## Findings
1. **`borg_caution()` top-of-function order** is documented in a comment block and matches the
   code: nasty-situation detection → danger evaluation → heal/defend → stair-flee → critical needs
   (light/food) → foot-flee to stairs → retreat to happy grid → back away → cures → emergency
   heals → recall. [SEEN] (borg-caution.c:670-798 comment, :799-2034 body)
2. **Heal vs. defend ordering is class-dependent**: non-blind/confused/cut/poisoned Mages defend
   first then heal; everyone else heals first then defends. [SEEN] (borg-caution.c:1001-1023)
3. **"Too many escapes" cap**: if `borg.escapes > 3` (and not fighting a unique/Morgoth) or
   `borg.escapes > 55` (even fighting a unique), the Borg sets `goal.leaving` (depth ≤ 98) and, if
   `escapes > 3`, also `goal.fleeing`. Escapes counter increments once per successful
   `borg_escape()` call in `borg_caution()` (borg-caution.c:1654-1663), but several escape tiers
   decrement it right back with `borg.escapes--` when the "escape" was only a Phase Door, on the
   reasoning that "a phase isn't really an escape" (e.g. borg-escape.c:806, 912, 952, 1020, 1081,
   1162, 1228). [SEEN] (borg-caution.c:859-883)
4. **Flee/leave danger thresholds** (flags only; actual stair-taking is separate): "excessive
   danger" sets `goal.fleeing` when `pos_danger > CURHP*2` (and CLEVEL<50, not fighting a unique,
   depth<100) (borg-caution.c:1087-1099); in town, `pos_danger > CURHP` sets `goal.leaving`
   (borg-caution.c:1101-1111); low-HP flee is `CURHP < MAXHP/3` OR (`CURHP < MAXHP/2` AND `CURHP <
   CLEVEL*3`), each with only 25% chance per turn and only if `<3` Cure Critical potions and `<1`
   Heal potions on hand (borg-caution.c:1895-1918); bleeding/poisoned with `CURHP < MAXHP/2` is the
   same 25%-chance flee (borg-caution.c:1920-1943). [SEEN]
5. **"Desperate for stairs"**: if fleeing/scary-guy/danger-vs-level and stairs are tracked, the
   Borg will run for known up- or down-stairs if within `b_j < 3` steps and current danger is
   survivable (`pos_danger < CURHP`), or unconditionally if `b_j <= 2` steps
   (borg-caution.c:1284-1419). A separate check refuses to flee from fast-moving adjacent monsters
   unless already low-level-safe (skips fleeing from Grip/Fang specifically, borg-caution.c:1389).
   [SEEN]
6. **Retreat-to-happy-grid** (borg-caution.c:1421-1649) triggers at `danger > avoidance/3` (or
   surrounded with any danger), simulates walking up to 10 grids toward a remembered "happy" tile
   (`borg_happy_grid_bold`), requires the first step's danger `< CURHP*4/10` (normal) or up to
   `CURHP*15/10`–`CURHP*4` if surrounded, and each subsequent grid's danger must stay `<
   pos_danger`. [SEEN]
7. **Back-away** (one square) (borg-caution.c:1666-1848) triggers at the lower threshold `danger >
   avoidance*4/10` (or surrounded), scans the 8 adjacent grids, rejects any grid `> avoidance`,
   requires ≥40% danger reduction if `MAXCLEVEL >= 35` (`k > b_k*6/10` skip) or ≥20% reduction
   otherwise, and breaks ties using `borg_freedom()` (a bonus for proximity to known stairs,
   borg-escape.c:287-319) rather than danger alone. [SEEN]
8. **`borg_escape()` 8-tier danger ladder** (borg-escape.c:645-1286), each tier's trigger is a
   fraction of `borg.avoidance` (a "risky" config flag adds 3 to each numerator,
   borg-escape.c:718-719):
   - Tier 1 "really scary": `b_q > avoidance*4.5` generic (higher for uniques/Morgoth fights, as
     low as `*1.5` if fighting a non-unique wandering monster and `*1.7` for weak uniques,
     borg-escape.c:723-738) → try dimension door/teleport self/portal/shadow shift/scroll/staff/
     activate/teleport-level in that order, twice (low then high fail-rate allowed), then phase
     door as last resort (with `borg_caution_phase(75,2)` landing-zone check).
   - Tier 2: `b_q > avoidance*1.1-1.3` → teleport methods only, no fallback flee-flag.
   - Tier 3: `b_q > avoidance*1.0-1.3` (or `ISHEAVYSTUN`) → phase then teleport; if both fail, sets
     `goal.fleeing`/`goal.leaving`.
   - Tier 4: `b_q > avoidance*0.6-0.9` (weighted by CLEVEL<35 or CURHP<⅓) → same pattern.
   - Tier 5: CLEVEL<10 and `b_q > avoidance*0.5-0.7`.
   - Tier 6: spellcaster class, low mana (`CURSP <= MAXSP/10`, `MAXSP>=100`), `b_q >
     avoidance*0.6-0.8`.
   - Tier 7 "Shoot N Scoot": has Phase/Portal spell and `borg_shoot_scoot_safe()` says OK — lets a
     caster hit-and-retreat.
   - Tier 8: `borg.times_twitch > 50` (a stuck/looping counter) forces a phase/teleport regardless
     of danger. [SEEN]
9. **Landing-zone safety check** (`borg_caution_phase`/`borg_caution_teleport`,
   borg-escape.c:324-514): Monte-Carlo — samples up to 100 random destination squares at the
   spell's range, evaluates `borg_danger()` at each, and counts how many are "too scary"
   (`p > CURHP`); if the count exceeds an `emergency` parameter (20-75 depending on caller), the
   jump is vetoed as unsafe. Unknown/unexplored squares count as scary only if `MAXHP < 30`.
   [SEEN]
10. **`borg_recall()`** (borg-escape.c:55-128) also decides, when recall triggers, whether to
    answer "reset recall depth?" — resets to current depth unless deep (≥98) and not yet ready for
    Morgoth, or fighting toward specific uniques by depth-proximity logic. [SEEN]
11. **Healing tiers in `borg_heal()`** (borg-caution.c:60-667) scale the potion/spell chosen to
    `pct_down` (% of max HP missing) and always require `danger < CURHP + heal_amount`, where
    `heal_amount` scales with the wound size (`clw_heal = hp_down*15/100`, min 15;
    `csw_heal=20%,min25`; `ccw_heal=25%,min30`; `cmw_heal=30%,min50`; `heal_heal=35%,min300`,
    borg-caution.c:80-95), plus a "no rope-a-doping" guard (`clw_heal > danger/3`, etc.) that
    refuses a heal too small relative to incoming danger. Tiers: CLW at 30-40% down
    (borg-caution.c:353-362), CSW at 40-50% (:363-371), CCW at 50-55% (:373-380), 200hp "Heal" at
    ≥55% (:398-412), 300hp Healing spell at >50%/>60%/>65% (:425-484), and a final
    "healing final check" using EZ-heal potions when `pct_down > 75` and `danger > CURHP` and no
    teleport/escape items are held (:491-498). A random `chance` roll gates ordinary healing
    (:323-351) — e.g. at `pct_down<=15` only a 2% chance to bother healing at all, rising to ~99%
    at `pct_down>=75`.
12. **Confusion/blindness are handled twice**: once inside `borg_heal()` at the very top
    (borg-caution.c:144-230, with an 85% random gate on blindness) and again after the escape/
    retreat/back-away block if still confused (borg-caution.c:1852-1869) — i.e. curing status is
    tried both before and after attempting to flee. Poison and cuts get "critical check" branches
    (`CURHP<2` or `<MAXHP/20`) that boost mana to max and force-cast Cure Poison/Herbal
    Curing/Holy Word/Healing even into negative mana (borg-caution.c:544-663). [SEEN]
13. **Fear cure** (borg-caution.c:1871-1890) is gated by a 70% random chance, or always for a
    Warrior out of missiles; uses Boldness/Heroism/Berserk potions, Berserk/Heroism/Holy Word
    spells, or `act_cure_paranoia`/`act_hero`/`act_shero` activations. No stun/paralysis-specific
    cure branch was found in these three files — paralysis is not mentioned at all in
    borg-caution.c/borg-escape.c/borg-fight-defend.c. **[Contradiction/gap — see below.]**
14. **`borg_defend()` is a value-maximizing dispatcher, not an ordered list** (borg-fight-defend.c:
    3551-3614): it calls all 33 `BD_*` candidates (`borg_calculate_defense_effectiveness`,
    :3444-3546) in `borg_simulate` mode, keeps the best positive score, then re-invokes only that
    one for real. Most "danger-reduction" buffs (speed, all 5 resists, PFE, shield, glyph, door
    creation, mass genocide, genocide, banishment, teleport-other) score `danger_before -
    danger_after` (with `+2` nudges for some spells) computed by literally flipping the relevant
    `borg.temp.*` flag and re-running `borg_danger()`; morale buffs (Bless, Hero, Berserk, Smite
    Evil, Rapid Regen) instead return small fixed scores (1 or 5) regardless of magnitude
    (borg-caution.c-style pattern repeated at borg-fight-defend.c:158-238, 1291-1513) — meaning a
    real defensive/damage-reduction action will always outrank a morale buff when both are legal.
    [SEEN]
15. **A resistance refresh check precedes the simulate loop**: if `borg.resistance` (Resistance
    spell timer) is about to expire (`< borg_game_ratio*2`) and true danger (computed with
    `borg_attacking=true` to avoid counting fear noise) exceeds the regional-fear baseline or a
    unique is near, the Borg immediately re-casts Resistance before considering anything else.
    [SEEN] (borg-fight-defend.c:3559-3582)
16. **Every defend action that consumes a charge/spell sets `borg.no_rest_prep`** to a cooldown (in
    "borg time" units — values seen: 2000 for Stoneskin, 10000-13000 for Bless/Grim
    Purpose/Hero/Regen, 11000-13000, 16000-21000 for resists/PFE/speed, 18000 for
    detect-invis-potion) so the Borg won't immediately burn the buff by resting for mana right
    after casting it (examples: borg-fight-defend.c:225, 371, 448, 533, 716, 800, 884, 957, 1045,
    1098, 2724, 2730). This flag is consumed only in `borg_check_rest()`
    (borg-flow-misc.c:1262-1265), outside the three assigned files. [SEEN, cross-file]
17. **Resting itself lives entirely outside the assigned files.** `borg_caution()` only calls rest
    once, for "waiting on recall" (`borg.goal.recalling && borg_check_rest(...)`, keypress
    `R500`, borg-caution.c:1034-1048). All other resting ("rest to full", "rest for mana", "rest to
    recharge a rod") is in `borg_recover()` (borg-recover.c, not in scope) guarded by
    `borg_check_rest()` (borg-flow-misc.c, not in scope), which refuses to rest if: a multiplier
    (breeder) was just killed (<4 turns ago); any monster is within 1 grid (unless it never moves);
    a multiplier is within 10 grids; any monster with LOS has a ranged attack; regional fear
    exceeds `CURHP/20` or per-monster danger exceeds `CURHP/10` (relaxed at depth 100); or the Borg
    is on lava without fire immunity. There is one "defensive rest" inside the assigned files,
    `borg_defend_aux_rest()` (BD_REST, borg-fight-defend.c:2976-3045): a single `,` (hold-position)
    keypress used only at depth 100 while waiting for Morgoth in a prepared position, gated on no
    monster with LOS/ranged-attack visible. This is not the Angband `R` rest command. [SEEN]
18. **`borg.avoidance` is usually just current HP.** A grep (not a full read) of assignments shows
    `borg.avoidance = borg.trait[BI_CURHP]` in the common case, but it is boosted to `CURHP*2` or
    even `MAXHP*4`/`30000` in specific "stuck"/no-path situations (borg-think-dungeon.c:2100-2186,
    borg-update.c:2077) — this matters because nearly every threshold in borg-escape.c and
    borg-fight-defend.c is expressed as a fraction of `borg.avoidance`, not a fixed HP number.
    [SEEN via grep, not full read — flagged as partial]
19. **"Grid scoring" for retreat uses `borg_freedom()`** (borg-escape.c:287-319), which is *not* a
    general corridor/adjacent-monster-count score — in these three files it only rewards proximity
    to the nearest tracked up/down stairs (`1000 - 2*distance`, extra `+2000-500*d` bonus if
    `d<4`). No corridor-width or adjacent-monster-count scoring function exists in
    borg-caution.c/borg-escape.c/borg-fight-defend.c; "fewer adjacent monsters" is only used as a
    *reject* filter (skip grids adjacent to a monster) in the retreat/back-away code
    (borg-caution.c:1580-1599, 1739-1765), not as a continuous score. **[Contradiction with the
    brief's Q6 framing — see below.]**

## Contradictions and surprises
- The brief's Q6 assumes grids are "scored" for corridor-ness/adjacent-monster-count. In fact only
  two numeric scores exist for grid choice: local `borg_danger()` (dominant) and `borg_freedom()`
  which is purely "distance to known stairs" (borg-escape.c:287-319). Corridors and monster
  adjacency are binary reject filters, not part of a weighted score, in these three files.
- No paralysis-cure or stun-cure logic was found in any of the three assigned files (only
  heavy-stun is referenced, as a trigger for extra escape urgency, e.g. borg-escape.c:663,723,844,
  895). If paralysis handling exists in the Borg at all, it is outside this assignment's scope
  (possibly implicit — a paralysed Borg cannot act, so there may be nothing to code).
- Resting safety (Q4) is *not* implemented in borg-caution.c at all despite being asked about
  there; it is a separate subsystem (borg-recover.c + borg-flow-misc.c). This Clerk read those two
  functions partially to avoid leaving Q4 unanswered, but they are outside the assigned scope, so
  treat Findings 16-17 as secondary/cross-file confirmation, not primary coverage.
- Escapes counter mechanics are subtler than "N escapes allowed": Phase Door successes decrement
  `borg.escapes` right back down in most tiers (borg-escape.c comments: "a phase isn't really an
  escape"), so the 3-escape cap (borg-caution.c:861) effectively counts only *teleports/recalls*,
  not phase doors, even though both are dispatched from the same `borg_escape()` function.
- Defence is a value-maximizing simulation identical in shape to the Borg's attack-selection
  pattern (`borg_simulate` global flag reused, same "call twice: once to score, once for real"
  idiom) — this is a a broader Borg design pattern worth flagging to the Advisor beyond this
  assignment.

## Transfer notes
- Tiered "how scared am I" thresholds as fractions of current HP (not fixed numbers) map cleanly
  onto our danger-table approach; the Borg's tiers (0.5-0.9x, 1.0-1.3x, 1.5-4.5x current HP) could
  seed our own `flee_hp`/danger-ratio bands. [INFERRED]
- The whole escape system assumes turns pause for the Borg to compute (common.md caveat): our
  Pilot cannot try 8 fallback escape methods in sequence like `borg_escape()` does — in real time
  every failed key sequence costs a monster's turn. We should instead pick ONE escape method
  up-front per danger tier rather than cascade through spell→scroll→staff→phase like the Borg
  does. [INFERRED]
- The Monte-Carlo landing-zone check (`borg_caution_phase`/`borg_caution_teleport`) is elegant but
  assumes an internal fully-known map/`borg_danger()` model; not portable without our own map+
  danger model, and even then may be too slow to run every "tick" in real time. [INFERRED]
- `borg.escapes` per-level cap (3 teleports/recalls before forced level-leave) is a simple,
  transferable idea: track escape count per level and force a stair-scum/leave once exceeded, to
  avoid infinite-loop death spirals. [INFERRED]
- The "no_rest_prep cooldown after buffing" idea (don't immediately rest away a buff you just
  paid for) is simple and portable if our Pilot ever uses consumable buffs. [INFERRED]
- Value-maximizing defence dispatch (score every option, take the best) is more robust than a fixed
  priority list but requires a fast danger/what-if evaluator; likely too heavy for our Pilot, whose
  Navigator instead should hand down simple static rules (e.g., "quaff CLW if HP<50%"). [INFERRED]
- `borg_freedom()`'s stairs-proximity bias is directly relevant to our stair-scum design: even the
  Borg biases retreat/back-away decisions toward known stairs when danger ties, which supports our
  practice of tracking stairs and preferring paths toward them under pressure. [INFERRED]
