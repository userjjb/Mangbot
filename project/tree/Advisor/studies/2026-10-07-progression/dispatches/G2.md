# Dispatch: G2 depth readiness by band
- Brief: per depth band 0-2000 ft, what a solo warrior needs (clvl, HP, AC, resists, consumables, speed), the top threats and neutralisers, source disagreements, and what is buyable at CHR 4.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-07; sources: Borg `borg-prepared.c`, `borg-trait.c`; memos borg, danger-table, forum, shops, identify; `danger_table.csv`; 1.5 `init2.c` store tables, `object.txt`. CSV at `scratch/G2_bands.csv`.

## Answer
The Borg (the only source with numbers) gates depth on clvl >= dungeon level (dl = ft/50) up to dl20, clvl >= dl+5 for dl21-38, clvl 40 from dl34. Its gear gates: See Invisible at dl10, Free Action at dl20, rFire + 2 basics at dl21, all four basics at dl26, rPois + rConf at dl40. It sets no AC gate, no speed gate below dl46 and no rBlind gate below dl55. Almost everything past Phase/CLW/CSW/CCW/WoR is **not buyable** in 1.5: Free Action, See Invisible, rElec/rFire/rPois/rConf/rBlind and Scrolls of Teleportation must be found. The shop-buyable long escape is the Staff of Teleportation. HP numbers below are inferred, not code.

## Findings
1. **Borg clvl rule** [SEEN]. `borg_prepared()` returns "Clevel < depth" when `MAXCLEVEL < depth` (clvl < 50), then applies `borg_prepared_aux` (`borg-prepared.c:559-568`). For warriors, `clvl < depth-4` only matters for dl10-19 (`:222-226`), which the clvl >= depth rule already subsumes. `clvl < depth+5` applies at dl21+ while clvl <= 38 (`:305-310`); "level 40" is required above dl33 (`:374-376`). The gate runs only if `BORG_USES_DYNAMIC_CALCS` is off; I did not find that option's default, so the dynamic path is unchecked.
2. **HP gates are tiny** [SEEN]. Warrior: 30 HP at dl2, 50 at dl3-4, 60 at dl5+, unless "plays risky" (`:90-175`). The only high HP gate is 500 at dl46+ (`:418-420`). Our HP column for dl10+ is **[INFERRED]** from ~13 HP per clvl (Dive04: 146 at clvl 11) times the Borg's clvl; it matches the forum "~400 HP at 1900-2000 ft" (forum memo l.134) and the danger memo's ~10 HP/clvl as a lower bound.
3. **Consumable gates** [SEEN]. At dl2+ (clvl < 30): 2 cures, 1 WoR (`:113-124`). Dl10-19: light radius 2, **2 of (Scroll of Teleportation + Staff of Teleportation), 3 CCW**, See Invisible/ESP (`:140-175`). Restock (what forces a town trip) is lower: dl6-9 1 Phase + 1 cure; dl10-19 2 cures + 2 teleport; dl20-35 4 CSW+CCW + 4 teleport (`:740-770`). Dl26-39: 6 teleport + 10 CSW+CCW (clvl < 30) (`:322-340`). CCW/CSW requirements vanish at clvl 30.
4. **Phase Door does not count as an "escape" for the Borg** [SEEN]. `BI_ATELEPORT` counts Teleportation scrolls (+1 per Teleport Level scroll); `BI_AESCAPE` counts Staffs of Teleportation (`borg-trait.c:2374-2397,2477-2479`); Phase is `BI_APHASE`, separate. **This contradicts the shops memo §3** ("for us, escapes = Phase Door + WoR"). Since 1.5 sells no Teleportation scroll (shops memo §1.2), the Borg's 2-escape gate at 500 ft is only met by buying a Staff of Teleportation (3,100+).
5. **Free Action / SI / resist gates** [SEEN]. SI or ESP at dl10 (`:171-173`); FA at dl20 (`:270-272`); rFire and 2 of acid/cold/elec at dl21 (`:279-286`); rCold, rElec, rAcid at dl26 (`:330-335`); rPois + rConf + STR/DEX/CON >= 16 at dl40 (`:386-400`); speed +5 only at dl46, +10 at dl61, rBlind at dl55 (`:410,451,469`). AC is never tested (grep of `borg-prepared.c`).
6. **Where buyable at CHR 4** [SEEN: `init2.c:1088-1330`; prices from shops memo]. Temple: CLW/CSW/CCW, Boldness, Heroism. Alchemist: Phase, WoR, Identify, Magic Mapping, Detect Invisible scroll, **Resist Heat / Resist Cold potions (temporary)**. Magic shop: Staff of Teleportation, Staff of Detect Invisible, Staff of Mapping, **Amulet of Resist Acid** (base cost 300, `object.txt:1389-1393`; ~x1.5 here, stock not guaranteed), Wand of Slow/Confuse/Sleep Monster. Armoury sells armour (AC is the one buyable "resist"). Black market: random items (x3 price), Healing potion. Not in any normal store: Free Action (ring, base 1500, level 20: `object.txt:1208-1213`), See Invisible (ring base 340: `:1296-1301`), rFire/rElec/rPois/rConf/rBlind, Scroll of Teleportation, Speed potions. Everything in that list must be found (or bought in the black market at ~x3, unlikely).
7. **Threats and neutralisers** [SEEN in danger_table.csv, danger 4, with the danger memo]: see CSV and table. Highest-leverage per band: 250-500 ft the uniques (Brodda 4xd12, Grishnakh, Lagduf; Wormtongue needs rCold and FA); 500-750 FA (Homunculus, Druid Hold, Orfax Slow) and rFire; 750-1000 rConf (Umber hulk gaze has no save), FA (Evil eye), rPois/rAcid (hounds); 1000-1250 FA, Azog; 1250-1500 rBlind + rConf + FA together for Mind flayer, rPois + FA for Basilisk; 1500+ element resists for dragon breath (100-133), rNether for wraiths/shadows/Dread. Danger-4 rows for 0-350 ft don't exist: the rated-3 list at 0-250 ft is Grip/Fang, Novice priest/mage/ranger/paladin, Kobold shaman, Clear worm mass, Bullroarer (SEEN, csv danger 3).
8. **Speed** [SEEN/INFERRED]. The Borg doesn't gate on speed until +5 at dl46. The forum (§1, Zal) wants Speed potions for warriors below 1000 ft, but Speed potions are not for sale in normal stores. Our character can't meet any speed gate before 2000 ft except with found potions. Most danger-4 monsters at 1150-1500 ft are +10 speed (Azog, Wolf chieftain, Mim, hydra, Draebor, Basilisk, Beorn, bats, Ogre chieftain): leave on sight is the only answer without speed.
9. **Level-feeling/vaults** [REPORTED in forum memo]: vaults hold monsters up to +40 levels at 250 ft+; leave.

## Contradictions and surprises
- **Phase Door vs "teleport-class" gates** (finding 4) contradicts the shops memo, and means at 500 ft our character can't pass the Borg's escape gate without a 3,100+ staff. The identify memo also says the staff fails 67-83% at clvl 10 (identify memo l.143), 33-40% at clvl 15, ~20% at 20, so the "buyable long escape" isn't usable until clvl ~20.
- **Borg memo §1.2 table** says "Free Action at dl20 (1000 ft)". Code confirms: gate applies when depth >= 20 (`if depth <= 19 return` at `:262`). OK. But "clvl >= dl+5 at dl21-38" is only a warrior rule; confirmed.
- **Borg vs forum on rPois/rConf**: Borg dl40 (2000 ft), forum 1900-2000 ft, danger memo says rPois by ~1000 ft (air hounds, Basilisk) and rConf by ~800 ft (Umber hulk). The danger memo is the earliest and code-checked on monster data; the Borg's later date reflects its higher HP. No contradiction in the facts, only in risk tolerance.
- **Borg vs forum on See Invisible**: Borg 500 ft; forum 1000 ft; borg memo already recommends 500 ft. No monster data check was done here for how many invisible monsters appear at 500-1000 ft (Clear worm mass 150 ft, Poltergeist, Ghosts - not in danger 4); [INFERRED] SI at 500 ft is a Borg-only rule backed by 4.2 monster lists.
- The Borg plays Vanilla 4.2, where Teleportation scrolls are sold; 1.5 doesn't sell them. So its escape gates shift cost onto a Staff for us.
- No AC or speed gate exists in either the Borg or the forum checkpoints: the brief's AC and speed columns can't be filled from sources; any AC target is our own inference.
