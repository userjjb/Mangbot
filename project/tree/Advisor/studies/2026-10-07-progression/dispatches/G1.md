# Dispatch: G1 character mechanics by level (Half-Orc Warrior)
- Brief: exp, HP, blows/to-hit/dam, saves/stealth/search, class lines, energy/speed, stat drain/restore; formulas + CSV tables.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-07; sources: github/src/server/{xtra1,xtra2,cmd1,dungeon,melee2,spells1,spells2,use-obj,tables,init1,birth}.c, lib/edit/{p_race,p_class,object}.txt. All paths below under github/src/server/ unless stated.

## Answer
1. XP to reach clvl N = floor(player_exp[N-2] * 110 / 100) (expfact = race 110 + class 0). Monster XP = mexp*mlevel/clvl (with fractions carried).
2. HP = player_hp[clvl-1] + adj_con_mhp[CON]*clvl/100. Hit die 19 (race 10 + warrior 9); each level adds randint1(19) (mean 10), fixed at birth.
3. Blows: P = adj_str_blow[STR]*5/max(30,weight); D = adj_dex_blow[DEX]; blows = min(6, blows_table[min(P,11)][min(D,11)]) + extra. Sabre (weight 50) gives P = adj_str_blow/10.
4. The "6-7 swings per monster round" is explained by MONSTER_RECOIL (default on): a monster that melees you pays energy twice, so it attacks every other normal-speed turn; you get 2 turns = 6 swings (+ ENERGY_BUILDUP bonus).
5. Level gain restores nothing. Only Restore potions/mushrooms, Life, Restoring etc. restore stats; stat-gain potions restore first, then raise.

- Energy scale: 1000 gain vs 45000-50000 threshold (x5 finer than vanilla's 10 vs 100); only ratios matter.
- Uniques killed twice give no XP and no drop (xtra2.c:2934) -- affects farming plans.
