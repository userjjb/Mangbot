# Dispatch: G3 buyable gear, enchanting, prices
- Brief: list weapons/armour/magic-shop/alchemist gear a CHR 4 Half-Orc Warrior can buy, enchant mechanics, and a weight/dice CSV.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-07; sources: init2.c:1066-1318 (store_table), store.c:170-260/268-365/960-1050, spells2.c:390-400 and 2485-2830, use-obj.c:165-190, 430-620, 850-890, object2.c:1130-1470 and 2290-2360, xtra1.c:2860-2930, cmd2.c:3585-3640, object.txt, shop_own.txt, cost_adj.txt, S1_stock.csv, price_table.py, shops memo.

## Answer
1. Every shop item with dice, weight, AC, base cost and CHR 4 price range is in `scratch/G3_gear.csv` (125 rows). Weight is in 1/10 lb.
2. Enchanting never breaks or curses an item. A success chance of `(1000 - table[plus])/1000` applies per scroll. Under +4 the scroll is 90-100% reliable and costs ~195 per attempt.
3. To-dam enchanting looks like the best gold-to-damage trade for a 2-blow Sabre: each +1 adds 1 damage per blow, whatever the dice. I infer this; I did not model blows.
4. Staff of Teleportation really costs ~4,000-4,900, not 3,100-3,400 (see Contradictions).

8. **Enchant formula** [SEEN] (spells2.c:398-402, 2505-2620). Each scroll makes one attempt (use-obj.c:857-870). Armour scroll is +1 AC. To-hit and to-dam are separate scrolls.
   - Success chance: if plus < 0, always; if > 15, never; else `randint1(1000) > table[plus]`, with the table 0, 10, 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 700, 950, 990, 992, 995, 997, 999, 1000.
   - Per attempt: +0 100%, +1 99%, +2 95%, +3 90%, +4 80%, +5 70%, +6 60%, +7 50%, +8 30%, +9 5%, +10 1%.
   - At 195 gold (A = 155%), expected cumulative cost from +0 is about: +1 195, +2 392, +3 597, +4 814, +5 1,058, +6 1,336, +7 1,661, +8 2,051. Beyond +8 it is not viable (+9 ~2,700, +10 ~6,600).
   - **A failure just wastes the scroll.** Nothing breaks or curses. A successful enchant on a cursed item to >= +0 has a 25% chance to remove the curse (spells2.c:2557).
   - Artifacts succeed only 50% of the time. Piles resist (ammo is 20x easier). The "discount 99" hack applies only to spell casting, not scrolls (spells2.c:15-23).
9. **Is enchanting the Sabre worth it?** [INFERRED] Each +1 to-dam adds 1 damage per blow, so the Sabre's 2 blows get +2 per +1. Going from 8 to ~16 per turn at +4 costs ~814 plus the 73-81 Sabre. That is far cheaper than a Katana (580-648, 3d4 = +3.5/blow, and heavier). To-hit is worth much less. Buy to-dam scrolls first, and only change weapon if the blow count survives (the other Clerk's formula). Ordering by Armour AC: +1 AC for ~195 is poor next to a 16-19 gold Hard Leather Cap. A store-bought enchanted item costs only ~(145-170) per plus, cheaper than a scroll (195), but is rare.

## Contradictions and surprises
- **Staff of Teleportation cost.** The shops memo says "3,100-3,400 + charges (seen 3,371-4,185)". The code gives (2000 + 100 x charges 6-9) x A, which is 4,030-4,930 at full price [SEEN]. The logged 4,185 is exactly 2700 x 155%. The logged 3,371 is 2900 x 75% x 155%, a 25%-off line [INFERRED]. So 3,100-3,400 is the figure without charges and is misleading for budgeting.
- "Black market x3" in the memo omits the owner mark-up. Real is x5.1-6.0 base.
- Potion descriptions in object.txt (CSW "1d6+19", CCW "1d6+24") disagree with the code: CSW is `randint0(5)+20` and CCW `randint0(5)+25` (use-obj.c:492-503). Code is authoritative. No contradiction of brief "already known" items otherwise (the `(value x A + 50)/100` formula reproduces).

## Leads
- Whether the Sabre's blows survive heavier weapons (weight in CSV) is for the blows Clerk. CSV weights are 1/10 lb.
- Shop stock is random per restock (every ~33 s), so any item here may be absent.
- Armour to_h penalties (Chain Mail -2) apply to a Warrior's melee hit chance. I did not check how they are applied (calc_bonuses).
- Heavy armour weight against the weight limit with drained STR 18 was not examined.
