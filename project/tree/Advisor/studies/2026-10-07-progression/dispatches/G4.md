## Answer
Dive04 climbed clvl 1 to 6 in ~28 min of play at 50-100 ft, 6 to 10 in ~35 min at 250 ft, and (after dying and losing 2 levels) 8 to 11 in ~75 dungeon-minutes at 100-450 ft. Its measured XP rate is 5-9 XP/min at 0-500 ft, against Dive03's 23/min at 250-500 ft, 41/min at 500-750 ft and ~155/min at 750-1000 ft (clvl 16-22, only 15 min there). Dive03 needed 880-1540 XP per level at clvl 19-21, and it got a level every ~30-60 dungeon-minutes at 450-700 ft. Time lost to town waiting, stair-scum travel and Pilot stalls was 25-70% of each mission.

## Contradictions and surprises
- Dive03 "found 3000 gold pieces worth of gold" in town at 09-26 03:07:02 (events message right after a `goto`): not a monster drop; excluded from the gold figures [SEEN, decisions/events; cause not found].
- Dive03 has no "Welcome to level 15" message (14 -> 16 in one record) and "Welcome to level 17" twice (09-26 15:37 and 15:45): the second is the server rollback (next_steps.md notes the crash 09-26 15:41).
- next_steps.md says mission 7 Dive03 death was at 750 ft; the depth indicator reads d15 = 750 ft [SEEN]. Depth values 33-113 and negatives in decisions are wilderness/wrap artefacts (the signed-byte gotcha); I put those in the "town/surface" band.
- Dive03's first death (09-25 23:16, clvl 18, 1000 ft) is nowhere in next_steps.md's mission list; the logs show no resurrection event. clvl 18 -> 15 is inferred from the next Welcome message [INFERRED].
- The Navigator's own `navigator.md` times are estimates and run ahead of the clock (nav says so at m4); I used the logs.

## Leads
- XP/min must be rescaled for clvl: kills pay mexp x level / clvl. Dive03's 41 XP/min at 500-750 ft was at clvl 16-22, so Dive04 at clvl 11-14 would earn more per kill there but faces 52-HP-avg fights.
- 750-1000 ft XP/min (155) is from 15 min with no Free Action and 35 escapes: about half the time was spent escaping. A planner should not treat it as sustainable.
- Dive04 now has 2 blows with a 1d7 weapon: the kill-time data above suggests a damage upgrade (the +to-dam Rapier effect) matters more than blow count; a G-assignment on enchant scrolls vs weapon purchase would use these numbers.
- `audit_check` records `exp` only from 10-07 08:5x onward; earlier exp has to be estimated.
