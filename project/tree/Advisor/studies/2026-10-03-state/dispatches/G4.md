## Answer
The Navigator sees a text report built in `Pilot.report()` (pilot.py:2438). It is accurate for HP, depth (in feet), gold, blows and clvl (since 72ec098), and for pack counts only when the pack line is fresh. Most turns use `wait --brief`, which drops Pack, Equipment and Recent messages entirely, so it plays from memory between `status` calls. About 40 of its journal beliefs were checked and about 30 were exactly right. The errors came from (a) stale or lagging pack lines, (b) the Pilot's own wrong output (level shown as max level, false "quaffed" notifications, `wearall` choosing a torch over a lantern), (c) state the report never shows (max stat, thefts, recall pending, exp, AC, max depth) and (d) the Navigator's own mental tallies. It also once believed a dead character was alive.

## Findings

### 1. The interface (Q1)
1. **Full report format** [SEEN] (pilot.py:2438-2491). Lines, in order:
   - `NAME the RACE CLASS, level L[ (max M)], HP cur/max, <Town | N ft>, gold G, blows B, speed S`
   - `STR:18/30 INT:5 ... CHR:4  | Fed[ | blind, confused, afraid, poisoned, cut, stun][ | resting]`
   - `Standing on: <glyph> [(arrived by >)] | goal: ...`
   - `Equipment: name; name; ...` and `Pack: a) name; b) name; ...`
   - `Map (you are @ at y,x; rows a-b):` plus up to 23 rows of 67 columns
   - `Monsters in view: Name xN (lvl L[, UNIQUE]) nearest D away; ...` or `none`
   - `Items seen: <server item list lines>` (`?` if none known)
   - `Item squares nearby (glyph at y,x, distance): ...`
   - `Known stairs: < at y,x (D away), ...` (the 4 nearest)
   - `Since last report: HH:MM:SS what: detail | ...`
   - `Recent messages: ...` (the last 12 within the last 30 s)
   - `Orders: k=v, ...`
2. **Real examples** (from Navigator transcripts; the run logs do not store report text, because "status" events are excluded by `bulky`, pilot.py:2571):
   - Dive04, 2026-10-03: `Dive04 the Half-Orc Warrior, level 8 (max 10), HP 113/115, 50 ft, gold 56, blows 3, speed 0` / `STR:18/20 INT:5 WIS:10 DEX:18/10 CON:18 CHR:4  | Fed` / `Standing on: . (arrived by >) | goal: dive 200ft` / `Monsters in view: none` / `Known stairs: < at 27,140 (11 away)`.
   - During the death fight (2026-09-29 22:29:03): `... level 10, HP 78/135, 250 ft, gold 90, blows 3, speed 0` / `| Hungry` / `Monsters in view: Small kobold x1 (lvl 1) nearest 8 away; Large kobold x3 (lvl 5) nearest 3 away; Mughash the Kobold Lord x1 (lvl 7, UNIQUE) nearest 1 away`.
   - Post-resurrect: `level 10, HP 115/115, Town, gold 100, blows 1` with `Equipment:` and `Pack:` empty, `Standing on: 4 (arrived by <)`.
3. **`--brief`** (pilotctl.py:40-48) keeps only the first two lines plus lines starting `Standing on:`, `Monsters in view:`, `Known stairs:`, `Since last report:`. A quiet `wait` timeout always uses it (pilotctl.py:78). Transcripts show about 85-90% of turns are `wait --brief`, and `status` is almost always piped through `grep -E "Pack|Recent|Equipment"`. Counts per transcript: dive04 mission 10 had 17 brief waits and 6 status calls; dive03 mission 4 had 72 and 28.
4. **Units and format** [SEEN]:
   - Depth is feet (`depth*50`), `Town` only when depth == 0. A negative depth (wilderness) prints as e.g. `-5600 ft`; dive03 decisions on 09-25 22:34-22:39 hold depths -1 to -112. [INFERRED] from pilot.py:2447 plus those logged depths.
   - `level` is the current clvl (`world.clvl` = ind level[1], world.py:275-281). `(max M)` appears when max differs.
   - Stats print only `stat_use` (current, drained). The server also sends top and max (xtra1.c:122) but the report drops them, so drained or not is invisible.
   - `speed` is the raw `pspeed-110` (xtra1.c:413), so `0` is normal and `10` is +10. `blows` is `skills2[0]`.
   - Hunger: server 0 (faint) and 1 (weak) both print `Weak`; 2 `Hungry`; 3 `Fed`; 4 `Full`; 5 `Gorged` (pilot.py:2450, xtra1.c:241-281).
   - `Known stairs ... (N away)` is Chebyshev distance on the remembered map, not path length (world.py:286).
   - Pack letters are `chr(97+item)`.
5. **Other pilotctl replies** (pilotctl.py:85-97, pilot.py:2311):
   - Item actions (`wear/quaff/read/eat/destroy/...`): up to 4 "said" messages plus `Pack now: a) ...; b) ...`. Messages starting `You enter a maze`, `Looks like`, `You feel`, `You hear` or `You have found` are filtered out (pilot.py:2309), so e.g. "You feel much better" never shows.
   - `inscribe`: `Pack now:` only.
   - `goal X`: raw JSON `{"ok": true, "goal": "..."}` (plus `NOTE:` if max_depth capped it).
   - `order`: the JSON orders dict. `monster NAME`: text. `pickup/stairs/wearall/stop`: `{"ok": true}`, with no state.
   - Errors are raw JSON `{"ok": false, "error": ...}`.
   - `wait`: `ATTENTION what: detail  (goal: ...)` lines, then the report (full if events, brief if `--brief` or a quiet timeout).
   - Shop goals return their stock list or purchase lines in the `goal_done` detail.
6. **Missing from every report** [SEEN, code]:
   - SP, exp and exp-to-next, AC, stat max/drained, and max depth reached (the recall target).
   - Recall pending: `w.recall_pending` exists (world.py:61) but the report never prints it.
   - Light turns appear only inside the equipment name, and only in the full report.
   - Paralysis (`state[0]`), haste/hero/bless/resist timers (the `oppose` indicator), and slow/fast beyond the raw speed number.
   - Free Action / See Invisible (the Navigator types `free_action=yes` itself).
   - Level feeling (only via messages), time or turn, party and other players, monster asleep/health, inventory weight (the client sends it, c-tool.c:536; the report drops it), and equipment slot names or letters (the Equipment line has no letters).
   - Floor item under you shows only as a glyph in `Standing on:`.
   - Store contents appear only in the `goal shop N list` result.
7. **Two side effects that hide information** [SEEN]:
   - `report()` clears `self.news` after printing (pilot.py:2485-2488). Every `status | grep ...` call therefore consumes the "Since last report" lines (`tactic`, `danger_avoided`, `started`) without the Navigator seeing them.
   - "Recent messages" is only the last 12 messages from the last 30 s (pilot.py:2489), so a theft or drain message from 40 s ago is gone.
8. **"Item squares nearby" lists town store `0`** (the Home entrance) as an item: `0 at 15,109 (11)` in the post-resurrect report. `0` is not in world.TERRAIN (world.py:18). [INFERRED] from that report plus world.py:18.
9. **`takeoff L` by letter does not match the report.** `item_index` returns `ord(letter)-97` (pilot.py:2211-2212) whatever `equip` says, but the report prints no equipment letters. Only name lookup reaches equipment. [SEEN code; not exercised in the logs.]

## Contradictions and surprises
- "Already known" says the Pilot and Navigator are often wrong about slot letters. In these two journals the Navigator was wrong about the pack twice (row 4, row 7); most other beliefs were right. The sharper problems are state it cannot see (max depth, recall pending, stat max) and the Pilot's own wrong outputs.
- The current-level bug is confirmed in the logs: the report said `level 18` at 16:14:17 on 09-26 while the first "Welcome to level 18" came at 16:21:04; `level 10` after the 09-29 resurrection while clvl was 8. In current code `world.clvl` uses level[1] (world.py:275), and the 2026-10-03 transcript shows the `(max 10)` form working.
- The `quaffed` false positives are fixed in code (pilot.py:947-952) but the death on 09-29 still has them in its logs (row 34).
- The dive03 journal's last two lines assume the character was alive. It had died 12 minutes earlier (row 25).
- Even the "fresh" `Pack now:` reply was stale after a stack merge (row 7). That reply is not a safe source of truth right after merging or inscribing.
