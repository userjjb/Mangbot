# Dispatch: F1 News, Bug Reports and Technical Support subforums
- Brief: dated timeline of releases and server events from News; every bug or quirk in Bug Reports and Tech Support that could affect a bot (checked against 1.5 code where cheap); live-server rules a bot must respect.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-29; sources covered: `data/forum/corpus/news.txt` (all 2446 lines, 43 topics), `bugs.txt` (6 topics), `techsupport.txt` (5 topics), V1/V2/V3 dispatches (grepped for dates and hashes), upstream git tags and a few commits, 1.5 source `github/src/server/party.c`, `melee2.c`, `github/mangband.cfg`.

## Answer
1. The News forum gives a clear release timeline. Every release date matches the upstream tag date, with two exceptions (1.1.3 and 1.1.4, below). The forum says 1.5.0 is where time bubbles arrive and that 1.5.x would not be reset. The V dispatches agree with both.
2. The archived bug and tech-support topics hold almost nothing that endangers a bot. Only one bug, "see through closed doors/swapped players" (t=1633), touches 1.5. It was fixed after 1.5.2, in v1.5.3. One live-server quirk is real: the party-slot table (256 slots) is still coded to reuse slots by age (`party.c:63-80`).
3. The live-server rules (thread t=1388) never mention bots or automation. They do ban several things a bot could do by default: giving away or under-selling items, holding or storage characters, power-levelling, AFK in shops, littering in town, and saving in the dungeon. Rules were enforced by hand, from logs. Punishment was character deletion or a ban.

### Rules a bot must respect (live mangband.org server, t=1388; "this applies only to the mangband.org server", p=5890)
8. **No mention of bots, automation or macros** in the rules. [SEEN; the whole thread was read. The word "bot" appears only for the IRC bot that relays chat.] The macro guide (t=1500) is about in-client key macros only. The IRC bot lets admins watch chat, levelling, deaths and unique kills; logs also exist (p=5916, Warrior). A bot that levels suspiciously fast would be visible in those logs.
9. **Numbered rules (p=5890):** (1) when the ghost dies you start over, with no old items kept; (2) don't give things away, sell them, and don't gift or under-price items between your own characters; (3) no power-levelling, meaning diving with a much higher or lower level character to give one a free ride; (4) don't help too much: up to 500 gold per character level above 25 to a dead character, with a per-level schedule; (5) no swearing or blasphemy; (6) don't litter in town, use `k` (destroy) instead of dropping junk; (7) don't go AFK in shops (only one player per shop); (8) don't save in the dungeon, especially above 1000 ft, because the level stays static; (9) do not use holding or storage characters, buy a house. Rule 5 in the original numbering (ghost hiding in a wall) is later called obsolete (p=5920).
10. **Ironman additions:** no selling or trading discounted items between characters via shops (2009-08-02, p=7430); no summoning monsters in Ironman towns (2012-05-21). Break the rules and you get "at best a warning and at worst you lose your character and are banned" (`news.txt:318`).
11. **Player-killing and stealing:** Warrior says PvP "was removed some years ago" and that killing other players is not allowed. He also says taking dropped loot is "within the rules" but leads to "wars". Persistent conflict may end with "all your characters removed" (p=9647, 2014-01-24). Warrior says "I can log into your character and retire him or just change the password." Punishment is by hand, on "gut feeling" and the "spirit of the game". The 1.5.3 shipped cfg has `PVP_HOSTILITY = 2` (both must be hostile) (`github/mangband.cfg:92`) [SEEN]. The live server's value is unknown.
12. **Idling and inactivity:** artifacts are taken from characters inactive over 60 days (automated since 2018-11-22, t=2199); houses after 12 months (t=1732). Inactivity is measured as "logged in", not "played", so scheduled logins would keep them. Schroeder's proposal to count real play time (p=8272) was never adopted in the archive. [SEEN]
13. **Power-levelling threshold:** unclear. Posters discuss 5, 10 and 15 levels (p=5896, 5905, 5908). Warrior confirms nobody's savefile is deleted automatically (p=5916). Party diving with a high-level character to get a low-level one XP is the case at issue.
14. **Saving in the dungeon:** after 2008 the game "auto-bounces" the level. Crimson wanted the timer near 0 (p=5917). The saved level stays static for a while (p=5890). A bot should recall to town before quitting.
15. **Multiple characters:** allowed, but interaction between them is treated as cheating ("both characters online at the same time" is disallowed) (p=5917, Crimson). A team of bot characters trading items with each other would break rule 2.

## Contradictions and surprises
- **News does not contradict the V dispatches on any release date.** Two announcements lag the tag: 1.1.3 (post 2016-05-27, tag 2016-04-05) and 1.1.4 (servers updated 2018-11-22, tag 2018-11-29).
- **Possible gap in V2:** the 1.1.4 news claims a change in how often hounds and Quylthulgs cast. V2 lists no such change. The closest commit is `1789855` (monster energy on no-action). See finding 5. Whether it applies to 1.5.3 is unresolved.
- **Temple Healing (via V3, not checked in the memo myself):** V3.md:56 quotes Addendum 3 as saying 1.1 removed Healing from the shops. News t=2200 (2018-11-25) shows the Temple gained Healing in 2018, which agrees with V2/V3.
- **Rules were mostly unenforced in code.** Crimson says "there is nothing but player honesty to enforce" the power-levelling rule (p=5917). Warrior lists enforcement by reading logs (p=5916).
- **"Server moved from America to Europe" (2007-12-05)** and lag changed for players on each side (t=1031). No later server move is stated in News.

## Leads
- Ask the user or read the Discord: which version and which rules does the live server run today? News ends in 2020.
- The 1.5.3 fixes for #1303 (visibility on player swap and teleport) are in v1.5.3 and later but not 1.5.0-1.5.2. If the bot's client ever connects to an older 1.5.x server, view updates for other players and doors could be stale.
- Party creation from a bot: check whether the Pilot ever calls create-party. If so, `party.c:66-80` limits it.
- The Changelog and What's New pages referenced in the news posts (1.1.0 to 1.5.3) are not in the corpus. They would give the authoritative change lists.
- Forum-thread t=1388 later pages (`start=15`, `start=30`) contain code proposals for discount averaging (p=9167-9173). No sign it shipped.
