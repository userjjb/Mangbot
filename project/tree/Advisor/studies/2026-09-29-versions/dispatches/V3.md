# Dispatch: V3 gameplay changes, 2019-01 to 2022-03 (v1.5.0 to v1.5.3 and later)
- Brief: list the gameplay changes in the 723-commit span (Jan 2019 to Mar 2022), say what a player of the previous version would notice, what 1.5 took from vanilla versus kept MAngband-specific, what came after v1.5.3, and which forum advice this makes outdated or confirms.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-29. Sources: all 723 subjects in `scratch/V3_commits.txt`; full messages and diffs of about 70 gameplay commits (`git show` in `data/versions/upstream`); tag-to-tag diffs of `lib/edit/*.txt` and `init2.c`; our server source `/projectnb/jbrcs/mangband/github/src/server`; `lib/help/version.txt`; forum memo §1-§4 and Addenda A-C.

## Answer
Most of the span is UI, build and port work (SDL2 client, mobile, installers, mouse, macros). About 40 commits change play. The important ones:
- Time bubbles were retuned before v1.5.0 (fixed 5x slowdown at low HP; no speed-up with an awake monster in view).
- Player noise and stealth checks were rewritten.
- The old "classic MAngband hacks" came back as birth options: `energy_buildup` and `monster_recoil`, both on by default.
- Partial-blow and shots-per-turn energy accounting arrived.
- Cure-potion healing was changed to flat values (v1.5.3 only).
- Store stock changed: the Temple's Healing potions came and went, and the Black Market and house items were added.
- Free Word of Recall at birth (not for ironman); ghost, death and starvation fixes.

**Surprise for the Advisor:** our "pristine 1.5.3" server is NOT the v1.5.3 tag. It equals upstream `develop` HEAD (2022-03-13). Post-1.5.3 gameplay changes ARE in our server (finding 3).

## Q3: forum advice made outdated or confirmed
- Memo §1.3 and P3: "CLW about 15, CSW about 18, CCW about 27". CLW 15 and CSW 20-24 and CCW 25-29 are the v1.5.3 values (finding 8). The forum's 18 and 27 match the OLD dice averages (4d8 and 6d8). The Erratum's table is right for our server; the forum numbers are for versions before 1.5.3.
- §1.3 and Addendum 3: "one turn goes to the auto-retaliator" and "auto-retaliate eats your next move". Since 2019-02-14 retaliation doesn't happen when commands are queued or when afraid (finding 7). Queue the escape or heal; a pending command suppresses retaliation. Refined further: retaliation spends leftover energy in a `while (energy >= blow_energy)` loop (`cmd1.c:1432`). [INFERRED for the exact energy outcome; the code paths were read.]
- Addendum A1 and P7: CONFIRMED and now more precise. The slowdown is a constant 5x below `hitpoint_warn`x10 % HP. `hitpoint_warn > 9` with nothing in view turns it off (`xtra2.c:5201`). Rest gives 10x only with no awake monster in LoS; running gives 5x with none in LoS (dungeon only).
- Addendum A7 and P7: CONFIRMED, and the 2019 change (finding 2) is why running no longer speeds hounds up.
- Addendum 3 "1.1 deliberately removed the BM's Healing... don't plan on Healing": true of 1.5.3 (Healing only rare in BM). Not true of v1.1.4 to v1.5.2, where the Temple sold Healing x3.
- Domino/Emulord early game ("scum at 50 ft until you can afford WoR"): a fresh character now begins with a free WoR (finding 10).
- Parser strings (Addendum 2 #12; death lines): the death broadcast format changed (finding 12).
- Addendum B pickup conflict: `,` = hold and always picks up; `g` = `do_cmd_pickup` (`client/c-cmd0.c:119,190`). Explains the conflict (finding 14).
- Addendum A8 (no food in town unless gorged): CONFIRMED and it dates from v1.5.3 (`7ceb668`).
- P8/P14 (idle and disconnect): starvation while idle >= 180 s kicks the character (`options.h:70`); before 2019-08 the disconnect happened by a different rule (`eb9217b`).
- §5 and Addendum 2 #2 (Death mold disenchant): disenchant now hits only weapon to-hit and to-dam, or armor to-ac (finding 18).
- The forum's monster lore (hound depths, uniques): monster data is identical to v1.1.4 (finding 1); the Erratum's hound depths stand.

## Contradictions and surprises
- **Contradiction of the brief's premise:** the brief calls the target "our 1.5.3 server" and asks what came after v1.5.3 that is not in it. Our source already contains the two post-tag changes (finding 3). The only tag-only differences are 1.5.3 to HEAD ones, and those are in our server.
- The 1.5 "rewrite" changed the network layer and UI; game data is unchanged from v1.1.4 (finding 1). The changes in this span restore old behavior (the many "classic MAnghack" commits), not new mechanics.
- `f65112d`'s help text says speed bonuses are for "Warriors"; the code gives `SPEED_BONUS` only to Rogue (`p_class.txt:310`, `xtra1.c:2376`). The help text is wrong.
- `lib/edit/object.txt` says CCW cures "1d6+24" while the code is `randint0(5)+25` (25-29): a small inconsistency.

## Leads
- Which forum posts describe pre-Jan 2019 time-bubble behavior (proportional slowdown; run/rest speed-ups with monsters in LoS)? Check t=1601 p=6808-6823 dates.
- `hitpoint_warn` default and pref line `H:` were not touched in this span; A1's recommendation stands.
- Randarts are compiled in (`mdefines.h:1067`), but the live server config is unknown.
- `energy_buildup` and `monster_recoil` are per-player birth options (default TRUE); the recoil code has an `XXX` note "make it server-wide". A character created before 2019-06 may not have them (`load2.c:1428` special-cases these options).
- The 1.4.0 tag date (2018-12-01) vs v1.1.4 (2018-11-29): V2 should say whether "1.4" is a separate line.
