# Dispatch: What messages the Pilot matches today
- Brief: catalogue every message pattern in the Pilot, score it against the 300 most frequent logged messages, and flag fragile patterns.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-29; sources: pilot.py, world.py, mover.py, glyphs.py, pilotctl.py (grep of all message-text uses plus reading of each site), log_messages.csv (top 300 of 1826 rows), server_messages.csv, server src (melee1.c, xtra2.c, cmd1.c, cmd2.c) for phrasing checks.
- Scratch: scratch/P2_match.py (patterns copied verbatim into Python), scratch/P2_frag.py, scratch/P2_out.txt, scratch/P2_patterns.csv (38 rows).

## Answer
Message text is inspected in only three files, world.py (`_on_event`, the central place), mover.py (rubble/bump) and pilot.py (pickup, store, recall confirmation, unseen-attacker cause). glyphs.py and pilotctl.py inspect no message text (they parse monster.txt, the monster list and the brief). 260 of the top-300 messages match at least one pattern, but that is 48.6% by count (7544 of 15516; 40.8% of all 18489 logged). About 4,730 of the matched count is just "You hit/miss/have slain..." (sets `fight_t`), so the count-weighted share of danger/status/escape signals is small. Most unmatched volume is harmless noise (level feelings, stair-arrival text, light-source text). The real gaps are visible-monster breath/spell attacks, status effects (confused, terrified, paralyzed) and kills of unseen monsters.

## Contradictions and surprises
- RE_ATTACK's comment-level intent ("... hits you style messages") does not cover ranged monster attacks (breath, arrows, spells) in the phrasing the server actually emits (finding 4a).
- No "dies"/"is destroyed" message appears in the log at all; the fight_t branch for them is unexercised.
- The 40.8-48.6% "matched" figure overstates coverage because "You hit/miss" is 30% of all messages.
