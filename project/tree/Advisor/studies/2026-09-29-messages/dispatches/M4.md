## Answer
These files hold mostly command outcomes: trap effects, movement blocked, door/tunnel/stairs results, pickup/wield/drop confirmation, and refusals ("this command did nothing"). 62 rows are high relevance. Combat text here is only the player's own melee/missile results plus PvP messages. Monster attacks are not here (other Clerks). Key finding for the Pilot: trap and chest messages that share one text but mean different things (four "A small dart hits you!" traps, two "A small needle has pricked you!" chests), and the summon trap text that killed Dive03.

## Findings
1. **Summon trap / summon chest**: "You are enveloped in a cloud of smoke!" summons 2+1d3 monsters (cmd1.c:1049 trap; cmd2.c:423 chest, identical text). This is the Dive03 death-2 trigger. Event `summon`. [SEEN]
2. **Stairs**. Success: "You enter a maze of up staircases." (cmd2.c:82) / "You enter a maze of down staircases." (cmd2.c:186). Refusals (nothing happens): "I see no up staircase here." (:39) / "I see no down staircase here." (:130); "There is nothing above you." / "below you." (ghost or wilderness); "Morgoth awaits you in the darkness below." (ironman, going up, :58); "An unvanquished adversary pulls you back, you can descend no further." (quest level, not ironman, :148); "You are at the bottom of the dungeon." (depth>=127). Ghost variants "You float upwards/downwards." [SEEN]
3. **Trap door**: "You fell through a trap door!" (cmd1.c:924) = level change (depth+1, 2d8 damage unless feather fall, which adds "You float gently down to the next level."). In wilderness or on a quest level the text is instead "You feel quite certain something really awfull just happened.." (typo in source) and nothing happens (:920). [SEEN]
4. **Dart trap ambiguity**: "A small dart hits you!" is sent by four different traps (cmd1.c:1090/1106/1122/1138): slow, STR drain, DEX drain, CON drain. Same for "A small dart barely misses you." (four rows, all harmless). Only the follow-up message (other files) tells which. Likewise chest "A small needle has pricked you!" = STR drain (cmd2.c:386) or CON drain (:394). [SEEN]
5. **Gas traps announce even when resisted**: "A black gas surrounds you!" (blind unless resist_blind), "A gas of scintillating colors surrounds you!" (confusion unless resist_conf), "A pungent green gas surrounds you!" (poison unless resist/oppose), "A strange white mist surrounds you!" (paralysis 5-14 turns unless free_act). The text does NOT prove the status began; wait for the status message. Chest analogues: "A puff of green gas surrounds you!" (poison), "A puff of yellow gas surrounds you!" (paralysis 11-30) (cmd2.c:402,412). [SEEN cmd1.c:1152-1182]
6. **Other trap effects**: "You hit a teleport trap!" (teleport 100 squares), "You are enveloped in flames!" (4d6 fire), "You are splashed with acid!" (4d6 acid), "You fell into a pit!", "You fall into a spiked pit!" (same text for plain-spiked and poison-spiked pits, rows 25 and 29), "You are impaled!" / "You are impaled on poisonous spikes!" (cut/poison), "The poison does not affect you!" (resist). "There is a sudden explosion!" + "Everything inside the chest is destroyed!" (5d8). [SEEN]
7. **Walking into a trap you hadn't seen**: "You found a trap!" (cmd1.c:2061) then the trap fires immediately. "You have found a trap." (search, :546) means a trap became visible but did not fire. [SEEN]
8. **Move blocked** (18 rows). Known terrain: "There is a wall / a closed door / rubble / a tree blocking your way." Unknown terrain (dark or blind): "You feel a wall / a closed door / some rubble / a tree blocking your way." (cmd1.c:1883-1946). Town edge: "There is a wall blocking your way." (cfg_town_wall) or one of five ironman flavour lines (:1645-1649). Same text "There is a wall blocking your way." serves wall and secret door and the town-edge wall. - Format-column quirk: rows 12, 216, 218, 254 show run-together literals ("piece%s...s", "ana"); the real texts are singular/plural and a/an choices, handled in regexes.
- msg_print_aux (util.c:1853-1912): an identical message of the same type repeated back to back is sent as PKT_MESSAGE_REPEAT, not text; the client re-emits its last message (client/net-client.c:1807-1820).
