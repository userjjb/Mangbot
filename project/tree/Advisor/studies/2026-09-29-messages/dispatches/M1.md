## Contradictions and surprises
- Surprise: "blind" spell texts are keyed to the player being blind, not to visibility (finding 6a). A Pilot that treats "It ..." + normal text as "blind" would be wrong, and one that waits for "mumbles" to detect an unseen caster would miss most cases.
- Surprise: several disabling spells print the normal, named text even when blind (finding 6): "It commands you to return."
- Surprise: the message for a resisted vs landed attack often differs only in a follow-up line: "You resist the effects!" and "You stand your ground!" are shared between resist_X and saving-throw success (cannot tell which).
- Text typos to match exactly: "casts a acid bolt.", "You hear door burst open!", "1 coins were stolen!" (no singular form), "mumbles something about mushrooms." (a MOAN blow, not the spell "mumbles.").
- Blows carry no damage text; use HP change.

## Ambiguities (same text, different meaning)
- "<M> blinks away." = spell BLINK (`melee2.c:1578`) or level-1 paralyzer after successful paralysis (`melee1.c:1354`).
- "<M> recovers <his> courage." = timer expiry (`melee2.c:2674`) or heal spell (`melee2.c:1562`).
- "Your purse feels lighter." appears in two branches; the next line ("<n> coins..." vs "All of your coins...") separates them.
- "<M> crushes you." (blow) vs "<M> crushes <item>." (others' view of monster destroying an item): regexes overlap unless "you" is checked.
- "<M> mumbles." is the blind text for ~40 spells (attack, buff, summon).
- "You feel your life slipping away!" / "draining away!" / "keep hold..." repeat identically for EXP_10..80.
- "<M> makes a strange noise." (blind arrow, four kinds) vs "<M> breathes." (blind, 19 kinds).
- "You feel something focusing on your mind." = mind blast or brain smash.
- "The X starts moving faster." can be +10 or +2 speed steps (same text).
