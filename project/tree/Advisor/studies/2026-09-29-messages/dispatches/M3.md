# Dispatch: M3 message catalogue (cmd5.c, cmd6.c, store.c, use-obj.c, x-spell.c, object2.c)
- Brief: catalogue all 311 message calls in M3_input.csv, plus an escape and cure table (success and failure texts).
- Clerk run: 2026-09-29. Sources: `scratch/M3_input.csv` and the server source it cites (all six files at the cited lines). To fill the table I also read the effect functions those files call in spells1.c, spells2.c, xtra2.c, dungeon.c, util.c and object1.c, plus lib/edit/object.txt.
- Output: `scratch/M3_catalogue.csv` has 376 rows and every regex compiles. It has one row per distinct text, so multi-text calls give several rows. Rows X01-X26 are effect messages from files outside M3 that the table needs.

## Answer
The M3 files hold 311 calls. 15 of them are compiled out (see finding 6). Six are `#define` macro lines and not messages. The rest are 290 live message texts.

**The most important result for the Pilot: Phase Door, Teleportation and the staff of Teleportation send the reader no message at all when they succeed.** `teleport_player()` (spells1.c:167-283) prints nothing to the user. The only text is `<Name> blinks away!` or `<Name> teleports away!`, and that goes to *other* players who can see the caster. The Pilot can confirm a teleport only through the item-count line (`You have 4 Scrolls of Phase Door.` or `You have no more Scrolls of Phase Door.`) or through a position or map change. Every cure potion prints something *only if its effect changed something* (finding 2).

### 2. Silent-when-useless messages (do not treat silence as failure)
`hp_player()` prints nothing at full HP. `set_blind`, `set_confused`, `set_poisoned`, `set_afraid`, `set_stun` and `set_cut` print only on a real transition (xtra2.c). CLW's cut and confusion reduction only speaks when it reaches 0. So `You have N Potions of Cure Light Wounds.` (object2.c:4250, `inven_item_describe`) is the only always-present acknowledgement. **A potion "did nothing" is invisible except through that line.** [SEEN]

### 3. Other shared or ambiguous texts
- `You cannot see!` (cmd5.c) covers both blind and no light. The scroll version splits them (`You can't see anything.` versus `You have no light to read by.`).
- `Nothing happens.` is used by Teleport Level and by Word of Recall (ironman); the text does not say which.
- `A line of blue shimmering light appears.` is the same for a wand, a rod and the mage Spear of Light.
- `You feel much better...` (three dots, ACT_HEAL2) differs from the hp_player text `You feel much better.` (period); anchor the regexes to the ending.
- `Your feel your head clear.` is a typo in the source (Restore Mana potion and staff of the Magi); match it exactly.
- Several activation texts repeat across artifacts (`Your %s glows deep blue...` in five ACT_* cases, `glows white...` in two).
- `You have %s.` (object2.c:4250) and `You have %s (%c).` (store.c:1871) are the count lines; `<item>` may begin with `no more`.

## Contradictions and surprises
- Postmortem context (§1): "no Phase Door ... message in the fight" and "8 Phase Door acks matched to 0.001 s". By the source, the only Phase Door acknowledgement the user gets is the `You have N Scrolls of Phase Door.` line. There is no "you teleport" text, so an ack-based escape check is valid only if it matches that count line. [INFERRED from code; the run logs were not checked.]
- A staff of Teleportation prints no message to its user, yet the source gives it a near-message (`%s teleports away!`), so it looks as if it should. It is sent to others only.
- Confused players can use staffs, wands and rods (with halved skill), but cannot read scrolls or cast. Blind players can use staffs and wands but not read.
- The staff's skill roll is tested before the empty check, so an empty staff can produce `You failed to use the staff properly.` before it says `has no charges left.`
- Deep Descent is not in this version, although newer Angband has it. [SEEN by grep]
