## Answer
Only about 30 of the 125 rows are live player-visible text. The rest are dead code (23), message plumbing with no text (24), or no-packet flushes `msg_print(NULL)` (4). Live rows are: escape recall activation, faint-from-hunger, light going out or faint, pack overflow, pseudo-ID "feel" lines, dawn and dusk, party, PvP, chat, save and score. Almost every message has type 0 (MSG_GENERIC), so the client cannot classify it from the type. Only chat, whisper and yell/say carry a type. A separate PKT_SOUND packet carries a sound id for a few events, and that is the only non-text signal.

## Findings

### High-relevance messages in my files
1. **Recall activates** [SEEN] (dungeon.c:1652, 1665, 1686). One of three texts, chosen by where you are. The first two are sent only when a real recall fires; reading the scroll is elsewhere.
   - `You feel yourself yanked upwards!` (in dungeon, going to town)
   - `You feel yourself yanked downwards!` (in town, going to `recall_depth`)
   - `You feel yourself yanked sideways!` (in wilderness, or `recall_depth < 0`)
   - Bystanders in view get `<name> is yanked upwards!` and the like (`msg_format_near`, audience others). The Pilot must not treat these as its own.
   - `sound(MSG_TPLEVEL)` is sent first (dungeon.c:1646).
2. **`You faint from the lack of food.`** [SEEN] (dungeon.c:1271). Sent when food < PY_FOOD_FAINT (500), the player is not a ghost and is not already paralysed, with a 10% roll per processing tick. It paralyses for 1+d5 turns and ignores Free Action. Thresholds are in mdefines.h:242-247: MAX 15000, FULL 10000, ALERT 2000, WEAK 1000, FAINT 500, STARVE 100.
3. **Light** [SEEN] (dungeon.c:1512, 1519).
   - `Your light has gone out!` is sent when fuel reaches 0 and the player is not blind. When blind, the fuel is held at 1 instead.
   - `Your light is growing faint.` is sent when fuel < 100 and fuel % 10 == 0, so it repeats every 10 turns of fuel until the light goes out.
   - Neither message is sent for artifact, Feanorian or dwarven lights (they burn no fuel).
4. `Your pack overflows!` then `You drop <item>.` [SEEN] (dungeon.c:1092, 1098). The item is dropped automatically, so the Pilot may lose loot or a consumable.
5. `Your form feels much more familliar.` [SEEN] (dungeon.c:1993). The fruit-bat form has ended. The typo "familliar" is in the source.

### Extra topics from the brief (files outside my rows, checked directly)
6. **Hunger stages** [SEEN] (xtra2.c:1650-1750, `set_food`). The state index is 0 Fainting/Starving, 1 Weak, 2 Hungry, 3 Normal, 4 Full, 5 Gorged.
   - Getting worse:
     - `You are getting faint from hunger!` (sound MSG_NOTICE)
     - `You are getting weak from hunger!` (MSG_NOTICE)
     - `You are getting hungry.` (sound MSG_HUNGRY, 127)
     - `You are no longer full.`
     - `You are no longer gorged.`
   - Getting better:
     - `You are still weak.`
     - `You are still hungry.`
     - `You are no longer hungry.`
     - `You are full!`
     - `You have gorged yourself!`
   - Ghosts get no hunger messages (xtra2.c:1676-1682).
   - `You are getting hungry.` fires below PY_FOOD_ALERT (2000). The faint check in dungeon.c:1271 is what later paralyses.
7. **Level feelings** [SEEN] (cmd4.c:164-175, 196-211). The 11 texts by `feeling` index are:
   - 0 `Looks like any other level.`
   - 1 `You feel there is something special about this level.`
   - 2 `You have a superb feeling about this level.`
   - 3 `You have an excellent feeling...`
   - 4 `You have a very good feeling...`
   - 5 `You have a good feeling...`
   - 6 `You feel strangely lucky...`
   - 7 `You feel your luck is turning...`
   - 8 `You like the look of this place...`
   - 9 `This level can't be all bad...`
   - 10 `What a boring place...`
   - Town: `Looks like a typical town.`
   - **The feeling is sent only to the player who causes the level to be generated** (dungeon.c:2075-2080, only if `Depth > 0`). Other players who arrive later, or who arrive on an already-generated level, get nothing unless they use the feeling command. The cmd4.c comment says feelings are "disabled". Lower index means more interesting, opposite to a "better" scale.
8. **Time bubble / hitpoint warning** [SEEN].
   - There is no "time bubble" message. The bubble is silent: `time_factor()` (xtra2.c:5170ff) slows game time when health% <= `hitpoint_warn`*10.
   - The only hitpoint text is `*** LOW HITPOINT WARNING! ***` (spells1.c:617-628). It is sent once, when HP first drops to or below `mhp*hitpoint_warn/10` (old HP above it) and the ALERT_HITPOINT option is on.
   - `sound(MSG_HITPOINT_WARN, 29)` is sent right after, then `msg_print(NULL)`, which sends nothing.
   - If `hitpoint_warn` is 0, `warning` is 0 and no message is ever sent.
   - The Pilot should not rely on this message.
9. **Death** [SEEN]. `sound(p_ptr, MSG_DEATH)` (xtra2.c:2668) is sent when death processing starts. The only text in the first lines of the function is `You lose consciousness.` (arena only, xtra2.c:2679). In my files, `take_hit` (spells1.c:590-612) sets `p_ptr->death` with no message. The death text is not in my files; see Leads.
10. **Disturbance** [SEEN]. `disturb()` sends no text. `take_hit` always calls `disturb(p_ptr,1,0)` (spells1.c:558), which cancels resting, running and repeats. The recall, light-out and faint messages also call `disturb`.
11. **Arrival on a level** [SEEN]. The server sends nothing on arrival except the level feeling (item 7), and only in the dungeon and only to the level's generator. Staircase text and "You enter a maze of ... staircases" are not in my files.
12. Other live one-liners [SEEN]. All are low relevance except the first two.
    - Dawn and dusk: `The sun has risen.` / `The sun has fallen.` (dungeon.c:529, 560, town or wilderness only).
    - Pseudo-ID: `You feel the <item> (<slot>) you are <wielding|...> is <feel>...` (equipment) and `You feel the <item> (<slot>) in your pack is <feel>...`. Inside the text, `is`/`are` is chosen from the item count. feel is terrible, special, worthless, excellent, cursed, broken, good or average (heavy sensing), or cursed, broken, good (light sensing) (dungeon.c:67-128, 247-257). Pack items are only tested 1 turn in 5.
    - `<name> begins a new game.`, `<name> has entered the game.`, `<name> has left the game.` (net-server.c:208, 212, 307). These are broadcasts to all other players.
    - Party and PvP rows are social only. `You have been removed from your party.` is the same text for kicked (party.c:271) and voluntary leave (party.c:311).

## Plumbing: how messages are sent (util.c, net-game.c)
- **Transport.** `msg_print(p,msg)` calls `msg_print_aux(p,msg,MSG_GENERIC)`. It logs, dedups, then calls `send_message` (util.c:1853-1918). `send_message` sends packet PKT_MESSAGE with a u16 type plus the text, clipped to MSG_LEN 256 (net-game.c:923-946). Clients older than 1.5.2 get 78 characters.
- **Prefixes.** No general prefix is added. Text is exactly what the format produces. Prefixes are added only where a call site builds them:
  - chat: `[sender] text`
  - whisper: `[target:sender] text`
  - party chat: `[party:sender] text`
  - yell heard unseen: `Someone yells, "..."` (util.c:2138-2145)
- **Monster names.** `msg_format_monster` (util.c:2028-2085) formats twice, once with the visible name and once with a forced-hidden name ("It", "Something"). Each nearby player gets whichever fits what they can see. If the "unseen" format is NULL, players who can't see the monster get nothing.
- **Audiences.** `msg_*_near` reaches players in the view of the acting player, not the actor. `msg_broadcast` reaches everyone except the subject, with type MSG_CHAT (257).
- **The dedup trap for the Pilot** [SEEN] (util.c:1853-1918, client net-client.c:1807-1821). If a message identical to the last logged one is sent again with the same type, the server sends PKT_MESSAGE_REPEAT with only the type, not the text.
  - The stock client re-plays its own last message on receipt. Any consumer of the raw text stream would see no second copy.
  - Dedup applies only to logged messages: type <= MSG_CHAT, and text not starting with `[`. It compares against the previous logged message truncated to 78 characters.
  - [INFERRED] Whether the Pilot's client shows "(xN)" or just re-fires the message depends on the client code, which I did not check. The Pilot should count repeats, since two consecutive identical hits are otherwise one line. Two identical "The X bites you." lines do not arrive as two texts.
- **Types the client could use** [SEEN] (defines.h:691-859).
  - The type space is the sound/message-type list: MSG_HIT 1, MSG_MISS 2, MSG_FLEE 3, MSG_KILL 5, MSG_LEVEL 6, MSG_DEATH 7, MSG_TELEPORT 9, MSG_QUAFF 11, MSG_TPLEVEL 24, MSG_HITPOINT_WARN 29, MSG_MON_HIT..MSG_MON_MOAN 33-51, MSG_BLIND..MSG_CUT 53-75, MSG_BR_* 90-109, MSG_SUM_* 110-123, MSG_DRAIN_STAT 148, MSG_MULTIPLY 149, and so on.
  - A minority of calls pass a non-zero type to `msg_print_aux`, `msg_format_type` or `msg_format_monster`. That is 52 mentions of those functions, definitions included, and the common counts show MSG_RECOVER 23, MSG_PY_MISC 9 and MSG_HITWALL 8.
  - Every plain `msg_print`/`msg_format` (the great majority) is type 0.
  - **Consequence:** the type cannot replace text matching in general. It could help for the subset that has one (breaths, summons, monster blows, stat drain) only if the client exposes it. The rest of the study (other M clerks) should say per message.
  - `sound()` is a different packet (PKT_SOUND, util.c:887, net-game.c:964) that carries only the id. Sound ids seen next to my events include MSG_TPLEVEL at recall, MSG_HUNGRY at "getting hungry", MSG_NOTICE at faint, weak and full, and MSG_HITPOINT_WARN. MSG_DEATH is sent in `player_death`. `MSG_DRAIN_STAT` at spells2.c:302 is sound only, so that is another sound signal for stat drain.
  - Whether the pilot's client surfaces sounds is not checked.
- `msg_print(p, NULL)` sends nothing (net-game.c:929); it is a legacy "flush" call.

## Contradictions and surprises
- **The extractor picked up dead code.** 23 of the 125 rows are commented out, `#if 0`, or unreachable:
  - enter_wizard/debug/borg mode (dungeon.c:636-748): static and never called, and gated by ALLOW_WIZARD/ALLOW_BORG, both commented out in options.h:249-254.
  - The unique-respawn announcement (dungeon.c:1815-1829).
  - The generate.c cheat messages (`Monster nest`, `Monster pit`, `Lesser Vault`, `Greater Vault`, `Destroyed Level`).
  - The init1.c "Unknown player flag" messages.
  - The PvP-party hostility messages (party.c:628-661, 713-741).
  - Two `msg_print(NULL)` rows and the comment hit at files.c:3316.
  - Rows for these have blank regexes. The Pilot need not match them.
- **Level feelings** are sent to the level's creator only. A Pilot that expects a feeling on every arrival will wait forever.
- **The hitpoint warning is optional.** It depends on the ALERT_HITPOINT option and a non-zero `hitpoint_warn` (net-game.c:1478 sets it from the client).
- `Score not registered ...` texts are live (SCORE_* undefined), but reach only a player who quits or dies through close_game.
