# Dispatch: M2 message catalogue (spells1.c, spells2.c, xtra1.c, xtra2.c)
- Brief: catalogue all 333 message calls in M2_input.csv (spells1.c 101, spells2.c 83, xtra1.c 18, xtra2.c 131), plus the set_<status>() table and the stat drain/sustain texts.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-29; sources: scratch/M2_input.csv, server source in github/src/server/ (spells1.c, spells2.c, xtra1.c, xtra2.c, object1.c describe_use, util.c msg_broadcast), postmortem section 1.
- Output: scratch/M2_catalogue.csv (338 rows for 333 calls; the extra rows are calls that give several texts). Every regex was compiled and matched against a sample rendering, except the placeholder rows (flush and social), which are marked `^(?!)$` or `^.+$`. Builder script: scratch/M2_build.py.

## Answer
The Pilot can read nearly all of its status, stat, heal, escape and death information from these four files. Each status has an "on" message, and most have an "off" message. Only stun, cut and hunger have graded levels. Every set_<status>() message is sent only when the status actually changes. Re-applying an active status is silent. The stat drain texts are "You feel very weak." (drained) and "You feel weak for a moment, but the feeling passes." (sustained). The brief's example wording for the sustain text was slightly off. Blind-only "You are hit by ..." lines are the only damage messages in spells1.c project_p. When the player can see, no message is sent there. A resisted effect also gets no message there.

2. **Stat drain vs sustain** [SEEN spells2.c:271-303]:
   - Drained: `You feel very <weak|stupid|naive|clumsy|sickly|ugly>.` The stats are STR, INT, WIS, DEX, CON, CHR in that order. This is sent only if dec_stat() actually lowered the stat. **If the stat is already at its minimum, nothing is printed.**
   - Sustained: `You feel <adj> for a moment, but the feeling passes.` This is sent only when a drain was attempted against a sustain.
   - Restored: `You feel less <adj>.` This is sent by do_res_stat, and also by do_inc_stat when only the restore part worked (spells2.c:315-359). Both rows have the same text.
   - Gain: `Wow!  You feel very <strong|smart|wise|dextrous|healthy|cute>!` (two spaces after "Wow!").
   - GF_TIME (project_p) uses different words, not blockable by sustain: `You're not as <strong|bright|wise|agile|hale|beautiful> as you used to be...` (one stat), `You're not as powerful as you used to be...` (all six) [SEEN spells1.c:3883-3908].
   - Ring of Power has one line for all stats: `You are surrounded by a malignant aura.` (spells2.c:5004).
   - Nexus and polymorph scramble stats with `Your body starts to scramble...`. There is no per-stat text afterwards.

3. **Heal messages (escape verification)** [SEEN spells2.c:96-143]:
   - hp_player prints one line by amount healed: less than 5 gives "You feel a little better."; 5-14 "You feel better."; 15-34 "You feel much better."; 35 or more "You feel very good."
   - **Nothing is printed if HP is already full.** A quaff that heals nothing gives no "feel better" line, so the Pilot must not treat its absence as failure when HP is full.
   - The Pilot should still expect the item's own line (potion effects live in other files).

4. **Recall** [SEEN spells2.c:1131-1200]:
   - Start: `The air about you becomes charged...` The timer is 15+randint0(20) player turns.
   - **A second read cancels the first:** `A tension leaves the air around you...`
   - Ironman servers (cfg_ironman, unless the player has won) print "Nothing happens." for both scroll and rod.
   - The DM command uses the same text as a real recall start (xtra2.c:6489), which is a false-positive risk only for admin actions.

5. **Level teleport** (teleport_player_level, spells1.c:371-439) [SEEN]:
   - "You sink through the floor." / "You rise up through the ceiling." In town it is always "sink". In the dungeon it is 50/50, and it is always "rise" at max depth or on a quest level.
   - In the wilderness it says "A gust of wind blows you north/east/south/west."
   - "Nothing happens." is printed on ironman or in the arena.

6. **Blind-only attack text** (project_p, spells1.c:3560-3960) [SEEN]:
   - `fuzzy = blind` (spells1.c:3549). The lines "You are hit by acid! / fire! / cold! / lightning! / poison!" and the vague "something", "something sharp" and "something strange" are printed only if the player is blind.
   - The vague texts do not identify the element: "something" covers missile/sound/confusion/force/lite/dark/mana/meteor/holy orb/plasma/water. "Sharp" covers arrow, shards and ice. "Strange" covers nether, chaos, disenchant, nexus, inertia, time and gravity.
   - The CSV `event` column carries the GF type as `attack.unseen.hit.<gf>` for each line.
   - When the player can see, project_p prints nothing. The announcement ("breathes fire" and similar) comes from melee2.c, which is not in M2.
   - **A resisted effect gets no message.** Resist shows only as a different damage number, or as the absence of the status message (for example no "You are poisoned!").
   - Exp-drain texts are not blind-only. GF_NETHER and GF_CHAOS print "You keep hold of your life force!" (hold life, 75%), "You feel your life slipping away!" (hold life, 25%, small loss) or "You feel your life draining away!" (no hold life) whether the player can see or not. Nether resist prints nothing.

8. **Death** [SEEN xtra2.c:2672-2806]:
   - A living player gets "You die." then a flush, then (unless ironman/NO_GHOST option, or fruit bat) "You have been killed by <killer>." The player then becomes a ghost and is teleported.
   - Ghost destruction gives "Your incorporeal body fades away - FOREVER."
   - Arena defeat gives "You lose consciousness." (no death).
   - Broadcasts go to all other players. Row 1327 covers five texts. Examples: "<name> the level N <race> <class> was killed by <killer>.", "The brave hero ...", "... was turned into a fruit bat by <killer>!", "... committed suicide."
   - "<unique> rises from the dead!" is sent when a player dies (uniques below 65% of max depth are un-killed, xtra2.c:2500-2516).

9. **Kill messages** (mon_take_hit, xtra2.c:2977-3004) [SEEN]:
   - A kill with a spell, arrow or wand prints `<Monster><note>`: " dies.", " is destroyed.", " dissolves!" or " shrivels away in the light!".
   - A melee kill prints one of three lines, chosen by the monster type (not by weapon).
     - "You have killed X." is used when the monster was unseen.
     - "You have destroyed X." is used for demons, undead, RF2_STUPID monsters and letters E, v and g.
     - "You have slain X." is used for everything else.
   - Uniques additionally print "<unique> was slain by <name>." (monster_death, 2138) and broadcast it.
   - The monster name is `monster_desc` mode 0 ("the Grip, Farmer Maggot's Dog" or "it"). It is capitalised only via %^s in the spell forms.

## Contradictions and surprises
- **Sustain text differs from the brief.** The brief quotes "You feel weak for a moment, but it passes." The source reads "...but the feeling passes." (spells2.c:290). [SEEN]
- **Three input rows never fire.** Rows 762, 1340 and 1341 are inside comments (`/* ... */`), so they are dead code [SEEN]. Row 762 is a commented-out apply_morph case. Rows 1340-1341 are the "You are confused." aim warnings. Confused aiming is therefore silent, and the direction is randomised with a 75% chance (xtra2.c:5030-5035). The event vocabulary has `dead_code` for them.
- **`msg_broadcast` skips the sender** (util.c:1926-1929). Rows using it are audience `all` in the catalogue but never reach the acting player. For the level-up broadcast, that player sees only "Welcome to level N.".
- **`msg_format_near` and party detect messages are extra copies to others.** Detect messages also send "<name> senses the presence of ..." via party_msg_format_near to party members. These are not in the CSV because they are a different function; the Advisor may want a sweep of `party_msg_format_near`.
- "You feel less <adj>." comes from two functions (restore vs inc_stat) and is the same text in both.
- **The "flush" hack is rare here.** Only 8 of 333 rows.

## Ambiguities (same text, different meaning; same event, many texts)
- "You resist the effects!" has 7 sources (nexus x2, morph x2, slow save, sleep save, polymorph resist), all saving throws or resists with different effects.
- "There is a bright flash of light!" is a trap destroyed (769-770), a door destroyed (772) or, with a period, a recharge blowing up (906). "Click!" is a door unlocked or a chest disarmed. "Nothing happens." (750, 864) is a level teleport or recall blocked on ironman or in the arena.
- "You are unaffected!" is free action; "You disbelieve the feeble spell." and "You refuse to be frightened." each have a resist-flag and a saving-throw source. "You are hit by something!" covers 10 GF types.
- `<Monster><note>` has 5 call sites (784, 785, 786, 1332, 1333). Only 1333 with "dies." or "is destroyed." is a player spell kill.
- "The air about you becomes charged..." is a real recall or a DM command. "You have been killed by X." (1330) follows "You die." (1323) in the same death.

## Leads
- Attack announcements (breath, bolt, melee) live in melee1.c/melee2.c, not M2. They are the primary danger cues.
- Hunger effects (faint = paralysis, gorged = slow) and the recall-activation messages are in dungeon.c; I did not check them.
- `party_msg_format_near` calls (detect messages to party members) are not in the CSV.
