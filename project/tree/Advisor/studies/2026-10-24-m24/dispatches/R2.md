- Brief says "bad arrival ... costs the return scroll at once": true only for m24 (+92 s). In m22 the bad outcomes came after 4-8 min of normal exploring; those are "mid-trip emergencies on a level reached by recall", not arrival hazards.
- m22 is a confound (mob levels, Sonnet Navigator, 4 of 6 bad). The "arrival" rate depends heavily on whether m22 is kept.
- The Pilot's auto D/S use does not run under `goal explore`; m24 and m22 #1-#3 had devices unused.
