# Dispatch: recall arrival mechanics and escapes (R1, pass 1)
- Brief: what a recall/stairs arrival does (placement, timing, sleep, feelings, detection, pit frequency) and what a warrior can do about a bad one.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-23; sources: github/src/server/{generate.c, dungeon.c, spells1.c, spells2.c, use-obj.c, monster2.c, melee2.c, cmd2.c, cmd4.c, tables.c, mdefines.h}, github/mangband.cfg, lib/edit/object.txt, rulebook constants.csv / shop_stock.csv / char_level_speeds.csv. Scratch: scratch/R1/sim.py.

## Answer
A recall arrival is a uniformly random naked floor grid anywhere on the 198x66 level. It is fixed once per level and avoids only vault grids (CAVE_ICKY), not monsters, pits or nests. There is no grace period, and monsters in pits/nests are generated awake. The only instant exits are stairs (none known on arrival) and Teleport Level, which the shops don't sell. Deep Descent does not exist in 1.5. Shop escapes are Phase Door (15g), Staff of Teleportation (2000g, stock 2) and a second WoR (150g, 15-34 blocks). A pit/nest level always gives the feeling "You feel there is something special about this level" at 300-1000 ft, but only to the player who generates the level and only if that player generated a level more than 1000 game ticks earlier. Orc pits are about 1.5-6% of levels at 300-1000 ft.

## Findings
1. **Recall arrival = random spot.** [SEEN] `new_player_spot` (generate.c:315-338) picks y in 1..64, x in 1..196 uniformly. It requires `cave_naked_bold` (plain FEAT_FLOOR, no object, no monster) and not CAVE_ICKY, then saves the result as `level_rand_y/x`. dungeon.c:2146-2149 uses it for LEVEL_RAND (recall, Teleport Level, trapdoor?). The game then `scatter`s from that point to an empty grid (dungeon.c:2169-2183). The spot is chosen before the level's monsters are placed (generate.c:3358, then `alloc_monster` at 3367). There is no distance-from-monsters rule. Generation-time monsters use `alloc_monster(Depth,0,TRUE)`, so they have no minimum distance either (monster2.c:2311-2348).
   - [INFERRED] CAVE_ICKY is set only by vault builders (generate.c:2370; 876 clears it; 3461 and 3633 are town/arena code). Pits and nests are not ICKY, so you can land in a pit room. The pit's 95 inner grids are all filled with monsters, so only the 1-wide floor ring around the inner room is possible.
2. **Stairs arrival.** [SEEN]
   - Going down uses `level_down_y/x`, which is the position of the last-placed up staircase. Going up uses `level_up_y/x`, the last-placed down staircase (generate.c:626-655; dungeon.c:2127-2136).
   - The first placement try is `scatter` with d=0 (dungeon.c:2169), so you stand exactly on that staircase if no monster is there. Otherwise you stand within 1 grid, widening every 150 tries.
   - Each level has 3-4 down and 1-2 up staircases (generate.c:3351-3354). The staircase you arrive on is therefore the way back (up when you went down). It is not a "connected stairs" feature.
   - Option `dungeon_stair` ("connected stairs") is listed in tables.c:2798 but nothing in the server reads it. `create_down_stair/create_up_stair` are set in cmd2.c:104,208 and never consumed (grep shows only cmd2.c and variable.c). The option is dead code. mangband.cfg has no stair option (grep found none).
3. **Escape by arrival staircase.** [INFERRED from 2] After a stairs arrival the up staircase is underfoot, so '<' then '>' is the cheap escape. A recall arrival has no such staircase, which is the bad case in M24.
4. **WoR timing.** [SEEN]
   - `set_recall` sets `word_recall = randint0(20)+15`, i.e. 15..34 (spells2.c:1191). It is decremented once per "block" in process_player_end (dungeon.c:1625-1629). It is postponed one block if the player is in a store (1632-1637).
   - Reading it again while active cancels it: "A tension leaves the air around you..." (spells2.c:1195-1198). This is relevant to "read the second WoR at once", which would cancel a recall in progress.
   - A recall read in the dungeon goes to the town (dungeon.c:1652-1660). It does not go to max depth.
   - Block length is `time = level_speed/1000` ticks, divided by tf/100 (dungeon.c:1112-1116, per constants.csv). At 75 FPS and depth 12 (600 ft), level_speed=50000 (char_level_speeds.csv), so a block is 50 ticks, about 0.67 s.
   - Normal play with monsters in view: 15-34 blocks = about 10-23 s. Resting or running with no LOS (tf=1000) gives time=5 ticks per block, about 1-2.3 s. This confirms the "already known" ~10x.
   - The 75 FPS figure is from constants.csv, which I did not re-check.
5. **No arrival grace.** [SEEN] melee2.c:3420-3422 skips a player's monsters only while `p_ptr->new_level_flag` is set, which is the tick of the transfer. The 'Z' hound removal for LEVEL_DOWN and LEVEL_RAND deletes non-unique hounds within MAX_SIGHT (20) of the arrival spot (dungeon.c:2205-2243). Nothing else is removed or frozen. Levels are deallocated as soon as the last player leaves (dungeon.c:2036-2043), so every recall generates a fresh level.
6. **Awake or asleep.** [SEEN]
   - Ordinary level-gen monsters: `alloc_monster(...,slp=TRUE)` (generate.c:3367-3369). Each sleeps for `2*sleep + randint1(10*sleep)` if its race has sleep > 0 (monster2.c:1990-1994). Pit and nest monsters are placed with `slp=FALSE` (generate.c:1974, 2280-2324), so they are always awake, and the 95 is the exact pit count (38 + 48 + 9).
   - Vault monsters are placed asleep (generate.c:1096). Later spawns during play are awake: `alloc_monster(..., MAX_SIGHT+5, FALSE)` at dungeon.c:626.
   - Waking depends on player noise: `notice^3 <= noise` with notice = randint0(1024), and `d = 100/dist` per wake roll (melee2.c:2498-2530). Aggravation wakes everything.
   - Awake monsters within their `aaf` "sense" radius act regardless of walls (melee2.c:3515-3519). Orcs open secret doors (melee2.c:2870-2900), so the pit's secret door does not hold them.
7. **Level feelings.** [SEEN]
   - "You enter a maze of down/up staircases." is the stairs message (cmd2.c:186, 82). It has no meaning beyond "stairs taken".
   - "You feel your luck is turning..." is feeling index 7 (cmd4.c:164-175). It means rating 21-30 (generate.c:4227-4235). The rating comes from out-of-depth monsters (monster2.c:1860,1870), good items (object2.c) and vault ratings. It is not a pit signal.
   - The feeling is set only for the player who generates the level (dungeon.c:2074-2080). A player arriving on an existing level gets none. It is forced to 0 ("Looks like any other level.") if that player's previous generation was less than 1000 game ticks ago (generate.c:4244-4247; `old_turn` at 4327). That is about 13 s at 75 FPS, so a quick WoR/stairs chain gives no feeling.
   - A nest, pit or vault sets `good_item_flag` when `Depth<=40 && randint1(Depth²+1) < 300` (generate.c:1960, 2271, 2520, 2560). This is always true up to Depth 17 (850 ft). Artifacts also set it (object2.c:3303). The flag overrides the rating: feeling = 1, "You feel there is something special about this level." (generate.c:4239).
   - [INFERRED] If the M24 orc-pit level showed "luck is turning", that message came from another level, or the pit tell was lost. A pit level at 600 ft should show "something special", or "Looks like any other level" if suppressed. This needs the log Clerk to check which message came with which arrival.
8. **Detection ranges.** [SEEN] `DETECT_DIST_X=52`, `DETECT_DIST_Y=23` (spells2.c:1212-1213), a rectangle of 105x47 around the player clipped to the level. The level is 198x66 (defines.h:82,88), so that covers about 53% of the width and 71% of the height.
   - Magic Mapping scroll and staff, and the Rod of Magic Mapping, call `map_area`, which maps non-walls and adjacent walls in that box (spells2.c:1223-1280).
   - The Rod of Door/Stair Location (object.txt:3053-3060, "detects all secret doors and stairs") and the scroll and staff call `detect_sdoor`, over the same box (spells2.c:1991-2060). It turns every secret door into a visible door and marks all doors and stairs.
   - [INFERRED] A door or doors with no stairs in range is no help. A pit's inner room has exactly one (secret) door at the middle of one of its four walls.
9. **Escapes and prices.** [SEEN] (object.txt; shop_stock.csv)
   - **Phase Door** (scroll, 15g, Alchemist stock 3): `teleport_player(10)`, i.e. 5-10 squares, never into CAVE_ICKY, needs a naked floor grid (use-obj.c:795, spells1.c:167-227).
   - **Teleportation scroll** (`teleport_player(100)`, 50-100 squares, object.txt:1544; level 10, 40g base): not in any shop in shop_stock.csv.
   - **Staff of Teleportation** (use-obj.c:1124): Magic-User store, 2000g, entries=2, shop level 20. 50-100 squares, no failure check seen.
   - **Teleport Level scroll** (object.txt:1552): not in any shop. It goes 50% up / 50% down, and arrival is LEVEL_RAND (spells1.c:365-395). It is a no-op in Ironman, and IRONMAN=false in the cfg.
   - **Deep Descent**: not found. grep for "deep descent" and DEEP_DESCENT in src/server, lib/edit/*.txt and the headers returned nothing.
   - **WoR**: 150g, Alchemist stock 4. It is also the only way to a guaranteed fresh level.
   - **Potion of Speed**: not in any shop (the csv has none; the Black Market lists only Healing).
10. **Pit/nest frequency.** [SEEN code; simulated numbers]
    - The unusual-room test is `randint0(200) < Depth` twice (generate.c:3213-3217), where Depth is in 50-ft levels. A pit is built if roll k is in [25,40) after a vault fails or does not fit (3223-3229). A nest is k in [40,50).
    - Minimum level is 5 for both. At most one pit or nest per level (`dun->crowded`, 3057, 3115-3118). 50 room attempts per level. 1 in 15 levels are "destroyed" beyond Depth 10 (trivial rooms only, 3167, 3200-3208).
    - Pit type is `randint1(Depth)`: <20 orc, <40 troll, <60 giant, <80 dragon, else demon (generate.c:2060-2090). **At 600-950 ft (Depth 12-19) a pit is always an orc pit.** At 1000 ft it is 5% troll.
    - Nests below Depth 30 are jelly only.
    - My Monte Carlo (sim.py, 20k levels per depth, models room-block occupancy, dungeon_align and destroyed levels):

| depth | pit | nest | vault |
|---|---|---|---|
| 300 ft | 0.7% | 0.3% | 0.4% |
| 500 ft | 1.6% | 0.7% | 0.9% |
| 600 ft | 2.5% | 0.9% | 1.2% |
| 800 ft | 4.2% | 1.8% | 2.1% |
| 1000 ft | 6.3% | 2.5% | 3.2% |

    [INFERRED] These are not log-calibrated. They ignore get_mon_num failures (an "empty" pit is dropped).
11. **Pit geometry.** [SEEN code, INFERRED layout] build_type6 (generate.c:2024-2330):
    - Outer floor yval±4 by xval±11. Inner room walls at yval±3 and xval±10, with 19x5 interior filled with 95 monsters (every grid).
    - One secret door on a random side, at the middle of that inner wall. Centre (yval, xval) is on the 11-square block grid, as already known.

## Contradictions and surprises
- The brief says "Rod of Door/Stair Location". The object exists (object.txt:3053), but the shop sells the Staff (object.txt:2743, 350g), not the Rod. The Rod is a dungeon find at level 15, base 1000g.
- "Connected stairs" in the options table is a no-op, but you do arrive on the opposite staircase type (finding 2). So the effect exists by construction, not via an option.
- Pits and nests (all awake) are not vault-protected from recall or teleport arrivals. Only vault grids are excluded.
- A second WoR read while the first is pending cancels it (finding 4).
