## Contradictions and surprises
1. **next_steps.md's own mission narrative omits a death.** Common.md ("already known") and
   next_steps.md's mission list describe only the mission-7 death at 750 ft. But `git log -1
   --format=%B 3b7729f` (commit dated 2026-09-26 04:26, well before the numbered "mission 1"
   commits) describes an earlier death of Dive03 at 1000 ft to Stone trolls/Gnome mage/Black ogre —
   with the same signature (Phase Door reuse cooldown too slow) that killed it again in mission 7.
   next_steps.md's "Status 2026-09-26" section says only "stair-scum dive to 650 ft, two uniques
   killed" for that period, no death mentioned. [SEEN: commit message vs next_steps.md text] This
   means the class-2 danger-judgement failure pattern (continue exploring after a narrow escape;
   Phase Door cooldown too slow under 35% HP) manifested **twice**, a year apart in project time, and
   was only partially closed by the first fix — the second time it was fatal because a different bug
   (`escape()` returning False, #29) silently discarded the queued Phase Doors and WoR entirely.
2. **HANDBOOK's "WoR landed at 1000 ft next to 4 Black ogres" (#8) is a third, separate near-death**
   at 1000 ft (different monster line-up — Black ogres, not Stone trolls) with no commit or mission
   number attached to it in either source. It's unclear from these files alone whether this predates
   or postdates the 1000 ft Stone-troll death; the Advisor may want to ask the Architect directly.
3. **The two Phase-0 dives (dive01, dive02) restarted the pilot process very frequently** (dive01: 6
   times in ~80 min; dive02: 6 times in ~2 min) — consistent with `restart.sh` being used for rapid
   iterative deployment, not with crashes (pilot.log shows clean `ready` events each time, no error
   text). I flag this as [INFERRED] rather than [SEEN], since I did not find a log line stating why
   each restart happened.
4. dive04's Radiation eye at what should be a very shallow level (#45) reads as either a level-
   generation anomaly, a monster-glyph/name mis-decode (the same class of bug as #12, the Yellow
   mold that wasn't on the monster list at all), or the Navigator simply being wrong about vanilla
   Angband norms on this server (1.5.3 may differ). Worth checking `monster.txt`'s native depth for
   Radiation eye against the danger-table Clerk's output — flagged as a lead, not resolved here.
5. Given the failure table, danger-judgement and escape/heal are the two classes directly implicated
   in Dive03's fatal death, and both were "fixed" once already (after the 1000 ft near-death) before
   recurring in a different form at 750 ft — this is the clearest evidence in the whole record that
   surprises here get patched narrowly rather than modelled, echoing next_steps.md's own reflection
   #3 ("when the same surprise happens twice, stop patching and model the mechanism").
