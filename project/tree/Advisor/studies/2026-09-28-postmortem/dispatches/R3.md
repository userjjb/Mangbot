# Dispatch: Dive03's death and its three worst near-deaths
- Brief: reconstruct Dive03's mission-7 death second-by-second and its 3 worst prior near-deaths;
  check the "Pilot bug" claim; test a 0.6×HP-melee "leave" rule against each episode.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-28; sources covered: runs/pilot/dive03/{events,decisions}.jsonl (full files,
  81184 + 30767 lines), pilot.log (full), navigator.md (tail read), next_steps.md (mission-7
  section), Advisor/data/monsters/danger_table.csv (name lookups).

## Answer
Dive03 died at 22:28:39 (750 ft) to an Uruk, in a summon-trap ambush (4 Uruks + a Giant red
scorpion + a Shrieker mushroom patch) that the character walked into at full HP (253/253). It spent
its last 14 s reading Phase Door 8 times, quaffing 2 potions, and reading Word of Recall once — and
**none of the 11 attempts had any in-game effect**: no "you feel...", no inventory-count message, no
position change, ever appears in events.jsonl for these commands, while combat messages continued
uninterrupted. This directly confirms the Architect's diagnosis in next_steps.md (an `escape()` /
Mover race that lets `clear`+`walk` wipe queued emergency items). The three worst prior near-deaths
(31/152=20.4%, 51/244=20.9%, 56/182=30.8% HP) show the opposite: phase doors and potions worked every
time (inventory decremented, "you feel much better" appeared, HP recovered), so the death is not a
generic "big pack" story — it is this specific execution bug. The 0.6×HP rule would have fired near
the start of the death fight (HP 207, ~0.5 s after the trap) but ~6 s before the Pilot's actual first
escape attempt; given the bug, an earlier trigger would not have changed the outcome, since the
escape commands never executed regardless of when they were issued.

**The Pilot bug, checked against the log:** `next_steps.md:161-164` says `escape()` returning False
let the explore goal's Mover send `clear`+`walk` every ~2s, wiping queued Phase Doors/potions/WoR
[REPORTED, Architect's account]. The raw events confirm the *symptom* exactly: every item-use `ack`
in the fatal window (796.84–804.256s) is immediately bracketed by `ack clear` / `ack walk N` pairs at
~1.5–2s spacing (events.jsonl lines ~77880+; e.g. t=791.725 `clear`, 793.258 `clear`, 794.788 `clear`,
796.327 `clear`, 796.84 `custom r item=9`, 797.446 `custom q item=6`, 798.054 `clear`…), and **not one**
of the 8 Phase Door reads, 2 potion quaffs, or the WoR read produced any server message or position
change — the character's `pos` stayed [10,180] for the entire fight (decisions.jsonl), and the death
inventory dump still lists "Scroll of Word of Recall" and "Potions of Cure Critical Wounds (x2)"
unused (events.jsonl:81176 itemlist). [SEEN] This matches the count in next_steps.md — "8 queued
Phase Doors, 2 potions and the WoR" — exactly (8 `item=9` + 1 `item=6` + 1 `item=8` + 1 `item=10` = 11
acts, none executed).

**Contradiction found:** Dive03's own navigator.md tells a different story at 22:29: "tried read WoR
+ Phase: PILOT NOT ANSWERING (Connection refused) mid-fight" and later "pilot still refusing
connections after 12 min... stop: report as Pilot crash." But pilot.log shows **no restart or crash**
during the fight: the last "in the game as Dive03" ready line before death is 22:15:16, and the next
log line is "22:28:39 pilot stopping" — right at the moment of death, not before. events.jsonl also
logs continuous acks throughout the fight (walks, clears, item acts) up to t=805.115, one line after
death. So the Pilot process was alive and communicating with the game server the whole time; the
Navigator's "Connection refused" must be about a different channel (its own query/control socket),
not the game connection. **The proximate cause was queued-command loss, not a Pilot crash** — this
directly contradicts the Navigator's own contemporaneous belief, though it agrees with next_steps.md.

**What it could have done:** stairs were not known nearby (per the 22:28:32.833 `emergency_loop` note,
already true before the trap sprang, since 750ft this level had none discovered); 2 Cure Critical
Wounds potions and 1 Word of Recall scroll were in the pack and inscribed (item slots 8, 10) but
never actually consumed; had any one of the first 2-3 Phase Door reads executed, the character would
likely have escaped the ambush square, since the four Uruks + scorpion never moved off [10,180] in
the log (no monlist shows them anywhere else) and post-teleport HP (117-253 for most of the fight)
was well above lethal for a single follow-up hit.

### 3. Danger-sign-to-escape lag and the 0.6×HP rule
Rule: sum(monster count × melee_max × speed_x from danger_table.csv, matched by monlist name) ≥ 0.6 ×
current HP. Lookups used [SEEN, danger_table.csv]: Uruk 30×1.0; Giant red scorpion 15×1.0; Shrieker
mushroom patch 0×1.0; Cave orc 8×1.0; Hill orc 10×1.0; Lagduf, the Snaga 38×1.0; Tiger 22×2.0; Clear
hound 20×1.0; Dark elven warrior 16×1.0; Phase spider 20×2.0; Kobold archer 9×1.0; Mughash the Kobold
Lord 30×1.0.

- **Death:** danger sign (trap+monlist) 22:28:24.908, HP 253, sum=4×30+15+0=135 → 0.6×253=151.8,
  rule not yet met. HP next reading 207 (22:28:25.432): 135 ≥ 0.6×207=124.2 → **rule fires**, ~0.5s
  after the trap. Pilot's actual first escape act was at 22:28:31.567 — **rule leads by ~6.1s**. Given
  the execution bug, this earlier warning would not have changed the outcome (nothing the Pilot sent

1. **Navigator's own account of the death is wrong about the cause.** navigator.md (22:29, 22:41)

   events.jsonl show the process alive and logging without interruption through the death. [SEEN,
   contradicts common.md's framing too, which only mentions the STR-drain/blows detail, not the
   Navigator's crash belief — worth flagging to the Architect since the Navigator may misdiagnose
   similar events again.]
2. **The STR drain was two separate drains (3→2 then 2→1), not one "3→1" event** as common.md's
   shorthand suggests — a minor simplification, not an error, but the two `stat_drained` messages are
   6s apart and matter for the timeline.
3. Unlike the three near-deaths, the death fight shows **zero** successful item-use messages after
   the trap sprang — a clean, checkable signature of the bug (no "You have N Scrolls...", no "You
   feel...", no recall countdown message, ever, across 11 attempts).
