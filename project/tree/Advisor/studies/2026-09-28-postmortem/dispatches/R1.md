# Dispatch: Dive03 incident catalogue

- Brief: catalogue all `attention` incidents in dive03/decisions.jsonl, find the worst episodes, count
  recurring patterns, measure time use, and summarize the Navigator's mission-by-mission calls.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-28; sources covered: `runs/pilot/dive03/decisions.jsonl` (full, 30,767 lines),

## Answer
The 636 `attention` records break into 21 `what` categories dominated by `emergency` (164),
`goal_done` (188, not a danger signal), `low_supply` (63, almost all one 2-hour lantern-out-of-oil
spell), `unseen_attacker` (48), and `fight_going_badly` (30). The worst HP moments cluster on two
levels: depth 20 (1000 ft, 40 emergencies, including a full character death) and depth 13 (650 ft, 47
emergencies, fear/priest trouble). Stair-taking dominates all `act` records (2,705 of 4,128, ~66%),
confirming heavy stair-scum/flee behavior. Active goal-driven time splits ~33% explore, ~32% dive,
~26% shop/town errands, with dungeon time (~76%) far exceeding town time (~24%) of the ~443 min of
continuous play captured. **Important finding**: dive03 died twice, not once — an earlier,
unremarked death at 1000 ft on 2026-09-25 (Stone troll + Gnome mage, blows cut 4→1) precedes the
known mission-7 death at 750 ft: the same failure signature (a drain effect collapsing blows to 1
right before death) recurs in both.

## Contradictions and surprises
- **The file holds two deaths and (per navigator.md's own section headers) seven missions spanning
  three calendar days (09-25, 09-26, 09-27), not the "missions 3-7" framing in the brief/common.md.**
  navigator.md's own text has explicit "mission 2 start" (nominal 16:14), "mission 3 start" (nominal
  11:07), "mission 4 start" (12:05), "mission 5 start" (12:44), "mission 6 start" (13:35), "mission 7
  start" (22:15) headers — i.e. missions 1 and 2 are also dive03's, contradicting common.md's "Dive03
  played missions 3–7." I did not exclude mission 1/2 records from Q1's 636-count (the brief asked
  for "all attention records (636)", which only matches if missions 1-2 are included), but flag this
  for the Advisor to reconcile against the study's framing.
- **Navigator.md's nominal HH:MM timestamps are unreliable across days**: they read as one continuous
  day when they are not. Matching monster names (Wormtongue steal, Grishnakh fight) and specific
  detail strings (e.g. "lantern EMPTY (0 turns)") to `decisions.jsonl` shows mission 1 actually ran
  2026-09-25 20:55 through 2026-09-26 16:14 (with ~19 real hours of gaps the Navigator's log doesn't
  show as gaps), mission 2 ran 09-26 16:14-16:54, and missions 3-6 all ran 09-27 11:07-13:58 (matching
  nominal HH:MM exactly, just on a different date than a naive top-to-bottom read suggests). The
  Navigator itself flags one instance of this (line 69: "my timestamps since 12:05 were estimates and
  ran ahead; real clock is 12:21"), but the death-night mismatch (finding 3, this dispatch) is a much
  larger, unflagged instance.
- **The claimed "Pilot bug" cause of death is not corroborated by pilot.log or the death-second
  telemetry.** decisions.jsonl shows the pilot actively issuing correct emergency commands (phase
  door ×3, CLW ×12, CCW ×2, WoR-read) through the last 4 seconds before death, and pilot.log shows no
  crash/error/disconnect messages at all, with the actual client-reported death cause being "Killed by
  poison." This doesn't rule out a bug (e.g., the earlier `emergency_loop`/TypeError bug noted at
  navigator.md line 90 in mission 6 on the same run), but the specific claim that the pilot was
  "not answering" during the fatal fight is not supported by the primary logs I read.
- A first (unremarked) death at 1000 ft on 09-25 shares the exact same danger fingerprint as the
  final death: a stat/blow-reducing effect collapsing blows to 1 right at the moment of death, against
  a monster combo the character had no way to out-damage. This looks like a recurring, not one-off,
  failure mode.

## Leads
- The 1000 ft death on 2026-09-25 and what happened to the character between it and the 03:06 town
  respawn (hp max drops from 230 to 182, suggesting a rollback/respawn mechanic, not a fresh reroll)
  is outside this brief's scope but matters for the postmortem's root-cause story — worth a follow-up
  question to the Architect about MAngband's death/respawn/rollback behavior for bots.
  navigator.md line 13 independently mentions a "character rolled back" event after a different
  connection failure, suggesting this rollback mechanic is a known, recurring behavior worth its own
  note.
- events.jsonl (R3's scope) should be checked for the literal "Killed by poison" game message and any
  messages in the 30-60s before 22:28:39 to see exactly which attack(s) delivered the poison damage.
- The Navigator's real-time-tracking failures (both the mission-1/2 offset and the death-night
  narration-after-death) suggest the Navigator has no reliable access to real wall-clock time; this
  may be worth flagging to the Architect regardless of this study's other findings.
