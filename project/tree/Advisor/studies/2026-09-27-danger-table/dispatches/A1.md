## Answer
monster.txt's `I:` line is `speed:HPdiceDside:vision:AC:sleep` — HP is a dice string (e.g. "12d10"),
rolled with `damroll()` each spawn unless `FORCE_MAXHP` forces `maxroll()` (max HP every time)
[SEEN, init1.c:3037-3046, monster2.c:1913-1920]. Blow effects and spell/breath formulas are tabulated
below with exact resist divisors and saves, cited to melee1.c/melee2.c/spells1.c. The single biggest
surprise: MAngband 1.5's "smart" monster AI (spell avoidance based on known player resists,
`DRS_SMART_OPTIONS`) is **compiled out** (`src/options.h:437`, commented "--broken in mangband"), so
`RF2_SMART` only affects (a) a 50%-at-<10%-HP switch to escape/status spells, and (b) always-wakes-on-
light. Monsters never learn or "cheat" to avoid resisted spells in this codebase. Half-Orc Warrior
gets `RES_DARK` from race (p_race.txt:122) and Free-Action-independent fear immunity only at class
level 30 via `BRAVERY_30` (xtra1.c:662-665) — right at the top edge of the brief's clvl 1-30 range, so
fear is a live threat for nearly the whole run.

and breaths carry no monster-side to-hit roll** — they always connect if in range/LOS; only melee

## Flags legend
Parsing: `init1.c` case `'F'` calls `grab_one_basic_flag()` for each `|`-separated token
(init1.c:3130-3155); flags1-3 are "basic" (monster.txt `F:` line), flags4-6 are "spell" (monster.txt
`S:` line, parsed at init1.c:3157-3200 via `grab_one_spell_flag()`). `1_IN_X` sets
`freq_spell = freq_innate = 100/X` (both fields always equal; init1.c:3176-3191).

- **UNIQUE** (RF1, mdefines.h:2300): one-of-a-kind, no respawn once killed.
- **FORCE_DEPTH/FORCE_MAXHP/FORCE_SLEEP** (RF1:2308-2310): force native depth; force max HP roll
  (`maxroll` not `damroll`, monster2.c:1913-1920); **FORCE_SLEEP does NOT mean "starts asleep"** in this
  codebase — it gives the monster a much smaller starting energy pool
  (`randint0(level_speed(0)>>4)` vs normal `randint0(level_speed(0))`, monster2.c:1938-1946), explicitly
  commented "Reduce risk of instant death by breath weapons." A monster can still start awake with
  FORCE_SLEEP; it just can't act on its very first possible tick as easily. This differs from the usual
  vanilla-Angband meaning of the flag name.
- **FRIEND/FRIENDS/ESCORT/ESCORTS** (RF1:2312-2315): only **FRIENDS** and **ESCORT** are consumed by
  the spawn code (`place_monster_aux`, monster2.c:2139-2218); **FRIEND (singular) is never checked** in
  monster2.c — grep across src/server/*.c shows its only use is a lore/knowledge-flag notation in
  monster1.c:880, not a spawn trigger. Group spawning ("grp") is always passed TRUE at level generation
  (generate.c:1096,2438-2474), so FRIENDS always tries `place_monster_group`; ESCORT places up to 50
  escort monsters (loop breaks early on first failed pick), and ESCORTS makes each escort itself bring
  a group.
- **NEVER_BLOW / NEVER_MOVE** (RF1:2316-2317): NEVER_BLOW makes `make_attack_normal()` return FALSE
  immediately (melee1.c:191) — no melee attacks ever, though spells/breaths still work. NEVER_MOVE
  (not directly traced in this pass) is used in movement AI to skip normal movement.
- **RAND_25/RAND_50** (RF1:2318-2319): 25%/50%/75%(both) chance per move to pick a random direction
  instead of tracking the player (melee2.c:2739-2772).
- **ONLY_GOLD/ONLY_ITEM/DROP_60/DROP_90/DROP_1D2..4D2/DROP_GOOD/DROP_GREAT/DROP_USEFUL/DROP_CHOSEN**
  (RF1:2320-2331): loot-table only, not read further (object/ego out of scope per brief).
- **STUPID/SMART** (RF2:2336-2337): STUPID monsters rarely wake to light (10% vs 25% base,
  spells2.c:4335); SMART monsters always wake to light (spells2.c:4338) and, at HP<10%, have a 50%
  chance per spellcast to restrict their spell choice to the "INT_MASK" escape/status set — **RF5:
  HOLD|SLOW|CONF|BLIND|SCARE and RF6: BLINK|TPORT|TELE_LEVEL|TELE_AWAY|HEAL|HASTE|TRAPS|S_MONSTER(S)+**
  (mdefines.h:2582-2591; melee2.c:496-508). **The classic "smart_learn"/"smart_cheat" resist-avoidance
  system does not run** — gated behind `#define DRS_SMART_OPTIONS`, which is commented out
  (`src/options.h:437,559`) and the code says "--broken in mangband." All `int_outof`/
  `remove_bad_spells`/`update_smart_learn`-effect code inside `#ifdef DRS_SMART_OPTIONS` blocks
  (melee2.c:17-519, monster2.c:2825-2933) is not compiled. Calls to `update_smart_learn()` elsewhere in
  the live code are therefore no-ops when this macro is off.
- **INVISIBLE** (RF2:2340): needs see-invisible to detect (monster2.c:1409,1489).
- **COLD_BLOOD** (RF2:2341): not detected by infravision (monster1.c:672, monster2.c:1399,1488).
- **EMPTY_MIND/WEIRD_MIND** (RF2:2342-2343): "avoids telepathy" per source comment — not detected by
  ESP/ESP-type ability.
- **MULTIPLY** (RF2:2344, breeder): increments `num_repro`; new spawns only occur while
  `num_repro < MAX_REPRO` = **100** (mdefines.h:234, melee2.c:2688) — a very high per-level cap, so
  breeders (worm masses, lice) are capped late, not early.
- **REGENERATE** (RF2:2345): not traced this pass (out of budget), name is self-explanatory (faster HP
  regen).
- **POWERFUL** (RF2:2348): breath ball radius 3 instead of 2 (melee2.c:357).
- **PASS_WALL / KILL_WALL** (RF2:2354-2355): movement through/destruction of walls — not traced in
  detail (movement AI out of the melee1/melee2/spells1 core scope read this pass).
- **TAKE_ITEM/KILL_ITEM/OPEN_DOOR/BASH_DOOR/MOVE_BODY/KILL_BODY/WANDERER** (RF2:2352-2360): AI/movement
  interaction flags, self-explanatory, not traced.
- **ORC/TROLL/GIANT/DRAGON/DEMON/UNDEAD/EVIL/ANIMAL** (RF3:2372-2379): race/alignment flags used by
  slays (object ego code, out of scope) and by "protection from evil" in melee1.c:283-296 (evil
  monsters can be repelled: `(randint0(100)+lvl)>50` while `protevil>0` and player level >= monster
  level).
- **HURT_LITE** (RF3:2384): monster is hurt by being on a lit floor tile — mechanic lives in
  spells1.c:2939-2975 (light spells) — out of the specific files read for melee/spell-vs-player, but the
  flag name and lore text (monster1.c:697 "bright light") confirm meaning.
- **HURT_ROCK/HURT_FIRE/HURT_COLD** (RF3:2385-2387): monster takes bonus damage from those attack
  types (player-vs-monster; out of scope).
- **IM_ACID/IM_ELEC/IM_FIRE/IM_COLD/IM_POIS** (RF3:2388-2392), **RES_NETH/RES_WATE/RES_PLAS/RES_NEXU/
  RES_DISE** (RF3:2394-2398): monster resistances to the player's attacks — relevant only for how hard
  the monster is to kill, not to player danger; not traced further (in scope only "briefly" per brief).
- **NO_FEAR/NO_STUN/NO_CONF/NO_SLEEP** (RF3:2400-2403): monster immune to those player-inflicted
  statuses; not player-facing danger.

## Player (Half-Orc Warrior) defences
- p_race.txt Half-Orc entry (lines 115-123): `S:2:-1:0:0:1:-4` (stat mods +2 STR/-1 INT/+1 CON/-4 CHR);
  `R:-3:-3:-3:-1:0:7:12:-5` → **base save (sav) is -3**, i.e. Half-Orcs have a *worse* saving throw than
  human baseline, not better — this is a mild contradiction of any assumption that Half-Orc is a
  purely defensive pick; `X:10:110:3` → hit-die 10, exp penalty 110%, infravision range 3 (×10ft = 30ft);
  `F:RES_DARK` is the **only** intrinsic racial resist/sustain — confirms the brief's "already known"
  claim exactly, and there is no other racial resist, sustain, or free action.
- p_class.txt Warrior (lines 82-109): `F:BRAVERY_30 | PSEUDO_ID_HEAVY | PSEUDO_ID_IMPROV`. `BRAVERY_30`
  is defined at mdefines.h:2195 ("Gains resist fear at plev 30") and applied in xtra1.c:662-665:
  `if (p_ptr->lev >= 30) (*f2) |= TR2_RES_FEAR;` — confirmed exactly: fear resistance is a **hard
  level-30 gate**, no partial protection below that. Warriors have no other class-granted resist,
  sustain, Free Action, or See Invisible in `p_class.txt` (not read past the flag line, but no other
  `F:` flags are present in the Warrior block to grant them).
- **Net for a clvl 1-30 Half-Orc Warrior:** no Free Action, no See Invisible, no ESP, no ordinary
  resists besides dark, until items supply them; fear immunity only arrives at the very top of the
  brief's level range. This matches (and sharpens) the brief's "already known" note that Free Action
  becomes essential — the character has zero built-in protection from paralysis, poison, blindness,
  confusion, or fear for virtually the entire 0-1500ft/clvl1-30 span other than what equipment
  provides.

## Contradictions and surprises
1. **SMART monster resist-avoidance is dead code** in 1.5 (`DRS_SMART_OPTIONS` undefined,
   `src/options.h:437,559`, comment "broken in mangband"). Other Clerks/the Advisor should not assume
   monsters "know" or "learn" player resistances; SMART's only live effects are the low-HP
   escape-spell bias and always-wake-to-light. [SEEN]
2. **Non-elemental resists are weaker than "/3."** Sound/confusion/nether/chaos/disenchant/nexus/
   shards/light/dark use `dam*K/(d6+6)` (K=4,5,6), averaging 0.42x-0.63x, not the clean 1/3 (0.33x) that
   the classic elemental resists (acid/elec/fire/cold/poison, via `_dam()` functions) give. Only the
   five classic resists actually hit exactly `(dam+2)/3` and can stack with `oppose_*` to ~1/9. [SEEN]
3. **FRIEND (singular) flag appears to be non-functional** for actual group spawning in this codebase
   — only FRIENDS and ESCORT/ESCORTS drive `place_monster_group`/escort placement. If any monster.txt
   entries rely on bare `FRIEND` expecting a companion, that companion will not spawn via this path.
   [SEEN, absence confirmed by grep across monster2.c] — flag as a lead to check against actual
   monster.txt usage (which monsters use FRIEND vs FRIENDS).
4. **FORCE_SLEEP does not mean "starts asleep"** — it only shrinks starting energy to delay a monster's
   first action; a FORCE_SLEEP monster can still be awake on arrival. [SEEN, monster2.c:1938-1946]
5. **RF4_BR_MANA and RF5_BO_POIS are unimplemented no-ops** in melee2.c (both bodies are `/* XXX XXX
   XXX */; break;`) — any monster.txt entry with these flags will silently do nothing when that spell is
   picked (wasting the monster's turn), a genuine 1.5 gap worth flagging to the Advisor/Architect.
   [SEEN, melee2.c:876-881, 1272-1277]
6. **Monster bolts/breaths have no to-hit roll** — only melee blows use `check_hit()`/AC. This means
   AC does not protect against any ranged spell/breath damage at all (only melee), a distinction the
   danger table should track per-monster (dangerous ranged-only monsters aren't mitigated by armor).
   [SEEN, melee2.c:333-361 vs melee1.c:106-127]
7. Half-Orc's **-3 base saving throw** partly cuts against "already known" implied safety — a Half-Orc
   Warrior actually saves worse than baseline against all `skill_sav` checks (paralysis-without-FA,
   fear, blind, confuse, scare, hold, slow, mind blast/brain smash), somewhat undercutting the race's
   reputation, though RES_DARK and the STR/CON bonuses are unrelated compensations. [SEEN, p_race.txt:117]
