# Dispatch: out-of-depth threats, monster levels 31-40 (met at dl 25)

- Brief: rate all 122 monsters of native level 31-40 for danger to a dl21-30 Half-Orc Warrior
  (clvl18-28, HP180-300, FA+SI assumed) meeting them out of depth at dl25; give neutraliser,
  safe_when, recognition notes; cover pit/nest contents and recognition rules.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-27; sources covered: `scratch/B4_input.csv` (122 rows, read in full),
  `B_common.md`, `dispatches/A1.md`, `dispatches/A3.md` (all read in full), `data/monsters/monsters.csv`
  (617 rows, queried programmatically for every symbol+colour pair used in my band and for named
  checks below), `lib/edit/monster.txt` not re-read line-by-line (rates/dice already in the input CSV
  match A1's formulas; spot-checked a few oddities against `monsters.csv` only, per scope).

## Answer

Of 122 monsters (native level 31-40), **97 (79%) rate AVOID or LEAVE** (danger 3-5); only 3 rate
CAREFUL-adjacent low risk and 1 (Nexus quylthulg) is genuinely harmless. **24 rate 5 (flee on
sight)** - dragons/hydras with breath approaching cap, a few raw-melee monsters (Colossus, Giant
roc, Vrock) that no resist mitigates, and casters that summon reinforcements near their own level
36-40. The single biggest amplifier I found that the brief didn't mention: **44/122 (36%) have
speed >=120** (2-4x actions per player turn - tables.c:2169 formula from A1), which roughly doubles
or quadruples melee/spell throughput versus a naive one-action read of the stat block. With FA+SI
assumed (per B_common's dl21-30 doctrine), paralysis/hold/invisibility threats are mostly
neutralised; the residual danger is almost entirely raw damage (melee, breath, bolt/ball, and
save-or-nothing CAUSE_x/MIND_BLAST/BRAIN_SMASH) plus **summon spells (17 monsters)**, which can
call reinforcements at `(Depth+lev)/2+5` - i.e. close to the summoner's own high level (A3
finding 13), turning a single encounter into several.

## Pit and nest contents at dl 21-30

Per A3: nests draw `get_mon_num(Depth+10)` themed jelly if `randint1(Depth)<30`; at dl21-29 that
condition is true 100% of the time (max roll 29), so **every nest at dl21-30 is jelly-themed**
(animal only at dl30 exactly, 1/30 chance). Pits draw the same way, themed orc if `<20`, troll if
`<40` - at dl21-30, troll pits occur roughly 4-40% of rolls depending on exact depth, orc the rest.

- **Jelly-nest candidates from my band** (drawn at Depth+10=31-40): **Acidic cytoplasm** (lvl35,
  ACID touch, IM everything but acid) and **Black pudding** (lvl37, ACID touch, FRIENDS - it
  literally is a jelly with its own pack flag). Both AVOID(3). No other jelly exists at level
  31-40 in the whole game (`monsters.csv` full jelly list checked: after Black ooze at lvl23 the
  next is Acidic cytoplasm at 35, then Black pudding at 37 - a real level gap).
- **Troll-pit candidates from my band**: Cave troll(33), Half-troll(34), Skeleton ettin(33,
  TROLL|UNDEAD), Water troll(35), Olog(36), Mummified troll(37), Eldrak(37), Ettin(38), Spirit
  troll(40), Troll chieftain(40), plus uniques Bert/Bill/Tom the Stone Troll(34) if uniques are
  permitted in pit fill (not verified - A3 didn't check this specifically; flagged as a lead).
- **Orc-pit candidates: none.** Full scan of `monsters.csv`'s ORC flag shows the highest-level orc
  in the game is Azog (level23). An orc pit rolled at dl21-30 (drawing at Depth+10=31-40) can only
  ever produce orcs up to level23 - it cannot inject anything from my band. This means the "mostly
  orc, some troll" split A3 described is, in practice, "mostly harmless-relative-to-depth orcs,
  occasionally a genuine level31-40 troll" - the troll roll is the one that actually matters for
  danger at dl21-30.

  A3's finding; Stone/Cloud/Storm giants and Lokkak reach dl25 only via the nasty-monster boost,
  vaults, or summons, not pits.

## Recognition rules

- **Capital `D` (any colour) = always an ancient dragon in this game.** Checked all four colours
  used in my band (b, U, w, g) against `monsters.csv` levels 0-30: **zero matches**. No shallow
  monster anywhere uses symbol `D`. This is the single cleanest, color-independent OOD tell in the
  whole band - if the bot ever sees `D`, leave immediately, no further check needed.
- **Lower-case `d` is not reliable** - it spans Baby (lvl9), Young (lvl29-31), and Mature
  (lvl33-38) dragons at the *same* glyph+colour (e.g. `d,s` = Baby black(9) / Young black(31) /
  Mature black(36); `d,w` = Baby/Young/Mature white; `d,g`= Baby/Young green(9/29) **and Wyvern
  (lvl22, not even a true dragon, poison sting)**; `d,v`/`d,B` also cover ATTR_MULTI monsters whose
  displayed colour cycles constantly, so colour is meaningless for them). Recommendation: treat any
  `d` as "unknown severity, could be trivial or lethal" rather than reading colour as a danger
  proxy.
- **`p` (humanoid caster) at colour `R` is a trap**: Illusionist (lvl13, weak) shares `p,R` with
  **four** level36-40 casters in my band (Necromancer, Demonologist, Enchantress, Sorcerer) that
  summon and bolt for ~50-100 damage. Colour `U` similarly spans the "Sons of the Easterling
  chief" NPC family from level9 (Brodda) to level28 (Uldor) plus my band's unique Ulfang(34) -
  six shallow near-namesakes under one glyph+colour.
  Colour `b` spans rogues (Novice rogue lvl2 through Master rogue lvl23) and my band's Master
  thief(34) - same combo, wildly different danger.
- **`s,w` (skeleton) is shared by four native shallow skeletons (kobold5/orc8/human12/troll30)** and
  my band's Skeleton ettin(33) - genuinely hard to tell apart, though this particular OOD entry is
  low-danger (rating 2) so the false-negative risk is low here.
- **`.` (floor-mimic ambusher)**: my band's Trapper(36, PARALYZE, invisible+CHAR_CLEAR/ATTR_CLEAR)
  shares `.,w` with the native **Lurker** (lvl14, also NEVER_MOVE, also ambushes). Both are "don't
  step near suspicious floor" cases regardless of which one it is.
- **`Z,u`**: Impact hound(35) vs the much weaker native Earth hound(20) - both hounds, both
  breathe, but severity differs a lot; treat any `Z` as pack-hunting and dangerous by default.
- **`~` (mimic)**: Chest mimic(35) has no glyph/colour tell at all (it displays as an ordinary
  item) - the only rule is behavioural: any unidentified object that moves or attacks is a mimic.
- **`j` (ooze/jelly)**: Black pudding(37, `j,D`) has no shallow collision, but Acidic
  cytoplasm(35, `j,s`) likewise has none - jelly glyph/colour combos in this band are clean, unlike
  most others.
- **19 of 25 checked glyph+colour pairs in my band have zero shallow (dl0-30) collision** - for
  those, the bot can trust glyph+colour alone as an OOD signal. The failures cluster specifically
  around `d` (dragons/wyvern), `p` (humanoid casters/rogues/named NPCs), `s,w` (skeletons), `Z,u`
  (hounds), `.,w` (ambushers), `w,v` (worms), `j,D`... (see full per-row `notes` column in the CSV
  for every pair checked).

## Contradictions and surprises

1. "Already known" lists "draconic Q's" as depth-appropriate killers; the only Q-symbol monster in
   this level band is harmless (Finding 10) - likely refers to a monster outside levels 31-40, not
   confirmed here.
2. "Elder aranea" (forum) is a real, separate level48 entry distinguishable only by lowercase `r`
   vs my band's uppercase-`R` "Aranea"(38) - the forum reputation likely belongs to the deeper
   monster, not this one (Finding 11).
3. A3's OOD table implies orc pits matter at dl21-30; in practice they cannot exceed level23
   (Azog) content because no higher-level orc exists (Finding 8) - troll pits are what actually
   carries this band's danger.
4. Two monster.txt entries share the literal name "Carrion crawler" at different levels/pack
   status (Finding 9) - a name-based or glyph-based recognition rule cannot distinguish them.
5. Speed (2-4x actions at 120-140) is a bigger amplifier than raw dice suggest and isn't mentioned
   in B_common's mechanics list; 36% of this band has it (Finding 6).
