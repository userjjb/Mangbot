- Brief: list the forum's named monster dangers with their claims, check each against 1.5
  `monster.txt`/`monsters.csv`, flag contradictions, and tally YASD kills ≤1500 ft.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-27; sources covered: `memos/2026-09-26-forum-distillation.md` (full, incl. all
  addenda/erratum), `memos/2026-09-26-forum/{yasd,strategy,king_newplayer,live_batch2_yasd,

  monster names), `data/forum/corpus/yasd.txt` (grepped for death-count proxies),

  blow/spell/flag legend.

## Answer
Most forum-named dangers are real 1.5 monsters, present, and roughly the right *kind* of threat
(paralyzers, summoners, breathers), but several forum-cited **depths are far shallower than the
monster's native level** in 1.5 — consistent with vault/pit out-of-depth spawns, not with the monster
being a common shallow encounter. The single most important correction: the "already known" hound
depth table (earth ~900, water/energy ~1000, air ~1550) is **wrong for 3 of 4 named types** — in 1.5,
earth, air and water hounds are *all* native level 20 (1000 ft), and energy hound is level 18 (900 ft);
air hounds are not native to 1550 ft at all. Paralysis-monster claims (carrion crawler, ghoul, ogre
mage) line up almost exactly with the forum's 1250 ft Free Action checkpoint. Gorlim, Drolem, Elder
aranea, Mughash and Acidic cytoplasm are all real but their forum-cited encounter depths are
15–37 levels shallower than their 1.5 native level — every one of these is a vault/pit anomaly, not a
routine encounter, and Gorlim/Drolem/Elder aranea are native to level 41–48 (2050–2400 ft), **outside**
the brief's level-40 cutoff.

## Findings
1. **The hound depth table in "already known" is wrong for earth/air/water/energy.** 1.5 data: Earth
   hound lvl 20 (1000 ft, `u` brown), Air hound lvl 20 (1000 ft, `g` green), Water hound lvl 20 (1000 ft,
   `s` grey), Energy hound lvl 18 (900 ft, `b` blue) — all four are `FORCE_SLEEP|FRIENDS` pack breathers
   with no melee blows. Gravity hound is lvl 35 (1750 ft), close to the claimed 1800–1900. [SEEN,
   monsters.csv rows src_line 3014/3031/3061/2798/4901] Air hounds specifically are **not** native to
   1550 ft; that depth belongs to Mystic (lvl 33, 1650 ft) or is simply an out-of-depth sighting.
2. **Paralysis checkpoint confirmed almost exactly.** Carrion crawler lvl 25 (1250 ft, `STING:PARALYZE`
   ×2), Ghoul lvl 26 (1300 ft, `CLAW:PARALYZE` ×2), Ogre mage lvl 27 (1350 ft, spell `HOLD`) — this
   matches the forum's "first dangerous paralyzers... at 1250 ft" almost to the foot. [SEEN, src_line
   3615/9012/3819]
3. **Mystic stun mechanics confirmed in exact numbers.** Grand Master Mystic's blows are
   `KICK:HURT:20d2 | KICK:HURT:10d2 | HIT:POISON:20d1 | HIT:PARALYZE:15d1` — the forum's "20d1/15d1
   blows always roll max damage" is literally true (a d1 die has only one face). `KICK`/`HIT` methods
   apply stun regardless of resist (per A1's legend), so "melee stun is unresistable" is confirmed by
   the blow *method*, not by a spell effect a resist could block. Mystic spells include `S_ANIMAL` —
   since hounds carry the `ANIMAL` flag, "Mystics summon hounds" is mechanically plausible. [SEEN,
   src_line 4433/6895/7102]
4. **Gorlim's native level is 41 (2050 ft), just past the brief's level-40 cutoff**, yet the forum cites
   it at 550, 650, 1800, 2050 and 2100 ft — the two shallowest citations are 28–30 levels out of depth.
   Its spell list (`CAUSE_3|BO_WATE|BO_MANA`) exactly matches the forum's "water bolts stun and
   confuse" and "casts a mana bolt... you die" claims (BO_WATE stuns+confuses per A1; BO_MANA has no
   resist). [SEEN, src_line 6362] Its shallow sightings are unexplained by depth alone (no cited vault
   this time) — a genuine open question, see Contradictions.
5. **Drolem, Elder aranea, Acidic cytoplasm and Mughash are all real but the forum's cited encounter
   depths are far shallower than their 1.5 native level**, in every case attributable to a pit/vault:
   - Drolem: native lvl 44 (2200 ft), `EMPTY_MIND` (confirms "invisible to ESP" exactly), `BR_POIS`;
     forum cites 800–1000 ft repeatedly (24 levels OOD) via a "nature pit" (general_2.md, t=1218).
   - Elder aranea: native lvl 48 (2400 ft); forum death at 600 ft ("approached a U + escort", i.e. a
     spider vault, live_batch1.md) — 36 levels OOD.
   - Acidic cytoplasm: native lvl 35 (1750 ft), `TOUCH:ACID`×4 (confirms "damages every armour slot");
     forum cites ~800–1000 ft, again the same "nature pit" — 15–19 levels OOD.
   - Mughash: native lvl 7 (350 ft), UNIQUE, `ESCORT|ESCORTS`, no spells at all; forum cites a
     "confusion lock" at 950 ft with kobold shamans (live_batch1.md p=6806). Mughash himself has no
     confuse spell — the confusion must come from an escort (Kobold shaman, lvl 3, 150 ft, spell
     `CONF`) or from a heavily out-of-depth spawn. [SEEN, src_line 1309/9090/6700/9072/4918]
6. **Summoners in the "already known" list are all real and carry believable summon spells.** Silent
   Watcher (lvl 35, 1750 ft): stationary (`NEVER_MOVE`), gaze-only attacks (`GAZE:PARALYZE/TERRIFY/
   LOSE_STR/HALLU`, zero blow dice), spells `SHRIEK|S_MONSTER|S_MONSTERS|HOLD|CONF|MIND_BLAST`. Death
   Knight (lvl 38, 1900 ft): `S_MONSTERS` (summons up to 8), `BO_NETH`, `SMART`. Base Quylthulg (lvl 20,
   **1000 ft, in band**): `NEVER_MOVE|NEVER_BLOW|INVISIBLE`, spell `S_MONSTER`. Draconic quylthulg (lvl
   45, 2250 ft): `S_DRAGON` (confirms "summons dragons"). Scroll mimic (lvl 21, 1050 ft): spell
   `S_MONSTER`, matches "The Scroll mimic magically summons help!" verbatim. Druid (lvl 13, 650 ft):
   `S_ANIMAL` (could pull hounds, which are `ANIMAL`), close to the forum's "druid summoned earth
   hounds (900 ft)" (5 levels OOD). [SEEN, src_line 8985/5576/3093/7361/3201/(druid entry)]
7. **Molds and jellies match their forum reputations exactly.** Brown mold (lvl 6, 300 ft):
   `SPORE:CONFUSE` — confirms "brown mold confuses". Death mold (lvl 47, 2350 ft — deeper than 40):
   `SPORE:UN_BONUS` (disenchant) + `EXP_80`, confirming "disenchants gear" precisely, though at a native
   depth far below any shallow sighting. Ochre jelly (lvl 13, 650 ft, speed 120 vs the player's 110):
   confirms "moves faster than you do. TELEPORT!" exactly. [SEEN, src_line 1107/6838/2204]
8. **The unique-uncle cluster (Grip, Fang, Bullroarer, Orfax, Grishnákh, Wormtongue, Azog, Shagrat) all
   sit inside 0–1500 ft as claimed**, and one depth matches with unusual precision: Orfax's native level
   is 10 (exactly 500 ft), matching the forum's "Orfax (500 ft)" citation to the foot, and its spell list
   includes `CONF`, matching "something in the clump confused the level 13 dwarf warrior." Shagrat's
   native level 19 (950 ft) is much closer to the "1000 ft+" citation (yasd.md #44) than to the "550 ft"
   one (yasd.md #35), suggesting the 550 ft sighting was itself an anomaly (vault, or a different
   Shagrat report entirely). [SEEN, src_line 587/599/926/1665/1708/1324/3339/2858]
9. **"5/8 heroes killed by a Novice paladin" does not hold up against the 1.5 stat block.** Novice
   paladin is lvl 4 (200 ft), 27 avg HP, two 1d7 blows (max 14), spell `CAUSE_1` (3d8) and `SCARE` — not
   remotely capable of killing a level 25–29 character. yasd.md itself flags the "they gain XP"
   explanation as a joke; the underlying kill-count claim is either a database/naming artefact (many
   monsters share the "Novice paladin" name across colours/depths — the CSV has two identical entries
   at src_line 860 and 1416) or a mislabel of a much higher-level threat. Verdict: **the mechanism is
   unverifiable from the stat block; treat the kill count as folklore, not data.** [SEEN, src_line
   860/1416; INFERRED for the "why"]
10. **Every hound family the memo names is `FRIENDS` (pack-spawning) and `FORCE_SLEEP`** (starts with
    reduced energy, not necessarily asleep — per A1's correction of that flag's meaning), so "a pack of
    2-3 on arrival" is the expected spawn shape, not bad luck. [SEEN, monsters.csv hound rows]

## Contradictions and surprises
1. **Biggest correction:** the brief's "already known" hound depth table (earth ~900, water/energy
   ~1000, air ~1550) is contradicted by 1.5 data for 3 of 4 types — see Findings #1 and the claims
   table. This should be corrected before the danger table is finalised.
2. **Gorlim, Drolem, Elder aranea are native to level 41-48 (2050-2400 ft)**, all above the brief's
   level-40 cutoff, yet the forum repeatedly cites them at 550-1000 ft (25-36 levels OOD). Two of the
   three OOD cases have an explicit vault/pit cause on record (nature pit for Drolem/cytoplasm, spider
   vault for aranea); Gorlim's shallow citations (550, 650, 1800 ft) have **no stated cause** in the
   sources I read — worth flagging to the Advisor as an open question rather than assuming vault OOD.
3. **Mughash has no spells at all in 1.5** — the "confusion lock" the forum attributes to him must come
   from an escort (kobold shaman has `CONF`) or reflects a much older monster.txt where Mughash himself
   cast spells. Not checked against pre-1.5 monster.txt (out of scope).
4. **The "5/8 heroes killed by a Novice paladin" claim is not supported by the stat block** (Finding #9).
   yasd.md itself hedges this as likely a joke; I'm marking it PARTLY rather than CONTRADICTED because
   I can't rule out a naming/database artefact I haven't traced.
5. Against the brief's "already known": all other named monsters (Grip, Fang, Bullroarer, Orfax,
   Grishnákh, Wormtongue, Azog, carrion crawlers, ghouls, ogre mages, molds, jellies) hold up well, with
   Orfax and the carrion-crawler/ghoul/ogre-mage paralysis cluster matching to within one dungeon level.
