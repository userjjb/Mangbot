# Dispatch: B3 — threat profile and response, dungeon levels 21-30 (93 monsters, 1050-1500 ft)
- Brief: rate every monster.txt entry in scratch/B3_input.csv for danger to a Half-Orc Warrior bot at
  the dl21-30 assumed state (clvl 18-28, HP 180-300, save 33-43%, FA+SI by doctrine, resists maybe),
  give response/neutraliser/safe_when, per B_common.md's scale and output format.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-27; sources covered: scratch/B3_input.csv (all 93 rows), dispatches/A1.md (legend,
  read in full), raw monster.txt entries for ~14 spot-checked rows (Basilisk, Spectator, Mind flayer,
  Mim, 5-headed hydra, Beorn, Azog, Wolf chieftain, Ogre chieftain, Giant firefly, Disenchanter bat,
  and their neighbours), src/server/melee2.c (ARROW_1-4 formulas, RBE_HALLU case, for two flags A1
  hadn't pinned down).

## Answer
Of 93 monsters (dl21-30): 23 rate 1 (easy), 25 rate 2 (care), 33 rate 3 (dangerous), 12 rate 4 (lethal),
0 rate 5. No monster in this band earns instant "leave the level" on sight alone; the 12 rate-4 entries
(2 fast uniques, 1 non-unique pack leader, 2 hound packs, 1 poison-pack bat, 1 ogre-chieftain pack, 5
non-pack lethal singles) get response AVOID/LEAVE rather than flat FIGHT. The character's assumed FA+SI
by dl20 quietly defangs 10 paralyzers and 4 invisible monsters down from what would otherwise be 4-5
ratings — this is the single biggest driver of the table (see Findings #1). Full ratings are in
`scratch/B3_table.csv`.

## Findings
1. **Free Action (assumed present per doctrine) is doing most of the work in this band.** 10 of 93
   monsters carry a PARALYZE blow or HOLD spell (Carrion crawler, Ogre mage, Vampire, Gorgimaera, Mind
   flayer, Basilisk, Ogre shaman, Spectator, Ghoul, Ghast) [SEEN, B3_input.csv blow/spell columns].
   With FA assumed, these rate only 2-3; without it, per B_common's own worked example ("2+ PARALYZE
   blows... chain-paralyse... to death"), several (Ghoul/Ghast packs especially) would jump to 4-5. I
   flagged `safe_when` as "with FA" throughout so the Advisor can re-derive the no-FA case if doctrine
   is wrong for some players.
2. **Mind flayer's BRAIN_SMASH is NOT fully solved by Free Action** [SEEN, A1.md's spell table + confirmed
   raw entry monster.txt:3985-4000, `S:BLIND | HOLD | SCARE | MIND_BLAST | BRAIN_SMASH | FORGET`]. Per
   A1: on a failed save, BRAIN_SMASH lands blind+confuse+paralyze+slow *together*; FA blocks only the
   paralyze component. A character with FA but no resist_blind/resist_conf still gets blinded, confused,
   and slowed on the ~57-67% of casts that beat the character's 33-43% save. Rated 4 (AVOID); this is the
   most important correction to a "just get Free Action" mental model in the whole band.
3. **Poison-type melee blows bypass resist_pois entirely for the direct damage** [SEEN, A1.md: "resist_pois
   /oppose_pois block the status only, not the hp damage"]. 5-headed hydra (4x POISON 4d4, ~40/turn at
   normal speed, doubled to ~80/turn since it's +10 speed) is the clearest example: rPois stops the
   poison *status* but not one point of the base 4d4 dice. Rated 4 (LEAVE) — a resist-only mitigation
   plan fails here. Algroth, Wyvern, Giant yellow scorpion, Creeping adamantite coins, Bat of Gorgoroth,
   and the young green dragon's melee-adjacent poison contact all share this trap at smaller scale.
4. **17 of 93 monsters carry FRIENDS/ESCORT/ESCORTS** (pack spawning): Lugdush, Ugluk, Azog, Stone troll,
   Cave ogre, Algroth, Vibration hound, Nexus hound, Ice troll, Giant army ant, Ghoul, Ghast, Bat of
   Gorgoroth, Wolf chieftain, Ogre chieftain, Draebor, Beorn [SEEN, flags column]. Packs are the single
   biggest feature driving 3-4 ratings after paralysis/FA: hound packs (Vibration/Nexus, #294/295) and
   the wolf/ogre chieftain packs (#607/610) are rated 4 specifically because per-monster damage that's
   individually only "care" multiplies across an unknown pack size that the bot can't control.
5. **16 monsters carry a blind or confuse effect** (melee BLIND/CONFUSE or spell); of note, **melee
   BLIND and melee CONFUSE blows have no saving-throw alternative** — only resist_blind/resist_conf
   fully blocks them [SEEN, A1.md blow-effects table]. Catoblepas (guaranteed blind gaze), Giant brown
   tick (guaranteed blind sting), Giant firefly (guaranteed blind bite), and Spectator's confuse gaze all
   land every time they connect if the character lacks the specific resist — Free Action does not help
   at all here (contrast with paralysis).
6. **12 uniques in this band**, confirmed by name against "already known": Lugdush (dl21), Ugluk (dl22),
   Azog (dl23), Sangahyando and Angamaite (both dl24), plus Ibun/Khim (Son of Mim twins, dl24), Ulwarth
   (dl26), Mim (dl27, their father), Uldor (dl28), Draebor (dl28), Beorn (dl28) [SEEN, unique column].
   Shagrat/Gorbag/Bolg/Gorlim from the brief's "already known" list are **not present in this band** —
   consistent with them being shallower uniques for a different Clerk's input, not a contradiction.
7. **2 breeders** (MULTIPLY): Black ooze (dl23, slow speed90, weak — breeder tag barely matters) and
   Giant firefly (dl24, tiny HP but a guaranteed-blind bite and speed+10) [SEEN, flags column].
8. **8 summoners**: Scroll mimic, Dark elven druid (S_MONSTER|S_SPIDER|S_ANIMAL — three different pack
   types), Ogre mage, Ogre shaman, Uldor (S_KIN), Mage (S_MONSTER, 33% cast freq), Draebor (S_KIN), Ring
   mimic (S_MONSTER) [SEEN, spells column]. None of these get rated 5 individually but several sit at 3
   because the summon adds an unpredictable reinforcement risk on top of otherwise-manageable stats.
9. **Every rate-4 monster and why** (12 total): Azog (fast unique +10 spd, ~90 effective dmg/turn,
   ESCORT+ESCORTS pack) — Mim (fast unique, HP1100, immune to all 5 elements, ~81 effective dmg/turn +
   acid ball) — Vibration hound and Nexus hound (FRIENDS packs, ~30/turn melee EACH plus a partially-
   resistible breath EACH) — 5-headed hydra (poison-bypass melee ~80/turn effective at +10 speed) — Mind
   flayer (BRAIN_SMASH bypasses FA's protection, see #2) — Draebor (invisible fast unique, TELE_TO/
   TELE_AWAY/TELE_LEVEL can scatter the character away from stairs, plus S_KIN summon) — Basilisk (BR_POIS
   ~103-200 dmg unresisted from a fast, high-AC monster) — Beorn (fast unique, HP1400 — the highest HP
   and highest raw dps in the whole band, ~101 effective dmg/turn, pure melee no status) — Bat of
   Gorgoroth (FRIENDS pack of fast breathers, poison+dark breath each) — Wolf chieftain (non-unique but
   leads a full wolf pack via ESCORTS+ESCORT, fast, fear wail) — Ogre chieftain (ESCORT pack, fast,
   ~108 effective dmg/turn, the hardest-hitting plain melee monster in the band).
10. **Named-lore checks against "already known"**: "ogre mages paralyse" — **confirmed** [SEEN,
    monster.txt: Ogre mage carries `S:HEAL | HOLD | TRAPS | BA_COLD | S_MONSTER`]; Ogre shaman (dl27,
    same family) also carries HOLD, doubling the confirmation. "Air hounds ~1550 ft", "Mystics",
    "elder aranea", "acidic cytoplasm", "drolems" from the brief's already-known list are **not present
    in B3's 93 rows** — they are out of this band's depth/scope, consistent with the brief's own framing
    that hounds/drolems get deeper and Mystics etc. are presumably in a different Clerk's assignment.
11. **Surprises — monster far nastier or weaker than its name suggests**: Killer slicer beetle (dl30)
    hits ~45/turn, harder than several of its "Killer X beetle" siblings that do 10-21/turn — a plain
    beetle name hiding real melee output. Conversely, Banshee (dl24) is nearly harmless: both its blows
    (WAIL:TERRIFY, TOUCH:EXP_20) carry **zero damage dice**, so despite INVISIBLE+PASS_WALL it deals no
    direct HP damage at all. Purple worm (dl29, HP520) and Wereworm (dl25, HP1100!) are giant-HP "boss"
    monsters that only output ~14-18 damage/turn — tough walls, not real threats. Iron golem (dl22,
    HP960) is the same pattern: huge HP, one weak blow. Mim (dl27) is far more dangerous than his sons
    Ibun/Khim despite being their father in the same family — faster, immune to all five elements, and
    hits ~2.5x harder per effective turn.

## Contradictions and surprises
- Melee elemental blow effects (COLD/FIRE/ELEC/ACID) are **only reduced by the matching player resist,
  never by AC** [SEEN, A1.md: only HURT and SHATTER are AC-reduced]. This means Frost giant (dl28) and
  Fire giant (dl30) hit harder in practice than the plain Hill giant (dl25) despite similar dice, because
  the character's AC (which the assumed-state table lists as the main defence at this depth) does
  nothing against them. Worth flagging since the brief's assumed-state table emphasises AC growth by
  depth but that growth is irrelevant to ~30 of 93 monsters' blows.
- resist_pois blocking only the poison *status*, not the base blow damage, directly undercuts a naive
  reading of "get rPois and poison monsters are safe" for this specific band's poison-blow monsters
  (5-headed hydra especially) — see Finding #3.
- Mind flayer's BRAIN_SMASH partially defeating Free Action (Finding #2) is the clearest internal
  surprise versus B_common's framing of FA as the universal paralysis fix — it is the fix for paralysis
  specifically, not for the whole spell.
- No monster in this 93-row band rated a 5 ("flee on sight"). The nastiest entries (Beorn, Mim, Azog,
  Mind flayer, Draebor, the two hound packs, the wolf/ogre chieftains) all rated 4, which under the
  scale still allows AVOID/careful engagement rather than mandatory immediate level-leave. If the
  Advisor's synthesis expects at least one 5 per band, that expectation doesn't hold here — worth a
  cross-check against other Clerks' bands.

## Leads
- Whether Sangahyando/Angamaite and Ibun/Khim actually co-spawn as pairs (as their near-identical stat
  blocks and shared "Son of X"/"of Umbar" naming suggest) is a spawn-code question, out of this Clerk's
  scope (monster.txt stats only) — worth checking `generate.c`/unique-placement code if pairing changes
  the effective danger (fighting both at once roughly doubles melee output for each).
- Actual FRIENDS/ESCORT group sizes at dl21-30 (how many hounds/wolves/ogres actually spawn together) are
  not derivable from monster.txt alone; A1 mentioned `place_monster_group`/ESCORT loops without listing
  per-level group-size formulas. This matters most for the two hound packs and Wolf/Ogre chieftain,
  which I rated 4 partly on "pack size is uncontrolled" — a concrete group-size number would sharpen or
  soften those ratings.
- ARROW_4 (Manticore) and RBE_HALLU were not in A1's tables; I checked them directly in melee2.c/melee1.c
  (ARROW_4 = `bolt(GF_ARROW, damroll(7,6))`, no resist mentioned in the code path I read; RBE_HALLU is
  blocked by resist_chaos, melee1.c:1210-1220) — no monster in my band uses HALLU, so this is FYI for
  other Clerks.
- The README's alertness/"sleep" field description ("255 = ignores you") is technically about how large
  a starting sleep-counter roll a monster gets (`(val*2)+randint1(val*10)`, monster2.c:1980-1981), not
  literal permanent ignoring — Angel (alertness 255) can still wake normally once disturbed. Low-priority
  clarification for whoever writes the handbook text.
