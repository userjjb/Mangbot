# Dispatch: B2 — danger table, dungeon levels 11–20 (99 monsters, 550–1000 ft)
- Brief: rate every monster in scratch/B2_input.csv for danger/response/neutraliser to a Half-Orc
  Warrior at the assumed state for its native depth, per B_common.md's scale and format.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-27; sources covered: scratch/B2_input.csv (all 99 rows), dispatches/A1.md (legend,
  read in full), monster.txt raw entries for rows that looked odd (spot-checked src_lines 2098-2100,
  2571, 2204, 2858-2874, 3061-3078 in `/projectnb/jbrcs/mangband/github/lib/edit/monster.txt` — all
  matched the parsed CSV, no discrepancies found).

## Answer
19 of 99 monsters (19%) rate 4-lethal, 30 rate 3-dangerous, 35 rate 2-care, 15 rate 1-easy; none reach
0 or 5. The dominant lethal driver in this band is raw ESCORT/pack melee output from orc-type uniques
and captains (6 of the 19 fours), followed by paralysis-without-FA (2), unresisted big single-hit
spells/breaths (Chimaera's fire breath, Druid's BO_FIRE, Dark elven lord's BO_FIRE/BO_COLD, Paladin's
CAUSE_2, Nar's dual CAUSE_2+MIND_BLAST), and dl20 hound packs (earth/air/water, 3 of the 19). Free
Action and See Invisible remain the two highest-value items for this band, exactly as "already known"
predicts — but two contradictions of the depth claims for hounds turned up (below).

## Contradictions and surprises
1. **Hound appearance depths contradict the brief's "already known."** Brief says "hounds (Z) appear:
   earth ~900 ft, water and energy ~1000 ft." Actual data from monster.txt (via the parsed CSV,
   src_lines 3014/3031/3061): **Earth hound is level 20 (1000 ft)**, not ~900; **Energy hound is level
   18 (900 ft)**, not ~1000; **Water hound is level 20 (1000 ft)**, matching the claim. Fire and Cold
   hounds are also level 18 (900 ft), grouped with Energy hound. Light, Dark, and Clear hounds are level
   15 (750 ft), a tier the "already known" note doesn't mention at all. [SEEN, B2_input.csv rows for
   idx 193/194/203/221/222/223/238/239/241]
2. **Water/Air/Earth hound names don't match their breath elements**: Water hound breathes BR_ACID
   (not water), Air hound breathes BR_POIS (not air/wind), Earth hound breathes BR_SHAR (shards, not
   earth). Only Fire hound/Cold hound/Energy hound/Light hound/Dark hound match their names. [SEEN,
   B2_input.csv `spells` column]
3. **"Molds and jellies are traps only if meleed"** — true for 4 of 6 ooze-type monsters in this band
   (Grape jelly, Spotted jelly, Red mold, Magic mushroom patch, all NEVER_MOVE) but **false** for Ochre
   jelly and Gelatinous cube, which lack NEVER_MOVE and actively pursue. [SEEN, flags column]
4. **A stationary (NEVER_MOVE) monster can still force melee contact:** Evil eye has TELE_TO, which can
   pull the character next to it even if the character never approaches — breaking the general
   "NEVER_MOVE = only a risk if you choose to melee it" heuristic used elsewhere in this dispatch.
5. Golfimbul carries no active summon spell despite being a "chief" with an ESCORT — worth noting since
   I initially assumed (before checking) that all named orc-chief uniques summon; only Boldor (S_KIN)
   and the caster-type monsters actually cast summon spells in this band.
6. Melee CONFUSE (Umber hulk's gaze, Hummerhorn's bite) has **no saving throw fallback** per A1's blow
   table (only `resist_conf` blocks it), unlike spell-based CONF which always allows a skill_sav
   fallback — this makes these two more dangerous than their damage numbers alone suggest, and is worth
   flagging to the Architect as a distinct "no-save confusion" hazard class.

  input at all — they must fall in another band's depth range (likely B1, dl1-10, or possibly deeper);
  the Advisor should confirm which Clerk's input actually contains them.
- I did not trace `do_dec_stat()`/sustain-stat mechanics in detail (out of scope, flagged by A1); several
  monsters in this band (Moaning spirit, Giant red scorpion) have permanent stat-drain touches whose
  exact recovery cost (potion of restore vs. leveling) isn't covered here.
- HALLU (hallucination) resist wasn't confirmed by A1; Magic mushroom patch's SPORE:HALLU blows are
  rated as a minor nuisance on that basis — worth double-checking if hallucination turns out to have
  serious mechanical effects (e.g., miscast items) elsewhere in the codebase.
- I did not verify PASS_WALL/KILL_WALL movement AI in the primary source (A1 flagged this as untraced);
  Earth spirit, Moaning spirit, and Umber hulk's "attacks through walls" claim rests on the flag name
  and A1's partial note, not a direct code read.
