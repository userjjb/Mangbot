## Answer
The server has one clock: `dungeon()` runs once per tick at FPS=75 and increments `turn.turn` (dungeon.c:2308). Everything is derived from `level_speed(depth)=5*level_speeds[depth]`. A player acts when `energy >= level_speed`. Energy gain is `extract_energy[speed]*time_factor/100` per tick. All timeouts (light, food, regen, potions, recall) tick once per block of `time=int(int(level_speed/1000)/(tf/100))` ticks. Normal speed gives 1000 energy per tick, so a move takes about level_speed/1000 ticks (0.49 s in town, 0.60 s at DL1, 1.0 s at DL60, 1.33 s at DL85+). The biggest practical levers are the time-factor rules: resting with no awake monster in LOS runs the world 10x faster, running 5x, and low HP slows it to 0.2x. Odds: traps are uniform over 16 types with no depth dependence. Disarm, lock-pick and chest odds are all `max(2, skill - power)%`. Device use succeeds with P=(c-2)/c.

## Findings
1. **FPS=75** [SEEN] (`testserver/mangband.cfg:67`; `github/mangband.cfg:67`). The code default is 12 (`variable.c:182`) and `mdefines.h:46` has `FPS 12`; the cfg parse overrides it (`init2.c:2118-2120`). Timer interval is `1000000/75` µs and the timer loop catches up if late (`net-imps.c:533-543`), so the average is 75 ticks/s unless the CPU can't keep up. [INFERRED] The real wall-clock rate is still unmeasured.
2. **level_speeds** [SEEN] (`tables.c:2202-2216`): [0]=7500, [1]=9000, then +100 per level to [10]=9900, [11-15]=10000, +100 per level from [16]=10100 to [40]=12500, then the faster-growing part, [60]=15000, and 20000 from index 85 to 127. Parse the array rather than rely on my summary. `level_speed(d<=0)=level_speeds[0]*5` (`xtra2.c:5109-5113`). Examples, normal speed, 75 FPS:

Ticks/move (s/move): town 37 (0.49), DL1 45 (0.60), DL10 49 (0.65), DL30 57 (0.76), DL60 75 (1.00), DL90+ 100 (1.33). Running is 5x faster per step; resting block is time/10 ticks (DL1: 4 ticks).
Ticks/move is the integer `time`; seconds are `time/75`; mean recall is `24.5*time/75` s (DL1 14.7, DL30 18.6, DL90+ 32.7).
3. **extract_energy** [SEEN] (`tables.c:2171-2192`): index = pspeed. Speed 110 (normal) gives 1000, +10 gives 2000, +20 gives 3000, +30 gives 3800+, and anything at or below 69 gives 100. This is 100x vanilla's scale; the cap is `level_speed`. Player energy per tick is `int(extract_energy[pspeed]*bubble/100)` (`dungeon.c:943-976`). Searching mode costs -10 speed (`xtra1.c:2782-2790`), and food >= 15000 also costs -10 (`xtra1.c:2778`).
4. **Energy costs** [SEEN]. Cost is `level_speed` for walk and run steps (`cmd2.c:3039`, `cmd1.c:2947`), stairs, open, close, disarm, search, tunnel, throw, quaff, read, eat, use staff, aim wand, zap rod, activate, cast, wield, destroy and drop-gold (the `energy -=` lines in cmd2/cmd3/cmd5/cmd6.c). It is `level_speed/2` for takeoff, drop and refuel (`cmd3.c:587,676,1359,1454`). A blow costs `level_speed/num_blow` and attacks repeat while energy suffices (`cmd1.c:1429-1435`). A shot costs `level_speed/num_fire` (`cmd2.c:3582,3647`). Starting a rest costs `level_speed`, and energy is then forced to 0 each tick while resting (`dungeon.c:985-990`). Rest is refused if poisoned or at full HP and SP (`net-game.c:1996-2003`). **Holding or staying costs no energy and doesn't search**: that code is `#if 0` (`cmd2.c:3183-3200`).
5. **time_factor rules** [SEEN] (`xtra2.c:5164-5270`, `mdefines.h:262-271`):
```
tf = 100
health = chp*100/mhp
if health <= hitpoint_warn*10: tf = 100/CONSTANT_TIME_FACTOR = 20     # CONSTANT_TIME_FACTOR=5 IS defined (mdefines.h:271), so not proportional
los = any awake monster in LOS, or hostile player in LOS
if hitpoint_warn > 9 and not los: tf = 100
if resting and not los: tf = 1000
if running and not los: tf = 500
# then min over other players within 20 grids, same depth (chained)
```
   - **Low HP with warn<=9 and no monster in LOS stays at 0.2x**, since only warn>9 resets it. Tool-mode client default hpwarn is 6 (`client/c-init.c:718-727`), so at HP<=60% every non-rest, non-run action (walking, searching, quaffing) takes 5x the real time. Regen, light, food and timeouts also run 5x slower.
   - `time_factor()` returns 100 in town (`xtra2.c:5262`), but `process_player_end` calls `base_time_factor` directly (`dungeon.c:1053`). So in town, resting still gets the 10x block rate while energy is unscaled. Running in town gets an extra x5 energy (`dungeon.c:953-957`).
   - Monsters gain `int(extract_energy[mspeed]*time_factor(closest player)/100)` per tick (`melee2.c:3460-3479`), so they slow with you.
6. **ENERGY_BUILDUP and MONSTER_RECOIL** [SEEN]. Both default TRUE (`tables.c:2537-2541`). Buildup stores energy above `level_speed`, capped at one more `level_speed`, and adds it back next tick (`dungeon.c:960-973`). It resets to 0 on walk, run and rest-toggle (`net-game.c:1979,2017,2404`; `cmd1.c:2952`). Recoil: after a monster melee-attacks a player it loses another `level_speed` energy (`melee2.c:3035-3044`).
7. **Block formulas** [SEEN]. In `process_player_end`, `time = level_speed/1000` (integer), then `time = time/(tf/100.0)` truncated (`dungeon.c:1112-1116`). Per block (`turn.turn % time == 0`, line 1183): every timed counter is decremented once. That covers hero, shero, bless, fast, slow, protevil, resists, poison, stun, cut, confusion, blind and so on (`dungeon.c:1300-1478`). Light fuel drops by 1 (`1479-1520`), rod timeouts decrement, and pseudo-ID `sense_inventory` runs. WoR counts down (`1625-1636`). HP and SP regenerate. Food uses a separate clock, every `10*time` ticks (line 1118). Because these are game-time units, **real seconds scale with depth and with tf**. Resting makes a potion of heroism expire about 10x sooner in real time.
8. **Recall** [SEEN]. `word_recall = randint0(20)+15`, i.e. 15..34 blocks (`spells2.c:1191`). It is postponed 1 block while in a store (`dungeon.c:1632`). Seconds = blocks*time/75. At DL10 that is 7.4-16.6 s (mean 16 s), and about 1 s if resting with no LOS (time 4). Reading again cancels it (`spells2.c:1195-1198`). **@R depth rule** (`spells2.c:1148-1180`): default is `max_dlv`. `@R<n>`: n is used as a level; if `n%50==0` it becomes `n/50` (so @R50=level 1, @R100=level 2, and @R0 falls back to max). If `n>max_dlv` or `n==0`, then `max_dlv`. A negative (wilderness) value is replaced by 1 if the wild map was not visited. Read in the dungeon it goes to town; read in town it goes to `recall_depth`. `max_dlv` is the max of every depth arrived at (`dungeon.c:2062-2063`). Max depth is 127 (`cmd2.c:158`).
9. **Regeneration** [SEEN] (`dungeon.c:293-318, 1245-1305`):
```
pct = 197 (PY_REGEN_NORMAL); if food<1000: 98; if food<500: 33; if food<100: 0   # PY_FOOD_WEAK/FAINT/STARVE
if regenerate: pct*=2
if resting or searching: pct*=2
if poisoned or cut: hp pct=0 (mana pct unaffected)
per block: new = mhp*pct + 1442; hp += new>>16; chp_frac += new&0xFFFF (carry +1 on overflow)
```
   - Resting HP per block is ~0.60% of mhp (+0.02 hp). Blocks per real second while resting at DL1 are 75/4=18.75, so about 11% mhp per second and about 9 s from 0 to full. Not resting with no monster in LOS it is 0.30% per 0.6 s, about 0.5% mhp/s. [INFERRED] from the formulas. `notes_players.md:201` says resting "takes a few seconds", which is consistent. Mana: `msp*pct+524` (`dungeon.c:327-345`).
   - Faint below food 500: 10% per food event of paralysis for 1+randint0(5) turns. Starving: damage `(100-food)/10` per food event.
10. **Food** [SEEN] (`dungeon.c:1118-1146`): per `10*time` ticks lose `(extract_energy[pspeed]/100)*2` (+30 regenerate, -10 slow_digest; -100 if gorged); none in town or on special levels unless gorged. Normal speed: 20 per event, about 3.3 food/s at DL1 and 2.0 at DL60. Ration 5000, Waybread 7500, Slime Mold 3000, Biscuit 500 (`lib/edit/object.txt:261-297`). Thresholds FULL 10000, ALERT 2000, WEAK 1000, FAINT 500, STARVE 100, MAX 15000 (`mdefines.h:242-247`); start 9999 (`birth.c:630`).
11. **Light** [SEEN]. Fuel drops 1 per block, so one fuel unit costs `time/75` s (DL1 0.6 s, DL60 1.0 s). Max torch 5000, lantern 15000 (`mdefines.h:186-187`); store torch 2500, store lantern 7500 (`store.c:992-996`); floor torch `randint1(4000)`, lantern `randint1(7500)` (`object2.c:3104-3113`); flask +7500, torch merge `pval+5` (`cmd3.c:1363,1460`). 13. **Store maintenance and day/night** [SEEN]. Store maintenance runs when `turn.turn % 2500 == 0` (10*STORE_TURNS, `dungeon.c:1908`), which is every 33.3 s at 75 FPS. 14. **Traps** [SEEN]. `pick_trap`: `FEAT_TRAP_HEAD(0x10)+randint0(16)`, uniform, no depth dependence, trap door rerolled on quest levels and at depth>=127 (`object2.c:4170-4200`). Per-trap rows with lines are in the CSV.

15. **Search** [SEEN] (`cmd1.c:507-585`): each of the 9 grids independently, `randint0(100) < skill_srh` (%). srh is /10 if blind or no light, and /10 again if confused or hallucinating. It finds hidden traps, secret doors (+1 exp) and chest traps. `skill_srh = r_srh + c_srh + x_srh*lev/10 + 5*pval` (`xtra1.c:2289,2838,2436-2476`). 16. **Disarm and lock-pick** [SEEN]. Let `i=skill_dis`, /10 if blind or no light, /10 again if confused or hallucinating. `skill_dis = r_dis + c_dis + adj_dex_dis[DEX] + adj_int_dis[INT] + x_dis*lev/10` (`xtra1.c:2273,2813-2814,2826`; tables `tables.c:1366,1412`).

## Contradictions and surprises
- Memo mission15 (`memos/2026-10-07-mission15-answers.md:100-129`): confirmed on disarm/lock formula, chest table, contents and "opening fires trap". Small corrections: search never occurs on hold (confirmed), "1-in-41 spontaneous" depends on `skill_fos` (a warrior's 2 + race gives 1/(50-fos)). Lock-pick failure does not set a trap off, but a **successful** pick still fires the chest trap (`cmd2.c:951-1000`).
- The brief's list says "time_factor ... low-HP bubble hitpoint_warn": the effect is **constant 0.2x** (CONSTANT_TIME_FACTOR defined), not proportional to HP. It also **persists with no monsters in LOS** unless hpwarn=10 or 0 (see 5).
