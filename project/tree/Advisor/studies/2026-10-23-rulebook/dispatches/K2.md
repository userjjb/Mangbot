## Answer
Monsters pick uniformly among their spell flags once the per-turn roll passes. Chance per monster turn is floor(100/x)% for `1_IN_x`. Damage and effects are fixed in `make_attack_spell` plus `project_p` (formulas in the CSV and below). The "smart" spell filter is compiled out. One big surprise: **BOULDER does no damage to players** (no `GF_BOULDER` case in `project_p`). Blows are resolved in `melee1.c`; only HURT and SHATTER blows are reduced by AC. Monster HP is `damroll(hdice,hside)` unless FORCE_MAXHP. Breaths use CURRENT HP.

## Findings
1. **Spell trigger [SEEN]** (melee2.c:459-483). `chance=(freq_innate+freq_spell)/2`, and init1.c:3184 sets both to `100/x` (integer), so a `1_IN_x` is `floor(100/x)`%: 1_IN_3 is 33%, 1_IN_7 is 14%. Conditions: not confused, `cdis<=MAX_RANGE=18`, `projectable_wall` to the player. A cast uses the monster's turn (process_monster returns, melee2.c:2721). The target is the closest player, preferring one in LOS (melee2.c:3411-3457).
2. **Spell choice [SEEN]** (melee2.c:522-559). Uniform `randint0(num)` over all set flags in RF4+RF5+RF6; innate and spell flags are not weighted. If the monster is SMART, HP<10% and a 50% roll passes, the list is cut to INT_MASK (mdefines.h:2582-2594: HOLD, SLOW, CONF, BLIND, SCARE, BLINK, TPORT, TELE_LEVEL, TELE_AWAY, HEAL, HASTE, TRAPS and the S_ summons except S_KIN and S_HI_DEMON). `remove_bad_spells` ("smart_learn/cheat") is dead code: `DRS_SMART_OPTIONS` is commented out (options.h:437, "broken in mangband"). So monsters never avoid spells you resist.
3. **Breaths [SEEN]** (melee2.c:634-874). Damage is `m_ptr->hp/div`, capped, using current HP, not max. Divisor 3: ACID/ELEC/FIRE/COLD cap 1600, POIS 800, TIME 150, GRAV 200. Divisor 6: NETH 550, LITE/DARK/CONF/NEXU 400, SOUN/CHAO/DISE/SHAR 500, PLAS 150, WALL (force) 200, INER 200. BR_MANA is an empty case (melee2.c:876-881) and no monster uses it. `breath()` is a ball of radius 2 (3 if POWERFUL, melee2.c:357); the target takes `(dam+dist)/(dist+1)`, so full damage at the centre, and `project_p` caps at 1600 (spells1.c:3603-3606).
4. **Elemental resist maths [SEEN]** (spells1.c:990-1077). Acid, elec, fire, cold: resist gives `(d+2)/3`, a temporary potion resist (oppose) gives another `(d+2)/3` (stacking to about 1/9), immune gives 0. Acid is also halved `(d+1)/2` if an armour piece is damaged. Inventory damage takes 1/2/3 items for dam<30/<60/else. POIS: resist and oppose each `(d+2)/3`; if you have neither, poisoned += randint0(dam)+10 (spells1.c:3640). The "other" resists use `d*N/(randint1(6)+6)`: NETH, CHAO, DISE, NEXU and SHAR use N=6 (average factor about 0.65); CONF, SOUN use 5 (about 0.54); LITE, DARK use 4 (about 0.44). Averages are my arithmetic [INFERRED].
5. **Breath side effects** (spells1.c:3671-3932). Stun comes from SOUN (`randint1(dam>90?35:dam/3+5)`), PLAS (`dam>40?35:dam*3/4+5`), WALL (`randint1(20)`), GRAV, ICE and WATER, unless resist_sound. INERT (inertia) slows (+randint0(4)+4) with no Free Action check and no save. GRAV also slows, and teleports you 5 grids if `randint1(127)>plev`. NETH, CHAO and TIME drain exp (see CSV). TIME also cuts `stat_cur` to 3/4 on 9/10 outcomes. NEXU: teleport 200 (3/7), teleport-to-breather (2/7), teleport level with save (1/7), stat swap with save (1/7). SHAR cuts +dam. LITE/DARK blind +randint1(5)+2 unless rBlind. CONF confuses +randint1(20)+10.
6. **Balls and bolts [SEEN]** (melee2.c:913-1345). Dice by `rlev`: BA_ACID `randint1(rlev*3)+15`; BA_ELEC `randint1(rlev*3/2)+8`; BA_FIRE `randint1(rlev*7/2)+10`; BA_COLD `randint1(rlev*3/2)+10`; BA_POIS 12d2; BA_NETH `50+10d10+rlev`; BA_WATE `randint1(rlev*5/2)+50`; BA_MANA and BA_DARK `rlev*5+10d10`. Bolts: BO_ACID `7d8+rlev/3`; BO_ELEC `4d8+rlev/3`; BO_FIRE `9d8+rlev/3`; BO_COLD `6d8+rlev/3`; BO_NETH `30+5d5+rlev*3/2`; BO_WATE `10d10+rlev`; BO_MANA `randint1(rlev*7/2)+50`; BO_PLAS `10+8d7+rlev`; BO_ICEE `6d6+rlev`; MISSILE `2d6+rlev/3`; ARROW_1..4 `1d6/3d6/5d6/7d6` (no AC, no resist, no save). BO_POIS is an empty case. Bolts stop at the first monster or player (`PROJECT_STOP`, melee2.c:335). `rlev=max(level,1)`.
7. **BOULDER [SEEN, surprise]**. melee2.c:907 casts `bolt(GF_BOULDER, damroll(1+level/7,12))`. `project_p` (spells1.c:3613-4093) lists 35 `GF_` cases and none is `GF_BOULDER`, so it takes the `default: dam = 0` branch (4086-4092) and never calls `take_hit`. GF_BOULDER appears only in `project_m` (2495) and colour code (512). Result: boulders do no damage to the player and apply no effect (it still disturbs). The server binary was not run, so this is code-only. The danger table rates giants' "BOULDER bolt avg~30" as damaging (see Contradictions). Users: 16 monsters, levels 25-87.
8. **Saves, status and mind attacks [SEEN]** (melee2.c:1083-1460). The saving throw is `randint0(100) < skill_sav` for every spell below.
   - SCARE: resist_fear or save blocks; else afraid +randint0(4)+4.
   - BLIND: resist_blind or save blocks; else `set_blind(12+randint0(4))` (set, not add).
   - CONF: resist_conf or save blocks; else confused +randint0(4)+4.
   - SLOW and HOLD: free_act or save blocks; else slowed or paralysed +randint0(4)+4.
   - MIND_BLAST: save blocks; else 8d8, plus confusion unless rConf.
   - BRAIN_SMASH: save blocks; else 12d15, plus blind 8+randint0(8) unless rBlind, plus confusion unless rConf, plus paralysis unless free_act, plus slow +randint0(4)+4 **always** (Free Action does not stop it).
   - CAUSE_1..4: save blocks; else 3d8, 8d8, 10d15, 15d15 (CAUSE_4 also cut +10d10).
   - DRAIN_MANA: no save, no damage; drains `randint1(rlev)/2+1` mana and heals the monster 6x that. It does nothing to a character with 0 mana.
9. **Movement spells [SEEN]** (melee2.c:1574-1649). TELE_TO (`teleport_player_to`, lands you adjacent to the caster) and TELE_AWAY (`teleport_player(100)`: 50-100 grids away) have no save and no resist. TELE_LEVEL is blocked by resist_nexus or the save; otherwise it is 50% down, 50% up (always down in town; up if at max depth or on a quest level; "Nothing happens" in ironman or arena). BLINK is `teleport_away(m,10)` (lands 5-10 grids away); TPORT is `teleport_away(m,45)` (22-45 grids). HASTE: mspeed +10 until base+10, then +2 steps to base+20, with no decay. HEAL: `hp += rlev*6`, clears fear. SHRIEK aggravates: wakes monsters within 40 grids and speeds those in LOS to base+10 permanently (spells2.c:3313-3360).
10. **Darkness, traps, forget [SEEN]**. DARKNESS is `unlite_area(0,3)`. It is projected as `who=-player`, and `project_p` returns for self-sourced projections (spells1.c:3540), so there is **no damage and no blindness**, only unlighting the room. TRAPS: `place_trap` on naked floor grids in radius 1 (spells1.c:1894-1897); your own grid is not naked, so only the neighbours get traps. FORGET: save, else forget the map and unknown-item flags (spells2.c:1064-1125).
11. **Summons [SEEN]** (melee2.c:1704-1973, monster2.c:2382-2594). Each flag makes N calls to `summon_specific(Depth, player_y, player_x, rlev, type)`, so monsters appear around the **player**: scatter distance `i/15+1` over 20 tries, i.e. adjacent for the first 15. Monsters are placed **awake**, with groups allowed (`place_monster_aux(...,slp=FALSE,grp=TRUE)`), so FRIENDS/ESCORT species add a group of up to 32 (placement group size: `randint1(13)` ± level-difference term, capped at 32, monster2.c:2003-2040). Target level: `get_mon_num((Depth+rlev)/2+5)`, which itself has a 2x 1-in-50 "nasty" boost and best-of-2 (50%) or best-of-3 (10%) draws (monster2.c:563-713). If no monster of that class exists at or below the target, the call fails.

   | S_KIN | 6 | same glyph as caster, non-unique | caster's glyph |
   | S_MONSTER / S_MONSTERS | 1 / 8 | any monster, uniques allowed | |
   | S_ANIMAL | 6 | RF3_ANIMAL non-unique | 1 |
   | S_SPIDER | 6 | 'S' | 2 |

   | S_HI_UNDEAD | 8 | 'L','V','W' (uniques ok) | 24 |
   | S_HI_DRAGON | 8 | 'D' | 40 |

   | S_WRAITH | 8+8 | 'W' unique, plus 8 HI_UNDEAD | 40 |

   Class minimum levels come from monsters.csv [SEEN via script]. A unique is created only if some player on the level has NOT killed it, and its `cur_num < max_num` (monster2.c:17-35, 1834).
12. **Blow hit chance [SEEN]** (melee1.c:106-127). 5% always hit, 5% always miss, else hit if `randint1(power+3*rlev) > (ac*3)/4`. So P(hit) = 0.05 + 0.90 * max(0, (i-T)/i) with `i=power+3*rlev`, `T=floor(0.75*ac)`. Blows with effect 0 always hit for 0 damage. Powers: HURT 60, SHATTER 60, UN_BONUS 20, UN_POWER 15, ELEC/FIRE/COLD/CONFUSE/TERRIFY/HALLU 10, POISON/EAT_GOLD/EAT_ITEM/EAT_FOOD/EAT_LITE/EXP_x 5, BLIND/PARALYZE/LOSE_ALL 2, ACID and LOSE_STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHR 0 (melee1.c:243-271).
13. **Blow damage and effects [SEEN]** (melee1.c:491-1224).
    - AC reduces only HURT and SHATTER: `dmg -= dmg*min(ac,150)/250`, which is at most 60%. ACID/ELEC/FIRE/COLD blows use the `*_dam` functions (resists, no AC; acid is halved if armour is damaged).
    - POISON: poisoned +randint1(rlev)+5 unless rPois or opposed.
    - BLIND: +10+randint1(rlev) unless rBlind (no save). CONFUSE: +3+randint1(rlev) unless rConf (no save).
    - TERRIFY: resist or save blocks; else +3+randint1(rlev).
    - PARALYZE: free_act blocks, else save, else paralysed +3+randint1(rlev); it stacks without cap. A level-1 paralyser also blinks away.
    - LOSE_STR etc.: `do_dec_stat` is blocked only by sustain. It is a temporary drain (`dec_stat(...,10,FALSE)`, spells2.c:298); LOSE_ALL hits all 6 stats. No save.
    - UN_BONUS: disenchants one random slot unless rDisen. UN_POWER drains a charged wand or staff and heals the monster `rlev*charges`. EAT_LITE: -250..-500 fuel (not Dwarven lamps). EAT_FOOD removes one food item.
    - EXP_10/20/40/80: damroll(10/20/40/80,6)+(exp/100)*2; hold_life blocks 95/90/75/50% and otherwise takes 1/10.
    - SHATTER causes an earthquake (radius 8) if the blow does >23.
    - HIT, KICK, PUNCH, BUTT, CRUSH stun and HIT, CLAW, BITE cut, only on a critical (95% of max dice, `monster_critical`).
14. **Theft [SEEN]** (melee1.c:609-751). EAT_GOLD and EAT_ITEM do their blow damage first. Save (not if paralysed): `randint0(100) < adj_dex_safe[DEX index] + plev`. adj_dex_safe runs 0 (DEX 3), 6 (DEX 10), 10 (DEX 18), 25 (18/50), 50 (18/100), 100 (18/150+) (tables.c:1872). Gold: take `au/10+randint1(25)` (min 2); if >5000, `au/20+randint1(3000)`. EAT_GOLD: on a save the thief still teleports with 2/3 probability; on success it always teleports. EAT_ITEM: on a save the thief always teleports; otherwise it takes one of up to 10 random pack slots (not artifacts) and carries it if it can carry. The thief teleports with `teleport_away(m,45)` after all 4 blows (melee1.c:1346-1351); kill it to get the item back if it carried one.
15. **HP, level and uniques [SEEN]** (monster2.c:1913-1919, 563-713). `maxhp = FORCE_MAXHP ? maxroll(hdice,hside) : damroll(hdice,hside)`. HP is not scaled with depth; the monster's level is its race level. Out-of-depth comes only from `get_mon_num` (nasty boost: two independent 1/50 rolls, each `level += min(level/4+2, 5)`; best-of-2/3 draws), vault/nest marks `&` (+5) and `@` (+11) (generate.c:2437-2444), and summons.
16. **Speed and energy [SEEN]**. A monster gains `extract_energy[mspeed]` per game turn and acts at `level_speed(depth)` energy, so only the ratio to your own `extract_energy[pspeed]` matters (xtra2.c:5109, dungeon.c:943, melee2.c:3460-3476). Non-unique monsters get `mspeed = speed + rand_spread(0, extract_energy[speed]/1000)`: +/-1 index at 110, +/-2 at 120, etc. (monster2.c:1927-1935). Uniques get exactly the listed speed. **Monster recoil** (birth option, default TRUE, tables.c:2540): after a melee blow the monster pays `level_speed` energy a second time (melee2.c:3035-3044), so it needs two periods to act again. Spells do not recoil. Timed effects (paralysis, slow, confusion) tick once per `level_speed/1000` game turns (dungeon.c:1108-1183), which is about one normal-speed player turn [INFERRED from dungeon.c plus `level_speed`].

## Contradictions and surprises
- **build_danger_table.py / danger table rate BOULDER as damaging** (danger_table.csv rows for Hill, Frost, Fire giants: "BOULDER bolt avg~30 no resist"). By the code (finding 7) it does 0 damage. The Advisor should test this from a live log before dropping it from ratings (lead A).
- **Breath numbers are at full HP.** `breath_avg_max` (script lines 45-49) uses avg or max HP, but `hp/div` uses CURRENT HP (melee2.c:642). An upper bound; a wounded breather hits less.
- **ENERGY list stops at speed index 139** (script line 28), defaulting to 3.7 above. The real table runs to 4900 (tables.c:2171-2190); no effect while levels are <=40. `speed_x` also ignores per-monster speed variation, HASTE (+10, then +2s) and recoil. The danger table's `melee_avg` is dice only: it ignores hit chance, AC reduction (HURT/SHATTER) and recoil (blows cost 2 periods).
- Script tags: "CONF_BLOW (no save)" and "BLIND_BLOW" are correct (no save). PARA_BLOW also has a save; fine. BRAIN_SMASH slow ignores Free Action, a detail the tags lack. Per-flag breath cap/divisor table in the script matches the code exactly (19 breaths checked).
- The brief says a monster level boost exists for out-of-depth. It does not scale stats or HP; only the generation boost above (finding 15).
- `monsters.csv` goes to level 100 (spells/blows up to lvl 100); I covered all levels, not just 0-70. CSV has the n for 0-70.
