# Dispatch: A1 ability inference (messages that prove an ability or resist)
- Brief: complete table of server messages/indirect evidence that reveal equipment abilities; what the server and the tool client `flags` query show for unidentified gear.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-23; sources covered: server melee1.c (blow effects 240-300, 490-1230), melee2.c (spell cases 128+10..160+14), spells1.c (project_p 3510-4110, *_dam 930-1075, apply_nexus/disenchant), spells2.c (do_dec_stat, self_knowledge, player_flags_spoil), xtra2.c set_*, cmd1.c traps, cmd2.c chest traps, cmd3.c do_cmd_wield, use-obj.c, dungeon.c, xtra1.c (calc_bonuses, prt_flags, indicators), object1.c (object_flags_aux, object_flags_known), tables.c indicators, client c-tool.c (flags, status), lib/edit/ego_item.txt; K2 dispatch used, not redone. Paths are under `github/src/server/` unless noted.
- CSV: `scratch/A1/A1_abilities.csv` (44 rows). Extra: `scratch/A1/A1_ego_flags.csv` (all 118 ego items with tval range and flags), generator `make_csv.py`.

## Answer
1. Only a few abilities have a message that appears **only** with them: free_act and rBlind/rNexus ("You are unaffected!"), hold_life ("You keep hold of your life force!" and "...slipping away!"), sustains ("...for a moment, but the feeling passes."), feather_fall (the "You float gently..." trap lines). Most others are visible only as the *absence* of a status message after a no-save source (trap, blow, food, potion), or as HP numbers.
2. **Wielding teaches nothing**: no "You feel..." message, no flag learning. The only wield text is `You are wearing X (c).` and, for cursed items, `Oops! It feels deathly cold!`. But `calc_bonuses` uses the item's real flags, so speed, stealth, searching, infravision, blows, shots and stats (all sent to the client) change at once, unknown item or not.
3. The tool `flags` query shows **only known flags**. An unidentified item contributes nothing, so Boots of Free Action show an empty grid column until the item is identified. For identified (ID_KNOWN) ego items the grid gets base plus ego flags, but not random extra resists or artifact flags (those need ID_MENTAL).

## Findings
1. **"You are unaffected!" is printed for five different checks [SEEN]**: HOLD spell `free_act` (melee2.c:1448), SLOW spell `free_act` (1425), BLIND spell `resist_blind` (1380), TELE_LEVEL spell `resist_nexus` (1637), PARALYZE blow `free_act` (melee1.c:970). Also PvP `GF_OLD_SLOW`/`GF_OLD_SLEEP` (spells1.c:4025, 4042). Disambiguate with the spell's own line just before it: HOLD "stares deep into your eyes!" or "mumbles." (1445); SLOW "drains power from your muscles!" (1421); BLIND "casts a spell, burning your eyes!" (1378); TELE_LEVEL "gestures at your feet." / "mumbles strangely." The Architect's "unaffected => Free Action" rule is wrong for BLIND and TELE_LEVEL lines. Use it only after HOLD/SLOW/paralysis blows. (Brief cites 1445-1448; HOLD block is 1440-1457.)
2. **"You resist the effects!" proves the player LACKS the matching resist [SEEN]**. In HOLD, SLOW, BLIND, TELE_LEVEL and the PARALYZE blow the resist/free_act test comes first and prints "unaffected"; "resist" is the saving-throw branch (`randint0(100) < skill_sav`). Same text is used for MIND_BLAST, BRAIN_SMASH, CAUSE_*, FORGET (saves only). For nexus breath, a rNexus player skips `apply_nexus` entirely (spells1.c:3817-3823), so any apply_nexus message ("You resist the effects!", "Your body starts to scramble...") proves no rNexus.
3. **Fear and confusion messages cannot separate resist from save [SEEN]**. SCARE gives "You refuse to be frightened." for both rFear and a save (melee2.c:1355-1365); CONF gives "You disbelieve the feeble spell." for both (1401-1411); the TERRIFY blow gives "You stand your ground!" for both (melee1.c:938-946). Only no-save sources work: CONFUSE blow (melee1.c:916), confusion gas trap (cmd1.c:1160-1167), potion/mushroom of Confusion, mushroom of Paranoia (use-obj.c:43-51), since "You are confused!" / "You are terrified!" never appears with rConf/rFear. Caveat: hero/berserk give a temporary resist_fear (xtra1.c:2757-2760).
4. **Free Action, cleanest tests [SEEN]**. Paralysis-gas trap "A strange white mist surrounds you!" (cmd1.c:1182) paralyses unless free_act, with no save (1183-1186). Chest "A puff of yellow gas surrounds you!" (cmd2.c:412) and Potion of Sleep / Mushroom of Paralysis (use-obj.c:79-87, 308-316) are the same. A set_* function prints "You are paralyzed!" only on the 0-to-nonzero transition (xtra2.c:321-346). No such line after the trap text means free_act. There is no ability check inside any `set_*` (grep of xtra2.c).
5. **Free Action does not stop everything [SEEN]**. BRAIN_SMASH always slows (melee2.c:1146), INERT breath and GRAVITY slow with no free_act test (spells1.c:3839-3846, 3918-3924). So "You feel yourself moving slower!" says nothing about free_act. Weak paralysis blow (power 2) still goes through hit-roll first (K2 finding 12).
6. **Life drain gives three distinct strings [SEEN]**: "You keep hold of your life force!" (only with hold_life; 95/90/75/50% for EXP_10/20/40/80, 75% for BR_NETH/BR_CHAO), "You feel your life slipping away!" (hold_life, roll failed, drain is 1/10), "You feel your life draining away!" (no hold_life). Lines: melee1.c:1104-1203; spells1.c:3690-3701, 3739-3750. With rNether, BR_NETH prints none of these (the drain block is in the else branch, spells1.c:3684-3705). Potion of Lose Memories does nothing with hold_life (use-obj.c:320-327).
7. **Sustains [SEEN]**: `do_dec_stat` prints "You feel weak|stupid|naive|clumsy|sickly|ugly for a moment, but the feeling passes." only with the matching sustain (spells2.c:271-292; order STR, INT, WIS, DEX, CON, CHR from desc_stat_neg 257-266). Without it: "You feel very weak." etc. Sources: LOSE_x blows (melee1.c:995-1075; LOSE_ALL tries all six), dart traps STR/DEX/CON (cmd1.c:1100-1141), chest needles STR/CON (cmd2.c:384-397), mushrooms and Potions of Weakness etc. **No sustain check** in Potion of Ruination (use-obj.c:331-342, `dec_stat` directly) or BR_TIME (spells1.c:3874-3911: cut to 3/4).
8. **Feather fall [SEEN]**: trap door "You float gently down to the next level." (cmd1.c:927; else damage 2d8), pit "You float gently to the bottom of the pit." (965), spiked pits "You float gently to the floor of the pit." plus "You carefully avoid touching the spikes." (981-982, 1011-1012). "You fell through a trap door!" (924) prints for both.
9. **Elemental and poison resists are silent [SEEN]**. Resist gives `(d+2)/3`, immunity 0 damage, with no text (spells1.c:993-1075; K2 finding 4). Evidence is the HP drop compared with the dice, plus inventory side effects: an acid hit that prints "Your X is damaged!" (spells1.c:962-967 region) proves not immune, since `acid_dam` returns before `minus_ac` (995). Poison: a POISON blow, poison breath, gas trap (cmd1.c:1170-1177) or poison food with no "You are poisoned!" means rPois or an active potion (check the `oppose` indicator). The spiked-pit text "The poison does not affect you!" (cmd1.c:1030) is rPois OR potion.
10. **Nothing is printed for these at all [SEEN]**: random teleportation (dungeon.c:990-994, 1% per player turn, `teleport_player(40)`), experience drain (dungeon.c:1529-1538, 10% per player turn, exp and max_exp both -1), aggravate (monsters notice every turn; melee2.c:2503-2518), see_invisible (invisible monsters become drawn, monster2.c:1405-1414), telepathy (monsters drawn outside LOS; `esp == TR3_TELEPATHY` exact test, monster2.c:1200-1204), regeneration (HP regen doubled, +30 food use; dungeon.c:1138, 1281), slow_digest (-10 food use, dungeon.c:1141), permanent light (radius, xtra1.c:2050-2090).
11. **Numbers the client already gets, with unknown items included [SEEN calc; REPORTED delivery]**. `calc_bonuses` reads `object_flags()` (full) for every worn item (xtra1.c:2399-2420). The client receives `speed` (= pspeed-110, xtra1.c:413), `stat0..5`, `skills` (thn, fos, thb, srh, sav, dis, stl, dev as value/factor pairs; stealth factor 1) and `skills2` = [blows, shots, infravision] (xtra1.c:1111-1143, tables.c:797-803). 12. **What `flags` returns [SEEN]**. c-tool.c:590-615 reads `hist_flags[slot][7+f]` for the 32 names in `tool_flag_names` and reports slot letters a..l and `@`. Bit order matches the server grid (xtra1.c:885-899, mdefines.h:2094-2141): row 0..7 res_acid..res_dark, then res_blind..res_disen, slow_digest..hold_life, stealth..might. The server fills it from `object_flags_known()` (xtra1.c:938), which returns zeros unless `object_known_p` (ID_KNOWN, or EASY_KNOW+aware) (object1.c:1044-1060, mdefines.h:2677). `SPOIL_EGO_ITEMS` is not defined anywhere, so ego flags are not spoiled. Consequences: (a) an unidentified item shows nothing; (b) '*' (immunity) and '+' collapse to the same slot letter in the tool; (c) aggravate, teleport, drain_exp, impact, and the six sustains are NOT among the 32 names (sustains are in grid rows 1-6, `s`, not queried; `prt_player_sust_info` even uses full `f1` for stat pvals, xtra1.c:826-830); (d) the `@` column is `player_flags()`: race/class flags only (xtra1.c:649-676), not temporary effects.

## Contradictions and surprises
- Against the Architect's current rule: "You are unaffected!" alone does NOT prove Free Action (BLIND/TELE_LEVEL print it for rBlind/rNexus) (finding 1).
- Against the brief's assumption "sustain refused => 'You feel very weak for a moment, but the feeling passes'": actual text is "You feel weak for a moment, but the feeling passes." ("very" appears only on the drain message).
- Possible surprise: Dive04's boots would show an empty `flags` column even though calc_bonuses was granting Free Action; the Pilot could not have learned it from `flags` (finding 12).
- `calc_bonuses` sets `telepathy |= f3` for other items (xtra1.c:2512-2518); harmless because the test is exact equality, but the field is not a boolean.

## Leads
- Pilot-side design: a per-event "evidence ledger" keyed on the preceding spell line (HOLD, SLOW, BLIND, TELE_LEVEL) plus a trap-based Free Action check (paralysis gas trap/chest, no "You are paralyzed!").
- Does the pilot see messages for trap triggers? Check whether the message log keeps "A strange white mist surrounds you!" and the following lines in order.
- Use pseudo-ID text ("excellent") on boots/gloves/cloaks as the cheap "don't sell, probably ego" trigger. Dive04 would probably have gotten "excellent" (warrior) if it had waited; log check needed.
- Is `self_knowledge` output reachable from the tool client (it uses special_file_type, not a message)? Not checked.
- Identify scroll: does it set ID_KNOWN only (base+ego flags known) or ID_MENTAL (spells2.c:2886 vs 2991)? Which scroll maps to which was not traced.
