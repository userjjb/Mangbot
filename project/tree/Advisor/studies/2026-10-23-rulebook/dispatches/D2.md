3. Unique drops: 13 unique kills logged (7 distinct uniques in the two logs). Inscribed items are rare in the logs because the item list never shows inscriptions (only "You see/You have" messages and floor events do). The observations (one ego armour from Wormtongue, "good"-sensed Brodda items) do not contradict the model but cannot test it.

## Contradictions and surprises
- "3000 gold pieces worth of gold" (dive03 t=8.459, session start, townspeople on the monster list at t=7.96): 3000 is far above the 152 max for gold, and there is no "base" with 3000. The pilot was apparently in the town; this looks like a character start/test grant or a stolen-gold return, not a drop. Unexplained. [INFERRED]
- Wormtongue (m21) was killed at depth 14 per the carried "level" event; the brief had no depth. The kill message order and Lucerne Hammer fit. "Brodda's Scimitar lost" (m23): confirmed it was picked up by a Blubbering icky thing at t=757.4 and reappeared on the floor (t=761.5), so it was recovered/seen, not simply lost.
- Item lists show no inscription, so the unique-name test only works for items we walked on. That is why most unique kills yield 0 inscribed items although every one has e_items >= .9.
- Depth is not reset at session start: after a restart, the last "level" event is carried over, and dive03 has no pilot_start events at all (t jumps backward). Rows tagged at session start can be town (the 3000 row shows depth 20 carried over).
