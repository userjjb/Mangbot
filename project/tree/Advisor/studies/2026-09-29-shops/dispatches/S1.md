# Dispatch: S1 - what each store stocks and how the stock turns over
- Brief: stock tables (tval/sval to names), Black market rules, fill/maintain algorithm, maintenance timing in game turns and real seconds, per-item in-stock probability.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-29; sources: src/server/init2.c, store.c, dungeon.c, net-server.c, tables.c, mdefines.h, src/common/defines.h, lib/edit/object.txt, github/mangband.cfg, testserver/mangband.cfg. CSV: scratch/S1_stock.csv. Simulation script: scratch/S1_sim.py (sim.py).

## Answer
Each store 0-5 keeps between 13 and 36 stock slots (mean about 26). Each maintenance drops 1-9 slots and adds 1-9 slots, each new slot drawn uniformly from a 32-entry table (duplicate entries = more weight). Maintenance runs on EVERY store at once every 2500 server ticks. At FPS=75 (mangband.cfg) that is once every 33.3 real seconds, whether or not anyone is in town. Nothing is always stocked. My simulation gives about 0.88 (CLW at Temple), 0.98 (Phase, WoR at Alchemist), 0.93 (CCW), 0.77 (Main Gauche, Rapier), 0.98 (Flask) for an undisturbed store. These are far higher than the observed shortages, so player purchases or the stack-deletion mechanics below probably matter more than the table weights.
