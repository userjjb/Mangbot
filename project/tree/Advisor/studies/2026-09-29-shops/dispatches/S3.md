# Dispatch: light and food burn in game turns and real minutes
- Brief: S3 - where light/food are decremented, capacities, refuel rules, messages, tick rate, real-minute lifetimes.
- Clerk run: 2026-09-29; sources: github/src/server/{dungeon.c,xtra2.c,tables.c,mdefines.h,cmd3.c,store.c,birth.c,object2.c,use-obj.c,net-server.c,init2.c,variable.c}, github/src/options.h, github/lib/edit/object.txt, github/mangband.cfg, testserver/mangband.cfg. (Paths below are relative to github/src/server unless stated.)

## Answer
Light and food are both decremented inside `process_player_end()` (dungeon.c), once per `time` game turns, where `time = level_speed(depth)/1000` = 37 in town, 49 at 500 ft, 52 at 1000 ft, 57 at 1500 ft. The server runs 75 game turns per real second (cfg FPS=75). So one light unit is about one player turn (0.5 s in town, 0.66 to 0.77 s at depth). Light burns in town, day or night, in stores and while AFK. Food does NOT digest in town (unless gorged). Approximate lifetimes in real minutes:

| | town (0 ft) | 500 ft | 1000 ft | 1500 ft |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Torch, full 5000 | 41.1 | 54.4 | 57.8 | 63.3 |
| Torch, store-bought 2500 | 20.6 | 27.2 | 28.9 | 31.7 |
| Lantern, full 15000 | 123 | 163 | 173 | 190 |
| Ration (5000 food) | no digestion | 27.2 | 28.9 | 31.7 |

Caveat: resting with no monster in line of sight makes light burn 10x faster in real time in town (see finding 5). That may explain "three torches burned out while idle in town".

## Findings
1. **Light burn code [SEEN]** (dungeon.c:1183 `if ( !(turn.turn % time) )`, block runs to past 1480; light at dungeon.c:1470-1512). `o_ptr->pval--` on the wielded TV_LITE each time the block fires, unless artifact, Dwarven/Feanor lantern, or pval==0. No test on depth, daylight, store_num, resting or idle. Only blindness matters (keeps pval at least 1). I checked with awk that no one-tab `}` closes the block between 1184 and 1500.
2. **`time`** [SEEN] dungeon.c:1112-1115: `time = level_speed(p_ptr->dun_depth)/1000; time = time / ((float)timefactor / 100);` (int, truncated). `level_speed()` = `level_speeds[max(Depth,0)]*5` (xtra2.c:5109-5113). tables.c:2200: level_speeds[0]=7500, [10]=9900 (500 ft), [20]=10500 (1000 ft), [30]=11500 (1500 ft). Depth is a level index, 1 level = 50 ft. This follows the table's own comments, which I did not check against the generator. Results: time = 37, 49, 52, 57. `timefactor` = `base_time_factor(p_ptr,0)` (dungeon.c:1053), normally 100.
3. **Food code [SEEN]** dungeon.c:1118 `if ( !(turn.turn % (time*10)) )`, so digestion runs every 10x `time` game turns. In town or on special levels it is skipped: dungeon.c:1122-1126 `/* Noone uses food in town */ if ((depth==0 || check_special_level) && food < PY_FOOD_MAX) do_digest = FALSE`. Ghosts skip it too. Amount per event (dungeon.c:1133-1146): `i = (extract_energy[pspeed]/100)*2` = 20 at normal speed (extract_energy[110]=1000, tables.c:2169-2185), +30 if regenerate, -10 if slow digestion. Gorged (>=15000) loses 100 per event. Resting and stores do not stop digestion. Speed +10 doubles it (extract_energy 2000 gives 40) because `time` does not depend on player speed.
4. **Hunger thresholds [SEEN]** mdefines.h:242-247: MAX 15000 (Bloated), FULL 10000, ALERT 2000 ("Hungry"), WEAK 1000, FAINT 500, STARVE 100. New character starts at 9999 (birth.c:630). Messages (xtra2.c:1720-1745): "You are getting hungry." (<2000), "You are getting weak from hunger!" (<1000), "You are getting faint from hunger!" (<500). Below 1000, regen is reduced. Below 500, 10% chance per regen tick of "You faint from the lack of food." (paralysis 1-5 turns, dungeon.c:1263-1273). Below 100, take_hit of (100-food)/10 per event ("starvation"). Only in food code where `FAINT` is used I saw the faint check. Starving AFK players are disconnected: options.h:70 `DISCONNECT_STARVING (60*3)` seconds, checked in dungeon.c:1160 with afk_seconds (incremented per real second, net-server.c:692). Only applies when food<100 and digestion is active (i.e. not in town).
5. **Time bubble effect on burn rate [SEEN/INFERRED]** `base_time_factor` (xtra2.c:5164-5250): resting with no monster in LOS sets timefactor=MAX_TIME_SCALE=1000 (xtra2.c:5204); running with no LOS sets 500 (5206); low HP (health <= hitpoint_warn*10 %) gives /5 (CONSTANT_TIME_FACTOR 5, mdefines.h:271) if monsters are in LOS or hitpoint_warn<=9. In the dungeon, energy gain is also scaled by `time_factor()` (dungeon.c:945-949), so per player turn nothing changes but real time compresses (10x faster rest). But `time_factor()` returns 100 in town (xtra2.c:5262) while process_player_end uses `base_time_factor` directly (dungeon.c:1053). INFERRED consequence: a player resting in town with no monster in LOS has time=int(37/10.0)=3, so a torch burns 1 unit per 3 game turns = 25/s, and a full 5000 torch lasts 200 s (3.3 min); food is not affected (no town digestion). Not tested live. Resting stops when HP and SP are full (dungeon.c:1300-1304 disturb), so only long rest counts / `R` with count matter.
6. **Game-turn rate [SEEN]** net-server.c:428 `add_timer(NULL, ONE_SECOND/cfg_fps, dungeon_tick)`, ONE_SECOND=1e6 us (net-server.c:18); dungeon_tick calls dungeon() once (net-server.c:699-705); each dungeon() does one `ht_add(&turn,1)` (dungeon.c:~2297). cfg: `FPS = 75` in github/mangband.cfg:67 and testserver/mangband.cfg:67 (default in variable.c:182 is 12; the dungeon.c:2006 comment says "about 50"). The FPS option is parsed at init2.c:2118-2123 (timer reinstall is a TODO). The tick rate is best-effort: heavy load could slow real time, which I did not measure. Game clock in util.c:2957 uses `turn/cfg_fps` as seconds.
7. **Player turn length [SEEN/INFERRED]** Energy per game turn at normal speed = extract_energy[110] = 1000 (dungeon.c:942, tables.c:2180). An action costs level_speed(depth) = 37500 (town), 49500, 52500, 57500. So a player action takes 37.5, 49.5, 52.5, 57.5 game turns = 0.50, 0.66, 0.70, 0.77 s at 75 FPS at normal speed, one normal-speed player turn per action. Running in town multiplies energy x5 (RUNNING_FACTOR 500, dungeon.c:950-953). Energy caps at level_speed (dungeon.c:975). Deeper levels are slower per player turn (a design comment at tables.c:2195-2199).
8. **Capacities [SEEN]** mdefines.h:186-187 `FUEL_TORCH 5000`, `FUEL_LAMP 15000`. object.txt (line 17 header says `I: tval:sval:pval`): Wooden Torch `N:346 I:39:0:4000` (drops get random 1..4000 via object2.c:3104-3107 `randint1(pval)`), Brass Lantern `N:347 I:39:1:7500` (random 1..7500), Flask of oil `N:348 I:77:0:7500`, Ration `N:21 I:80:35:5000`, Hard Biscuit 500, Jerky 1500, Slime Mold 3000, Waybread 7500, Ale 500. **Store stock override [SEEN]** store.c:992-995: every store-created torch gets pval=FUEL_TORCH/2=2500 and every lantern 7500 (half full). The General store's torches therefore last half of a full 5000 (20.6 min in town, 27.2 min at 500 ft, 28.9 at 1000 ft, 31.7 at 1500 ft). Starting kit (non-ironman, birth.c:892): 3-7 torches with pval 1500..3500 (rand(3,7)*500), plus 3-7 rations (birth.c:886).
9. **Refuel rules [SEEN]** cmd3.c:1293-1400 (lantern): accepts flasks (item_tester at cmd3.c:1315 checks lantern sval too, so lanterns can refuel lanterns). Adds `o_ptr->pval` (7500 for a flask) capped at FUEL_LAMP; msgs "You fuel your lamp." / "Your lamp is full."; costs half a turn. Torch (cmd3.c:1454-1480): accepts a torch item; `pval += o_ptr->pval + 5`, cap FUEL_TORCH; msgs "You combine the torches." then "Your torch glows more brightly." or "Your torch is fully fueled."; half turn. The torch consumed is destroyed regardless of the excess. The object.txt text at N:346 confirms torch+torch, and N:348 says flask+lantern. Flasks cannot fuel torches (torch hook accepts only SV_LITE_TORCH; cmd3.c:1410).
10. **Low-fuel messages [SEEN]** dungeon.c:1495-1510: at pval 0 "Your light has gone out!" (disturb); when pval<100 and multiple of 10: "Your light is growing faint." (10 messages over the last 100 units, i.e. the last 100 units: 49 s in town, 65 s at 500 ft). Inventory redraw every 100 units. Light radius comes from calc_torch (xtra1.c:2061-2069), radius 1 torch / 2 lantern only if pval>0, so at 0 fuel there is no light.
11. **Ration in real minutes [SEEN formula, INFERRED numbers]** Each ration (5000) = 250 digestion events at normal speed with no regen/slow-digest = 250 x (10 x time) game turns. That is 27.2 min at 500 ft, 28.9 at 1000 ft, 31.7 at 1500 ft, and 0 in town. From full (10000) to "Hungry" (<2000) is 8000/20 = 400 events, about 43.6 min at 500 ft. A newly born character starting at 9999 reaches "Hungry" after about 44 min of dungeon time. Regeneration items double the drain (+30 per event, 50 vs 20 = 2.5x).
12. **Arithmetic steps** minutes = units x time / 75 / 60. Torch 5000, town: 5000x37=185000 gt /75 = 2467 s = 41.1 min. Lantern 15000, 500 ft: 15000x49=735000/75=9800 s = 163 min. Ration, 500 ft: 250x490=122500/75=1633 s = 27.2 min.

## Contradictions and surprises
- Brief's "Three torches burned out while idle in town" is consistent with burn in town (41 min per full torch at normal time) and store-bought torches (2500, 20.6 min in town): three stock torches is roughly 1 hour. Food does not digest in town, so a food-vs-idle worry does not apply there.
- Surprise: resting in town accelerates torch burn 10x in real time (finding 5) because of the base_time_factor/time_factor mismatch. Unverified live.
- Surprise: store torches are only half full (2500), not 5000; drops are random 1..4000.
- The code comment dungeon.c:2006 says FPS "about 50" but the cfg says 75 and the default is 12. The 75 figure is for the shipped mangband.cfg files; I did not check what the running server used.
- `time` truncation: 37.5 vs 37 means light burns 1.3% faster than one unit per player turn in town; negligible.
