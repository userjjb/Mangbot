# Dispatch: S2 shop prices, discounts, sell values
- Brief: buy/sell formulas, discounts, object costs, owners, and check against mission 8 prices (CHR 4 Half-Orc).
- Clerk run: 2026-09-29; sources: src/server/store.c, object2.c, tables.c, init1.c, init2.c, xtra1.c, dungeon.c, spells2.c, lib/edit/{cost_adj,shop_own,object,p_race}.txt, HANDBOOK.md:265-270 (for the mission 8 numbers). Values CSV: scratch/S2_values.csv. Helper scripts: scratch/s2_calc.py.

## Answer
Buy price per item = (value * adjust + 50) / 100 (integer), where value = object cost (after any discount), adjust = greed + racefactor + CHR_adj - 200, floored at 100. There is no haggling: the final price is always the owner's `greed` (min) figure. CHR 4 gives adj_chr_gold = 125 (the worst allowed, apart from CHR 3 = 130). For a Half-Orc warrior at CHR 4 the buy multiplier is about 1.35 to 1.62 depending on shop and owner. Selling to a store pays value * (400 - greed - racefactor - CHR)/100, capped at 100%: about 38-65% at the Armoury, 38-55% at the Weaponsmith, 39-45% at the Alchemist. The formula reproduces nearly all mission 8 prices exactly. The one exception is CCW 152 (Temple), which no Temple owner produces at CHR 4.

9. **Stock discounts** [SEEN] `mass_produce` store.c:268-370. ## Contradictions and surprises
- HANDBOOK's CCW 152 cannot be reproduced at CHR 4 from any Temple owner (see 15).
- The brief expected discounts of 25/50/75/90 from `mass_produce`: confirmed, but with a single 2% chain, so 75%/90% are very rare (0.16% / 0.10% per new item). Yet the HANDBOOK reports 75% off WoR/Identify and Enchant To-Hit at 75% off in mission 8. That is unusual luck or a repeated stack. Not a contradiction of the code, just a surprise. Also the `store_shuffle` discount is always exactly 50%.
- `max_greed` in shop_own.txt is not used in any price; only the first greed number matters (store.c:1345 computes it only for an unused variable).
- The BM will buy any item type (no switch case).
- Stores price the sale of *unknown* ring/potion/etc. at a flat guess, so selling unidentified items is a small loss/gain vs the identified value; not relevant for a warrior.
