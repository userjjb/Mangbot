## Answer
A shop pays `V_seen x S`, where `V_seen` is the item's **true** value only if the item is "known" to the server (ID_KNOWN, or EASY_KNOW and aware). Otherwise it is the kind's base cost, or a flat 5/20/20/70/50/90/45/45 for unaware food/potion/scroll/staff/wand/rod/ring/amulet. Wands and staffs are not EASY_KNOW, so an unidentified-charges wand or staff sells at bare kind cost. Armour or weapon with unknown plusses or ego sells at bare kind cost too. That is the whole mechanism behind every large sale loss: **12 sales fell short by at least 25 gold, and 2 more were irreplaceable items.** In m17-23 the Dive04 logs show 47 sales that paid 5,339 gold where identified sales would have paid about 6,882. That is a shortfall of 1,634, of which 1,287 came from 9 `!`-forced sales. Gold stolen was 302. Destroys came to about 520 at shop value. The biggest single leaks are the Free Action boots (546), the Staff of Sleep Monsters (337), and the m22 loot left behind (about 1,000).

## Proposed "value lost" definition (Q3)
`value_lost = sale_shortfall + abandoned + overridden/mistake + theft`, in gold, each with a confidence tag; plus a separate irreplaceable-gear list (not summed).
1. **Sale shortfall** = `fair_pay - received`, where `fair_pay = buy-back listing price x S/A` (the shop re-lists the sold item identified). Count only shortfall >= 25 (small unknown-junk gains net out). Tag `forced` when the sale used `!`.
2. **Abandoned** = true value x S if the item was seen on the floor and never picked up in a mission where the pack filled. Count only jewellery, devices, `{good/excellent}` gear, unique drops. Ignore plain armour and weapons without a quality feeling. Unknown flavour counts at the 70/50/90/45 floor, tagged "minimum".
3. **Destroy** = true value x S, only for non-cursed items that are not plainly junk (skip `{cursed}`, `{worthless}`, skeletons, water, mushrooms).
4. **Theft/destroyed by monster** = gold face value, or what an item cost; none if recovered.
5. **Irreplaceable flag** = ego/artifact items whose store re-listing is more than 1000, or Free Action, See Invisible, speed, resist items, Teleportation. Free Action boots would be one flag; ceiling 549 as sale shortfall, but replacement 2265.
6. **Mistakes with a cost** (Remove Curse after wearing unidentified jewellery) counted as the gold actually spent.
