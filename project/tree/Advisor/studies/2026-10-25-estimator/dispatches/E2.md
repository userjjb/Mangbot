# Dispatch: E2 mechanics for an emergency option simulator
- Brief: exact rules (file:line) for potions, scrolls/staff, post-teleport monster behaviour, spells/breaths, fear/groups, status effects, stairs.
- Clerk run: 2026-10-25; sources: github/src/server/{cmd6,use-obj,melee1,melee2,xtra1,xtra2,cmd1,cmd2,dungeon,spells1,spells2,monster2,tables}.c, src/options.h, lib/edit/{object,p_race,p_class}.txt, rulebook constants.csv + fight_sim.py docstring. Full table: scratch/E2/mechanics.csv (46 rows). Paths below are under github/src/server/ unless noted.

## Answer
Quaffing and staff use have no status gates (blind, confused, afraid and dark are all fine). Scrolls are blocked by blind, confusion and an unlit own grid, but not by fear. Monsters have no flow or pathing in this build: they walk straight at our exact grid whenever they are within their `aaf` or in LOS. A Phase Door (5-10 grids) therefore changes little, while a Teleport (50-100) drops almost every monster out of activation range. Monsters cast or breathe only with a projectable path and cdis<=18. Spell choice is uniform over the monster's list. Stairs remove us from the grid instantly, so no monster acts after the command.

## Findings
1. Potions [SEEN] (use-obj.c:467-510, cmd6.c:155-216): CLW heals 15 and cures blind, cut-20, conf-20 (only partial conf). CSW heals 20-24 and cures blind, conf, cut. CCW heals 25-29 and cures blind, conf, poison, stun, cut. None of them cure fear. Healing is +300. Heal amounts are trivial next to 310 HP; their value is the status cures. No failure chance, no restrictions, one `level_speed` of energy (cmd6.c:205).
2. Heroism/Berserk [SEEN] (use-obj.c:467-481; xtra1.c:2016-2017, 2709-2722, 2756): heal 10/30 first, then fear cleared, then the timer is set. Hero: to_h +12, mhp +10. Berserk: to_h +24, **AC -10**, mhp +30. Both give resist_fear (fear blows and spells fail while active). The +mhp is not a flat heal: chp is rescaled by new_mhp/old_mhp (xtra1.c:2019-2040), so Berserk adds about 30*chp/mhp (about 12 HP at 40% HP) and reverses when it expires. constants.csv says "heal 10/30" and omits the to-hit, AC and mhp parts (an omission, not an error).
3. To-hit enters melee as `chance = skill_thn + (p->to_h + weapon to_h)*3` (cmd1.c:1426-1427, BTH_PLUS_ADJ=3 mdefines.h:157). So Hero is +36 on chance and Berserk +72. At clvl 25 skill_thn is 194 before stats (char_skills_by_clvl.csv row 25). Hit test: 5% auto-hit, 5% auto-miss, else randint0(chance) >= ac*3/4 (cmd1.c:51-71). **An unseen monster halves chance** (cmd1.c:1441, 57). Blind therefore means about half the hits (inferred: mon_vis false when blind; not traced).
4. Speed [SEEN] tables.c:2171-2192, char_extract_energy.csv: +10 gives 2000 against 1000, x2 exactly (+20 is x3 at 3000). Slow (-10) gives 500. The potion is `randint1(25)+15` blocks, +5 if already fast (use-obj.c:436-445). Timers count blocks, one block = one normal-speed turn (dungeon.c:1332-1380), so hasted we act twice per block [INFERRED from constants.csv game_tick_block_interval] and a Speed potion gives about 32-80 hasted actions.
5. Scrolls [SEEN] (cmd6.c:261-322): reading is refused if blind ("can't see anything"), if the own grid is not seeable (`no_lite`, cave.c:379), or if confused. The refusal happens before the energy is spent, so a refused read costs no turn. Fear does not block it. No fail chance. Phase = teleport_player(10), Teleport = (100) (use-obj.c:795-807). `teleport_player` picks d in [dis/2,dis], an empty floor grid, not a vault (ICKY), and widens after 500 failed tries (spells1.c:167-215).
6. Word of Recall: a second read **cancels** a pending recall (spells2.c:1191-1203 sets word_recall=0), so the Pilot must not read it twice. Magic Mapping maps x +-52 and y +-23 (spells2.c:1212-1213, use-obj.c:902-907), with no monster detection. Deep Descent: absent (no match in object.txt or src). Teleport Level scroll exists (use-obj.c:809).
7. Staff of Teleportation [SEEN]: object.txt:2616-2622 (tval 55 sval 4, level 20), teleport_player(100) (use-obj.c:1124-1130). `do_cmd_use_staff` has no blind, confused, dark or fear gate (cmd6.c:409-519). Energy is spent before the roll (cmd6.c:468), so a failed use or an empty staff costs a full turn. Skill: skill_dev = r_dev -3 + c_dev 18 + 7*25/10 (=17) + adj_int_dev[INT] = 32+adj (p_race.txt:117, p_class.txt:85-86, xtra1.c:2276, 2829; matches char_skills_by_clvl.csv "32 before INT"). adj_int_dev is 0 for INT<=7, 1 for 8-14, 2 for 15-17, 3 for 18 (tables.c:1274-1314). With c = 32+adj-20 = 12+adj, P(use) = 1-2/c = **0.83 (adj 0) to 0.87 (adj 3)**, i.e. a 13-17% fail. If confused, skill is halved to 16, c=-4, and P(use) is about 4% (1/8 x 1/3). I did not read our character's INT.
8. Monster movement [SEEN]. `MONSTER_FLOW` is commented out ("broken in mangband", options.h:399-403), so get_moves_aux is dead code. get_moves (melee2.c:2187-2420) aims at the player's exact current grid by a direction table, with no pathfinding and no LOS needed, then tries 5 alternates. A monster acts in process_monsters (melee2.c:3374-3560) only if cdis <= aaf (walls and LOS ignored), or cdis <= MAX_SIGHT=20 with LOS or aggravate. Monsters with cdis>=100 are skipped unless WANDERER. aaf in monster.txt: 20 for 256 of 616 races, 30 for 77, 40 for 25, 100 for 16, under 20 for roughly 200 (I counted `I:` field 3). There is no noise/scent tracking after a teleport. Noise only affects waking sleepers (melee2.c:2490-2500).
9. Consequences [INFERRED from 8]. After a Phase (5-10 grids) nearly all awake monsters still know where we are and close in d-1 actions at equal speed, or d/2 for +10 monsters against our normal speed. A wall between us and them stalls them (no flow), so phasing into another room or behind a wall buys more than phasing in the open. After a Teleport (50-100 grids) only the aaf 100 races (16) and WANDERERs keep coming; the aaf 40 races (25) lose us if the distance is over 40. This is also our extra time before a Recall (15-34 blocks) fires.
10. Monster spells need cdis<=MAX_RANGE (18) AND `projectable_wall` from monster to player (melee2.c:459-486). Out of LOS or beyond 18, a monster never casts, breathes or summons, though it may still walk toward us. The cast chance is (freq_innate+freq_spell)/2, and init1.c:3184 sets both to 100/x, so it is 100/x percent per action. A confused monster never casts. The spell is picked uniformly: `spell[randint0(num)]` over all RF4/5/6 bits (melee2.c:571). The smart-spell filter is compiled out (DRS_SMART_OPTIONS commented out, options.h:437), so no monster avoids spells we resist. The only smart behaviour: an RF2_SMART monster under 10% HP, 50% of the time, restricts itself to INT_MASK spells (melee2.c:488-511). This matches fight_sim.py ("one spell, uniform").
11. Breaths [SEEN] (melee2.c:642-880; spells1.c:3603-3608): damage uses CURRENT hp. /3 with caps acid/elec/fire/cold 1600, poison 800, time 150, gravity 200. /6 with caps nether 550, light/dark/confusion/nexus 400, sound/chaos/disenchant/shards 500, inertia 200, plasma 150, force 200. Water and mana were not read (see build_monsters.py spell_dmg). Player damage is capped at 1600. A breath is a **ball of radius 2 (3 if POWERFUL) centred on the player** (flags GRID|ITEM|KILL, no STOP; melee2.c:347-361), so we take the full hit (r=0, no division) and it passes over monsters in between. Blast grids are chosen by `los` from the centre (spells1.c:4612-4635). Other monsters inside the blast are hurt too (only the caster is excluded, spells1.c:2370), so friendly fire is possible. Bolts use PROJECT_STOP|KILL (melee2.c:333-338, spells1.c:4508) and stop at the first monster in the path, so a bolt is absorbed by a monster in front of us. There are no arcs or cones in 1.5, and no splash onto us from hits on others, apart from the ball radius.
12. Summons [SEEN] (melee2.c:1704-1975, monster2.c:2552-2592): monster level (dun_depth + rlev)/2 + 5, awake, groups allowed. Placement is a random empty floor grid within radius d = i/15+1 of us (20 tries, fails silently). Counts: S_KIN 6, S_MONSTER 1, S_MONSTERS 8, ANIMAL/SPIDER/HOUND/HYDRA 6, ANGEL/DEMON/UNDEAD/DRAGON 1, HI_DEMON/HI_UNDEAD/HI_DRAGON 8, WRAITH 8+8, UNIQUE 8+8. All need the LOS-path gate from 10. A hound summon with `grp` TRUE can bring a FRIENDS pack per spawn [INFERRED, place_monster_aux monster2.c:2154].
13. Status spells and blows [SEEN]. Spells (melee2.c:1347-1460): SCARE is nullified by resist_fear (hero/berserk) or by randint0(100)<skill_sav, else afraid += 4+randint0(4). BLIND uses 12+randint0(4), CONF 4+randint0(4), with resist or save. SLOW and HOLD use 4+randint0(4); free_act is immune before the save, and a save also works. Our saving throw at clvl 25 is 40 + adj_wis_sav[WIS]. Blows (melee1.c:892-990): blind 10+randint1(rlev) unless resist_blind; confuse 3+randint1(rlev) with **no save**; terrify 3+randint1(rlev) unless resist_fear or save; paralyze 3+randint1(rlev) unless free_act or save. Cut and stun come only from `monster_critical` (needs damage >=95% of max; melee1.c:68-100, 1225-1295). Stun above 50 gives -20 to-hit and -20 to-dam, 1..50 gives -5/-5, and >=100 means KO (energy 0; xtra1.c:2668-2682, dungeon.c:987). Stun recovers by adj_con_fix[CON]+1 per block.
14. Player-side status effects [SEEN]. Afraid: melee refused (cmd1.c:1399) and auto-retaliate off (dungeon.c:1038); quaff, read and staff still work. Confused: walking direction is fully random (cmd2.c:2987-2995), no reading, no running, auto-retaliate off, staff skill halved. Paralysed: energy held at 0 (dungeon.c:987), -1 per block. Slowed: -10 speed. Free Action stops paralysis and slow from monsters. At a constant 1 per block, a 10-block paralysis equals 10 normal turns, but our 310 HP and 4-8 action spell gaps make the Free Action case the one that matters.
15. Monster fear [SEEN] (xtra2.c:3081-3127): a monster that is not NO_FEAR flees after a hit if (hp%<=10 and randint0(10)<hp%) or (dam>=hp after the hit and 80%). Duration is randint1(10) + (20 if dam>=hp and pct>7, else (11-pct)*5). Pain cancels fear: monfear -= randint1(dam). It decays per monster action by randint1(level/10+1) (melee2.c:2645-2670). Fleeing monsters **still cast** (the spell check comes before movement), and a cornered one turns bold at once (melee2.c:3319-3330). The ALLOW_TERROR run test (options.h:293, melee2.c:2025-2090) can only fire at clvl 25 for races of level<=4 at cdis>5.
16. Groups [SEEN, by absence]: no pack AI in melee2.c (grep for pack/FRIENDS finds only generation code, monster2.c:2154-2165). FRIENDS groups are placed at generation: size randint1(13) +/- the level-vs-depth gap, capped at GROUP_MAX 32 (monster2.c:1997-2060), puddled adjacent. After that each monster independently walks at us. A surround happens only because blocked monsters try alternate directions (mm[1..4]). Hounds do not flank deliberately.
17. Stairs [SEEN] (cmd2.c:20-205): the command costs `level_speed` energy, removes us from the grid and sets new_level_flag at once. process_monsters skips players with new_level_flag (melee2.c:3396-3406), and the new level is built at the start of the next dungeon() pass before monsters move (dungeon.c:2058-2275). So no adjacent monster acts after the command. Monsters that already acted before our energy filled still get their turns first, as for any command. Going down is blocked on quest levels and at depth 127 (cmd2.c:150-170).

## Contradictions and surprises
- **No contradiction of constants.csv** in anything I checked: devices (cmd6.c:477-495), recall, teleport ranges, Deep Descent absent, and energy cost lines are all right. Gaps only: Hero/Berserk to-hit, AC, mhp and fear immunity are not recorded.
- Berserk **lowers AC by 10**, which fight_sim.py ignores (docstring covers monster side only). Both potions also give fear immunity.
- Smart-spell avoidance (a monster skipping spells we resist) is **compiled out**; spell choice is truly uniform. fight_sim's model is right on this point.
- The monster `aaf` is read with no LOS test, so monsters behind walls within aaf (20 for most) keep acting and walking straight, which is not what the "monsters lose us" folklore of vanilla flow says.
- Phase gives little against breath monsters in the open, but monsters with RF4/5/6 spells in LOS keep casting; ranges 5-10 are inside MAX_RANGE 18.

## What a simulator cannot model well, and why
- Monster positions after Phase/Teleport: no flow means walls decide arrival; a sim needs the actual map and the walk-straight rule, which our sight data does not give.
- Which monsters are awake and within aaf but unseen: they act and cast without being in the "in view" list.
- Door opening, bashing and rubble delays for monsters; mm[1..4] alternates in corridors.
- Summons: random race, random count, placed near us; only the distribution is known, not the specific monsters.
- Breath damage from monsters we have not yet identified; HP of unseen monsters (breath is current HP/3 or /6).
- Timer interaction with speed: effects count blocks, not player actions, so Speed shortens the status a given number of our actions covers.
- Critical-hit/stun/cut outcomes and our own criticals (not modelled by fight_sim.py).
- Multi-monster time ordering (energy accumulates per monster with recoil), so the order of events inside a round is approximate.
